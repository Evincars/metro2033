# Belorusskaya Trade Outpost

The **Belorusskaya Trade Outpost** is a minor location in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel))*, led by figures known as "the Managers."

## Overview

The Belorusskaya Trade Outpost sits between the [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza)-controlled Ring Line and the **Farms and Factories** — a collection of stations (Sokol, Aeroport, and Dinamo) that is largely responsible for livestock production, containing many pig pens and chicken farms. Its neutrality and self-reliance are the product of a strategic location between Hanza and the plantations. With the support of the latter, it could not be fully integrated into Hanza; it appears to be under Hanza's protection but not its jurisdiction.

The traders from Belorusskaya (on the Zamoskvoretskaya Line) call themselves "Managers." The station sees itself as a wealthy neutral spot, welcoming to all major powers — akin to Switzerland. In the novel it is also mentioned that drunk people at the station often disappear, rumored to be the work of the [Satanists](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Satanists). The Belorusskaya Trade Outpost also deals with the [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich), possibly buying slaves for the plantations.

## Diplomatic relations

- **Disposition toward player:** Neutral
- **Allies:** [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza), [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich)
- **Enemies:** [Satanists](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Satanists)
