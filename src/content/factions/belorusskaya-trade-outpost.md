# Belorusskaya Trade Outpost

A collection of trading stations including Sokol, Aeroport, Dinamo, and Belorusskaya. Belorusskaya is a leading producer of food in the Metro, particularly livestock. They maintain near-total neutrality, even going so far as to buy slaves from the [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich). In the present time, Belorusskaya has suffered from a spate of unexplained kidnappings (rumoured to be related to [Satanists](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Satanists)).
