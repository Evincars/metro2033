# Night Hunters

The **Night Hunters** were a bandit group that operated in the ruins of [Vladivostok](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vladivostok) in the early 2030s. They appear in the [Sam's Story](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam%27s_Story) DLC for *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*.

## Overview

The Night Hunters were a group of bandits who came to Vladivostok in the early 2030s, intent on claiming control of the ruins and the trade that flowed from the nearby port. Though small in numbers, they made up for this with specialized training and equipment, including their signature [Night Vision Goggles](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Vision_Goggles). This allowed them to demand tribute from the other local gangs, including ones much larger than themselves, and to eliminate anyone who tried to stand up to them. They eventually managed to control large swaths of the ruins and established multiple caches and safehouses for their members.

However, despite having claimed the region, their control did not last. At some point before 2036, the other bandit groups allied against the Night Hunters, and either killed or drove off the remaining members of the gang. Though some managed to survive, they would ultimately die within their secure safehouses, unable to launch an effective counterattack.

## Equipment

Though small in numbers, the Night Hunters utilized advanced equipment to combat larger forces. Their uniform was military-grade, consisting of a grey bulletproof vest, dark pants, and a green combat helmet equipped with night vision goggles, which each member used to access the various safehouses and caches set up throughout the ruins. They utilized [Sammy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sammy) rifles and [Stallion](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stallion) pistols, presumably bought from [Tom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tom) and his [army](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tom%27s_Army), as well as [Ashot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashot) shotguns.

## Role in Metro Exodus

[Sam](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam) encounters multiple Night Hunter safehouses and caches while exploring the ruins of Vladivostok in the *Sam's Story* DLC. By this time, the Night Hunters have already been wiped out, and Sam finds only their remains alongside useful supplies and equipment they left behind. Their night vision goggles and weapon stashes serve as valuable loot for the player throughout the DLC.

## Trivia

- The Night Hunters are already extinct by the time Sam arrives in Vladivostok, making them one of the few factions encountered only through environmental storytelling.
- Their reliance on night vision technology gave them their name and their tactical advantage over larger rival gangs.
- Each member appears to have utilized slight variations to the standard equipment loadout.
