# Children of the Forest

**Children of the Forest** (Russian: Dети Леса) are a [faction](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Factions) in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*. Consisting of two separate divisions, the **Pioneers** and the **Pirates**, most of them are hostile towards outsiders like [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom), especially the Pirates.

## Overview

The Children of the Forest are a group of children who were stranded at a scout camp when [World War III](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_III) occurred. Raised by their camp master, whom they came to call "[Teacher](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Teacher_(Metro_Exodus))", they developed the skills necessary to survive any threat the wilderness may present -- whether the changing weather, [mutant](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants) creatures, [bandit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits_(Faction)) attacks, or unwelcome guests.

They formed a tribe in post-apocalyptic [Kazakhstan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kazakhstan), created sometime after 2013. The Children of the Forest wear primitive clothes made from items found in nature, similar to the nomadic tribes of the past. Their faces are hidden by masks made from the skulls of [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants) in order to instill fear in the hearts of their enemies. As hinted at by their name, they tend to live in woodlands and other areas that remained clear or far from the urbanized centers of humanity's former glory now poisoned by radiation.

The Children of the Forest developed a justice system called the Forest Court, used mostly to judge outsiders and trespassers to determine whether they are hostile or not. If deemed non-hostile, they are allowed to pass through the territory peacefully, while hostile ones are dealt with swiftly.

## Role in Metro Exodus

The Children of the Forest appear in the [Taiga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Taiga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level of *Metro Exodus*. [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) encounters them while traveling through the forested valley. The faction is divided between the more peaceful Pioneers, who follow the Teacher's original ideals, and the aggressive Pirates, who have turned to violence and banditry.

The player's actions during the level determine whether the Children of the Forest remain allies or become hostile. If Artyom avoids killing the Pioneers and acts peacefully, [Alyosha](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alyosha) can form a bond with the faction, particularly with [Olga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Olga), one of the Pioneers. The moral choices in this level affect the game's ending.

## Trivia

- The Children of the Forest largely wiped out the [bandits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits_(Faction)) in their region.
- Their name and nature are reminiscent of the "Lord of the Flies" concept -- children forming their own society in the absence of adults.
- The faction's leader, the Teacher, is one of the few adults among them, having raised them since childhood.
