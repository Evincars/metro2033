# Molodezhnaya

The **Human-Mutant Society** of Molodezhnaya is a [faction](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Factions) of mutated people inhabiting Molodezhnaya station on the Filyovskaya Line of the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro). The station and its population are led by a mysterious mutant with hypnotic and telepathic abilities named [Zhabdar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Zhabdar). The station and its society appear in the *[Dark Tunnels](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Tunnels_(Novel))* novel and on the map of *[Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035)*.

## History

During the [nuclear war](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_III), a group of people took refuge at Molodezhnaya on the [Arbatsko-Pokrovskaya Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arbatsko-Pokrovskaya_Line). Because of the station's shallow depth and the extreme surface radiation seeping in, much of the population died off from disease, deformity, and radiation sickness. The survivors, now mutated, tried to migrate deeper into the radiation-free Metro.

The **"clean"** residents of the Metro did not welcome them. Mutants were often imprisoned or killed, hunted by [stalkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers) on the surface for sport with their heads hung as trophies, or enslaved and forced into a "freak circus." As a result of this persecution, the mutants returned to Molodezhnaya and formed a secret, isolated society.

Over the following two decades many continued to die from sickness and hunger, but a portion — mostly those born after the war — adapted to the harsh environment and even developed unique psychic or telepathic abilities. The station is accessible only by two secret passages known to the mutants and a few outside traders:

- A tunnel secretly dug over fifteen years, running to the area near [Timiryazevskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Timiryazevskaya) on the Serpukhovsko-Timiryazevskaya Line.
- An old highway running parallel to the Filyovskaya Line, eventually connecting to [Kievskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kievskaya) in [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) territory. Though irradiated and prone to causing hallucinations, it is safer than the abandoned Filyovskaya tunnels.

## Society

The station is led by [Zhabdar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Zhabdar), a mutant with hypnotic and telepathic powers. The platform is patrolled by sentries in khaki coats armed with [Kalashnikov assault rifles](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M)), self-made [double-barrel shotguns](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duplet), and [Ashot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashot)-style carbines. Bearing arms at the station is otherwise forbidden. There are no major tunnel defences, since the surrounding abandoned stations are too irradiated to pass through. The population survives by growing mushrooms and conducting limited, secret trade with outsiders for other food such as pork.

## Dark Tunnels

In the novel *Dark Tunnels*, protagonist **Anatoliy Tomski** and his companions reach Molodezhnaya after escaping the [Satanists](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Satanists) occupying [Timiryazevskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Timiryazevskaya). A hypnotic human-mutant named **Mobat**, native to the station, helps them escape and leads them in through the secret passage. Anatoliy speaks alone with Zhabdar and the group spends the night, fed and resupplied with weapons and ammunition. Tomski and his companion Krab note how clean, organized, hospitable, and peaceful the station is — observing that the mutants had retained a humanity and compassion often missing from the "clean" societies of the Metro. The next morning they depart via the highway passage toward Kievskaya.
