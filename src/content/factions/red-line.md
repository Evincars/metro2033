# Red Line

A Marxist-Leninist and neo-Stalinist faction, the Red Line is the haven of Soviet nostalgists and ageing former members of the CPSU and Komsomol. Led by General Secretary [Maxim Moskvin](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Maxim_Moskvin), the Communist Party maintains control much as in Stalin's time - through a program of censorship, political and social repression, and webs of spies and informants.

The Reds occupy most of the Sokolnicheskaya Line (literally, "Red Line"; the oldest in the Metro and a constant reminder of the glories of Russia's pseudo-socialist past) minus the two stations of the Ring, and possess the largest population and greatest territory in the Metro. Much like the Soviet Army of the Second World War, the Red Line relies on a massive, but poorly trained and equipped, conscript army. Of the Line's 15,000 citizens, 6000-7000 serve in the Armed Forces, engaged in a constant struggle against the Neo-Nazis of the [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich).
