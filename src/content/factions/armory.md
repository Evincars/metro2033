# Armory Station

> "Armory Station was where most of the weapons in the Metro were made. There had been an arms factory located above and after the nukes fell, many of the workers settled here."
> — Artyom

**Armory Station** (Russian: Кузнецкий Мост, *Kuznetsky Most*) is a major settlement under [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) control. Armory mass-produces most of the weapons seen in the post-apocalyptic Metro. Its real-world counterpart is the Kuznetsky Most station on the Tagansko-Krasnopresnenskaya Line, meaning "Blacksmith Bridge."

## Overview

The Armory claims to be a free station, but it operates under the "watchful eye of the Reds," enforcing a curfew and conducting frequent unscheduled searches. In practice it acts as a Red Line stronghold where most of the Metro's weaponry is manufactured. The station is very large compared with many others [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) encounters on his travels.

At one of the gates the local militia is nominally in control, but deeper inside the communist presence becomes obvious. Red Army iconography covers the walls, while loudspeakers and guards can be seen and heard throughout. The first part of the station holds several cages where people manufacture new weapons, alongside a cramped section of worker housing packed into a small space. The main passage houses workers at one end, [weapon vendors](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_Vendors) in the middle, and manufacturing cages at the other.

## History

Above Armory Station there was originally a weapons factory where arms and ammunition were manufactured, hence the name. After the nuclear disaster, the factory workers sought shelter in the Metro below, bringing their knowledge of engineering and machinery with them. The former weapon machinists established a large and wealthy settlement that would go on to produce almost all of the Metro's weapons. Years later, as threats from the [Nazis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nazis) increased, it came under the control of the communist faction. It is now responsible for the vast majority of the ammunition and weaponry created in the Metro, since it houses much of its original factory equipment.

## Trivia

- The Armory is the first place Artyom can purchase one of the two suits of armor available in the game (the heavy armor), and it is the only station where the stealth suit can be bought. However, these armor sets cannot be purchased in the Redux version of *Metro 2033*.
- Although the settlement is a Red Line stronghold, its entrances are guarded by militia rather than communist soldiers — likely because many loyal guards were sent to fight the Fourth Reich, leaving mercenaries to handle ordinary tasks.
- Upon leaving the Armory, a few *Piter* novels can be spotted under a rail cart.
- According to Andrew in *Metro: Last Light*, the Armory was later subverted by the Reds, who searched for traitors and spies while sending people to training camps or executing them ahead of the coming war.
- Armory Station is one of the few locations visited in both games, though never the same area — as with the Library, Polis, and D6.
