# Munai-bailer

**Munai-bailer** (Kazakh: Мұнай-байлер, roughly translated to "Oil-drillers"), also referred to as the **Fire Worshipers**, are a [faction](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Factions) in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)* who are hostile to [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and his companions. They are led by a man known as "[The Baron](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Baron)".

## Overview

The Munai-bailer are a group of around 700 raiders mostly made up of ex-soldiers, mobsters, and Svarog Oil workers who have managed to secure vast oil reserves in the [Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Caspian_Sea) region. They are one of the few post-apocalyptic factions to not only have access to working [vehicles](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicles), but maintain a large number of them. They have used these resources to enslave the local population and fend off any other raider groups making incursions into their region.

The Munai-bailer are noticeably better armed than most bandit groups, sporting advanced weapons like the [Bulldog](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bulldog) and the [Shambler](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Uboinik_(Shambler)), while even employing armored soldiers with [Gatling](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gatling) guns. Recently, they have started styling themselves as "fire gods", presumably to keep their slave population in line.

## Role in Metro Exodus

The Munai-bailer are the primary antagonists of [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level in *Metro Exodus*. The [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) crew encounters them while searching for water and fuel in the dried-up Caspian region.

[The Baron](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Baron) rules the region with an iron fist, using slave labor to extract oil and maintain his power. His lieutenant, [Saul](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Saul), serves as a key figure in the conflict. The local resistance, led by [Giul](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Giul), fights against the Munai-bailer's oppression.

Artyom's actions in this level, particularly regarding the enslaved locals and whether he spares or kills [Saul](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Saul), affect the moral outcome and ending of the game. The fate of the Munai-bailer remains uncertain after the Baron's demise and the destruction of their main base by Giul.

## Trivia

- In the Russian version of the game, they are also known as "neftyaniki" (oil workers) and "ognepoklonniki" (fire-worshippers).
- They are one of the few factions with access to working motor vehicles in the post-apocalyptic world.
- The name "Munai-bailer" comes from the Kazakh words for oil and wealth/power.
