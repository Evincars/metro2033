# Polar Dawns

**Polar Dawns** (Russian: Полярные Зори, Polyarnye Zori) is a town mentioned in *[Metro 2034](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2034)* and spoken about again in *[Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035)*. It is located in Murmansk Oblast, [Russia](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Russia), on the Niva River, 224 kilometres south of Murmansk and 1500 kilometers from [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow).

## Overview

The location is first heard of through [Homer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Homer), who describes it as untouched and fortified. The town and area around it contained no targets of significance, allowing them to be spared from nuclear annihilation. The Kola Nuclear Power Station, which was one of the most powerful in the country, has its own farm, agricultural land, and army.

The location contains enough fuel to power itself for centuries, maintaining luxuries of the pre-war era, such as computers, electronic libraries, and heating. This makes Polar Dawns one of the most advanced and self-sufficient survivor communities in the post-apocalyptic world -- a stark contrast to the struggling populations of the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro).

## Connection to Metro Exodus

While Polar Dawns does not appear directly as a playable location in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*, it represents one of the many survivor settlements beyond Moscow that [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) crew learn about during their journey across Russia. In *Metro 2035*, rumors circulate that survivors from Polar Dawns have arrived at the Moscow Metro, which ties into the broader theme of Metro Exodus -- that life persists beyond the metro tunnels.

## Trivia

- Polar Dawns is based on the real-world town of [Polyarnye Zori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyarnye_Zori), which indeed houses the Kola Nuclear Power Plant.
- It is one of the many survivor camps that radio operators have come across.
- The settlement's access to nuclear power gives it a significant advantage over other survivor communities that rely on scavenged resources.
