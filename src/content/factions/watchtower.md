# The Watchtower

**The Watchtower** is a religious faction appearing in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel))*, led by Elder John and based in a train-turned-monastery in the tunnel between Serpukhovskaya and [Tulskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tulskaya) stations.

## Overview

The Watchtower comprises many Jehovah's Witnesses, who operate and preach out of a subway train converted into a monastery. They believe the end-times are already upon them, and that the survivors of the [great war](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Great_War_of_2013) are those selected by God to ascend to heaven. [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) meets a member of this group, **Brother Timothy**, after escaping Serpukhovskaya.

Out of kindness, Timothy takes Artyom to the Watchtower and provides him with food and new clothes after a shower of running water. While Artyom stays and listens to some of the sermons of the Watchtower priests — Elder John in particular — he eventually leaves, grown tired of the religious preaching, which contradicts his own perceptions of the state of the Metro.

The whereabouts of the Watchtower are not directly specified, but it is probably located in a tunnel between Serpukhovskaya and Tulskaya stations.

## Diplomatic relations
