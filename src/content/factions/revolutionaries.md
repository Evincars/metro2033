# Revolutionaries

A military branch of the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line), though not directly affiliated anymore, the Revolutionaries travel the Metro attacking [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) territory and patrols, supplied by the Red Line. Following the ideas of Leon Trotsky and Che Guevara, they save [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) from certain death by a Reich noose.
