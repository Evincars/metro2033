# Revolutionaries

> "No, just simple communists."
> — Comrade Bonsai

The **Revolutionaries**, or **Trotskyists**, are a group of communists based at Avtozavodskaya who wage guerrilla warfare against the [fascists](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich), constantly harassing them.

## Overview

Allied with the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) but no longer directly affiliated with it, the Revolutionaries roam the Metro attacking [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) territory and patrols, supplied by the Red Line. Following the ideas of Leon Trotsky and Che Guevara, they save [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) from certain death by a Reich noose during his travels.

## Diplomatic relations

- **Base location:** Avtozavodskaya
- **Disposition toward player:** Neutral
- **Allies:** [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line)
