# Arbat Confederation

Occupying the three-pronged [Kievskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kievskaya) radial, the Confederation is distinguished by its ethnic diversity and hyper-capitalist economic system, dominated by an unusually high number of stalkers. Formerly a powerful faction, Arbat was an ally of Hanza in the [Hansa - Red Line War](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hansa_-_Red_Line_War), but was severely weakened by the war and forced permanently into Hanza's sphere of influence.
