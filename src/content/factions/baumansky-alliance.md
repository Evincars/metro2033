# Baumansky Alliance

The **Baumansky Alliance** (Russian: Бауманский Альянс) is an independent [faction](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Factions) of the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Metro_System). It is an alliance between three stations on the Arbatsko-Pokrovskaya Line — **Baumanskaya** (from which the Alliance takes its name), **Elektrozavodskaya**, and **Semyonovskaya**.

## Overview

Very little is known about the Baumansky Alliance, its origins, or its exact relations toward most other factions in the Metro. Its main trade is recreating and refurbishing pre-war technology recovered from the surface, before selling it to the other factions and stations of the Metro.

It is likely that the Alliance is under a protection pact with [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) in return for technology, but it appears to be powerful enough not to be fully dependent on them. The station of Elektrozavodskaya — named for the electric light-bulb factory that once stood on the surface nearby — runs a power plant, generating and supplying electricity to the Metro. Given Hanza's advanced technology and relatively wealthy living conditions, it is likely they purchase a considerable amount of the Alliance's products.

This important trade relationship ensures that the Alliance is not fully absorbed by Hanza, unlike other smaller factions such as the [Arbat Confederation](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arbat_Confederation), and it remains independent. Because the Alliance's goods are in high demand across the whole Metro, it can be assumed that it has few enemies.

Much of what is known about the Baumansky Alliance is speculative, however, as neither [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) nor [Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Ranger)) visit the Alliance in the novels or the games. Some additional information on the faction appears in the wider Metro Universe book series.
