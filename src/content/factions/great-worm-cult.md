# Savage Cannibals of the Great Worm Cult

A technophobic and xenophobic primitive faction, residing in [Park Pobedy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Park_Pobedy), the Great Worm Cult have descended into a state of primeval living. They worship an entity known as the "Great Worm", the being that created the entire world (i.e. The Metro), and frequently cannibalize those who use technology.

Their leader is actually an old man who hated how technology destroyed the world. The Great Worm Cult's primary enemies are the [Arbat Confederation](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arbat_Confederation), whose children they frequently steal.
