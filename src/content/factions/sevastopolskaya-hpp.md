# Sevastopolskaya HPP

The **Sevastopolskaya Hydroelectric Power Plant** (sometimes abbreviated **Sevastopolskaya HPP**, Russian: Севастопольская ГЭС) is a minor [faction](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Factions) that appears in the novel *[Metro 2034](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2034)*, led by Vladimir Istomin.

## Overview

Sevastopolskaya HPP spans three stations: **Sevastopolskaya**, **Kakhovskaya**, and **Varshavskaya**. As the name suggests, the faction possesses several hydroelectric turbines that use groundwater to generate electricity. It is one of the most important power sources in the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro), supplying the entire southern half of [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) with electricity.

Unfortunately, the stations under the faction's control are frequently attacked by [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants). The faction is also fully dependent on ammunition and food supplies from Hanza, which are not always sufficient.

Because of its strategic importance and hostile environment, most citizens of Sevastopolskaya HPP are trained soldiers. Barricades are set up at Sevastopolskaya to repel the hordes of mutants that often come from Chertanovskaya station. At Kakhovskaya, machine-gun nests are established and snipers rotate shifts to provide protection at all times. Varshavskaya itself is abandoned, but several turbines are located close enough to it that the station is usually considered part of Sevastopolskaya HPP as well.
