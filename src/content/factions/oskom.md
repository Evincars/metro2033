# OSKOM

**OSKOM Rapid Response Group** (Russian: Группа быстрого реагирования ОСКОМ) was a Russian military unit responsible for the defense of [Novosibirsk](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk) that subsequently came to be in charge of the survivors in the [Novosibirsk Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk_Metro) system.

The name **OSKOM** is an acronym, meaning **OS**obiy **KOM**itet (Russian: ОСобый КОМитет), which can be translated as "The Special Committee" or "The Emergency Committee" -- a fairly typical Russian name for a civil or military defence organisation. By the events of *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*, the entire group has been wiped out, except for the young boy [Kirill](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kirill_(Exodus)).

## Overview

OSKOM was presumably one of the [Russian Army](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Russian_Armed_Forces) units responsible for the defense and evacuation of Novosibirsk as [World War III](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_III) began. They were supported by a sizable armored unit that would eventually be wiped out by the cobalt bomb deployed against Novosibirsk. Unlike [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro)'s [Central Metro Command](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Central_Metro_Command), OSKOM was able to visibly remain in control of their metro and retain the cooperation of the civilian population for at least 20 years.

Their leadership included Lieutenant-General [Vinogradov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anatoly_Vinogradov) and later [Lieutenant-Colonel Khlebnikov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khlebnikov), who became the final leader of the organization. As radiation seeped into the shallow tunnels of the Novosibirsk Metro and anti-radiation medications ran low, riots broke out against the military leadership, eventually resulting in a civil war that wiped out most of the population.

## Role in Metro Exodus

OSKOM's story is explored in both the main game and [The Two Colonels](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Two_Colonels) DLC. In [The Dead City](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dead_City_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) chapter, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) travel through the ruins of Novosibirsk searching for a cure for Anna's radiation sickness. They discover the remnants of OSKOM's failed attempt to maintain order in the metro.

In *The Two Colonels* DLC, players experience the fall of the Novosibirsk Metro firsthand through the eyes of Colonel Khlebnikov, witnessing the organization's desperate final days as supplies dwindled, riots escalated, and [worms](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Worm) overran the tunnels.

## Trivia

- OSKOM is one of the few military organizations in the Metro universe that maintained effective governance over its metro system for an extended period.
- The organization's emblem features a shield design common to Russian military units.
- Kirill, Khlebnikov's son, is the sole known survivor of OSKOM by the events of Metro Exodus.
