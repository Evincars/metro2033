# 1905 Confederation

The **1905 Confederation** is a minor [faction](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Factions) in the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro) that is briefly mentioned in the books and referenced in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

- **Base location:** Ulitsa 1905 Goda
- **Disposition towards the player:** Neutral
- **Allies:** [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza)
- **Enemies:** [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) (2034)

## Overview

In [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)), the 1905 Confederation is an (assumed) left-leaning ideological confederation that fully controls the Barrikadnaya, Ulitsa 1905 Goda (which the Confederation is named after), and Begovaya stations. Originally, the Confederation also included [Polezhayevskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polezhayevskaya) and Oktyabr'skoe Pole, but both stations became abandoned following an accident at Oktyabr'skoe Pole, along with Polezhayevskaya being mysteriously attacked and its inhabitants slaughtered.

The Confederation then chose to demolish the tunnel between Polezhayevskaya and Begovaya to protect the remaining stations. Very little is known about the faction apart from this, though its proximity to [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) would suggest that the two are allies, or at least not hostile to each other. Its only known residents are [Mikhail Porfirevich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mikhail_Porfirevich) and [Vanechka](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vanechka).

### Metro: Last Light

Although never directly mentioned in *Metro: Last Light*, [Miller's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) map at [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)) shows that the [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) has taken over Barrikadnaya, one of the stations that used to belong to the 1905 Confederation and which also happens to be connected to Hanza.
