# 1905 Confederation

Occupying the Barrikadnaya, Ulitsa 1905 goda, and Begovaya stations, the 1905 Confederation is a small and little-known faction, likely a Hansa satellite. Its only known residents are [Mikhail Porfirevich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mikhail_Porfirevich) and [Vanechka](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vanechka).
