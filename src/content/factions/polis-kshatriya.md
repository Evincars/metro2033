# Polis Kshatriya

The **Polis Kshatriya** are the defensive militia of [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)) and the closest thing the station has to a military service. They appear in the *Metro 2033* novel, *Metro: Last Light*, and the Faction Pack DLC.

## Services

### Military

The Kshatriya are Polis's prime militia, guarding station entrances and patrolling nearby tunnels. The bulk of Polis's security, however, is provided by the [Spartan Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers).

### Stalkers

Like other groups, the Kshatriya also serve as [stalkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers). Polis commissions them to scavenge [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow) for pre-war items — computer parts, gun schematics, even posters — all to preserve pre-war culture and knowledge. Two named teams are known: one led by [Melnik](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Melnik) and one led by [Kuzmich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kuzmich), both of which ventured to the [Great Library](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Library) hunting for artifacts.

## Appearances

### Metro 2033

Kshatriya stalkers are not encountered in the [game](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)), but their military arm — the Rangers — appear frequently, and are seen guarding Polis during the level "[Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Metro_2033_Level))."

### Metro: Last Light

The Rangers again appear throughout the game, guarding Polis once during the level of the same name. The stalker branch appears in the DLC level "[Kshatriya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kshatriya_(Level))," featuring a five-member team: [Kuzmich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kuzmich) (weapons expert and likely leader), [Shaman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shaman) (artifact cataloguer), the stalker twins [Su](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Su) and [Bar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bar), and a [Ranger Trainee](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Trainee). Their assignment is to explore the Great Library for old-world knowledge, especially the fabled **"Map of Secret Metro"** that would help chart the [Metro 2](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2) lines. The stalker eventually recovers the map and returns alive, ending the assignment.

## Novel

In the *Metro 2033* novel, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) reaches Polis and seeks the Council's aid against the [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones) threatening [VDNKh](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/VDNKh_Station_(Location)). Believing Artyom to be a "chosen one" named at [Polyanka](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polyanka), the Council agrees to send help if he retrieves a sacred book. Alongside [Melnik](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Melnik), [Ten](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ten), and the Brahmin [Daniel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Danila), Artyom reaches the Great Library, where the group is ambushed by Librarians. Daniel is mortally wounded; Artyom mercy-kills him to also kill the Librarian behind him, recovers some papers, and — unable to return empty-handed — takes the surface route to [Arbatskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arbat_Confederation).

## Trivia

- It is never confirmed whether the book Artyom sought actually existed; given the Council's belief in Polyanka as a "Station of Destiny," it was likely invented.
- Kuzmich was probably a stalker himself before losing his leg, and now directs the team from base.
- Shaman is not truly a Kshatriya but a Brahmin — a Polis librarian who likely accompanies the team to identify and catalog artifacts.
