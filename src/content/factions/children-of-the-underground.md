# Children of the Underground

Residing in the mutant stricken station of [Paveletskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hole_Station_%28Location%29), Children of the Underground is the name for the forces protecting the station. Also known as Hole Station, Paveletskaya, missing its [hermetic door](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hermetic_Door), is constantly under attack by [mutant](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutant)s.

[Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) aids the Children of the Underground in fighting off the invaders, but the station eventually falls, with the Children of the Underground sacrificing themselves to cut off Paveletskaya and the mutants from the rest of the Metro.
