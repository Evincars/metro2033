# Children of the Underground

The **Children of the Underground** were a paramilitary group of Metro inhabitants that [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) encountered outside [Hole Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hole_Station_(Location)) (Paveletskaya). While holding a blockade to protect their settlement, they recruited Artyom to help defend it. The blockade ultimately failed as the [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants) broke through and slaughtered most of the settlement's people. The last survivor, a mortally wounded commander named [Maxim Komarov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Maxim_Komarov), tasked Artyom with relaying a message to [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)) — to destroy the tunnels (and with them their home) because the mutants could not be stopped.

## Relaying the message

The player receives positive Moral Points both for accepting this mission and for completing it. To accept it, the player must listen to the leader's dying words and take the tape from his hand as he offers it to Artyom.

To complete the mission, while on the surface level *Outpost* — on the way to Black Station — there is a transmitter at the top of the [Nazi](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nazis) outpost, guarded by two demons. Killing the demons and relaying the message unlocks the *DJ Artyom* achievement.

## The message

> "Polis — Children of the Underground here. If you're receiving this, it means that our Station has been overrun by mutants... and whoever is relaying the message is the last survivor of the valiant men covering the evacuation. You must demolish the tunnels. The airlock won't last long. This is Commander Maxim Komarov... Over and out."

## Trivia

- No matter how many mutants the player kills, the blockade always ends in failure — even if every attacking mutant is somehow slain.
- At the barricade, it is not shown exactly how some members of the group die; a wave of energy sweeps down the tunnel, knocking out the player and his comrades. It is presumed that more mutants invade the station when the wave hits.
- The Children of the Underground are probably a stand-in for the [Revolutionaries](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolutionaries), another paramilitary force who briefly aid Artyom in the novel.
- The name references the Vladimir Korolenko novel commonly abridged as *Children of the Underground*.
