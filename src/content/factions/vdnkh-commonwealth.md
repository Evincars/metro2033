# VDNKh Commonwealth

The **VDNKh Commonwealth** (Russian: *Содружество ВДНХ*) is a confederation of three stations — [VDNKh](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)), [Alekseyevskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alexeyevskaya), and [Rizhskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_Station_(Location)) — formed by some inhabitants of the Kaluzhsko-Rizhskaya Line in response to the perceived threat from the [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones).

- **Base location:** [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location))
- **Leadership:** separate station administrations
- **Disposition towards the player:** Friendly
- **Allies:** [Spartan Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers), [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location))
- **Enemies:** [Mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants), [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones) (formerly)

## Overview

Recently formed, the stations of the Commonwealth joined forces to further empower the line's mushroom tea industry and to prevent annexation from [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza). Every station in the Commonwealth has its own administration (formed from actual former Metro administration workers, even from the first Central Control era), and the cooperation consists of free travel for traders and citizens between the member stations. Passports are issued for each station to allow travel.

There is strict law enforcement and harsh punishment on all stations, and VDNKh enforces labor duties on every citizen. All men from VDNKh must take armed watch in all of the tunnels leading to VDNKh, including the northern tunnel to [Botanicheskiy Sad](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Botanicheskiy_Sad) where the [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones) assault from. A reserve troop of men is also on watch, carrying spotlights and large machine guns, ready if anything suspicious is called in by the guards in the tunnel.

This trio of stations is one of the few radial governments situated in a dead end that can actually make ends meet and even spare some resources for culture. They have a small library of their own and books purchased from Polis, along with a high literacy rate and courses for their children. The stability and relative prosperity are primarily due to "specialty" tea production, made for half of the Metro; this attracts people, but the VDNKh Commonwealth is not very immigrant-friendly, rarely letting anyone new into VDNKh.

## In the Game

In the game, VDNKh starts by [VDNKh](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) and [Rizhskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_Station_(Location)) forming an alliance in response to the immediate [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones) threat.

## Proximity to Botanicheskiy Sad

[Botanicheskiy Sad](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Botanicheskiy_Sad) (Botanical Gardens), the station after VDNKh, was lost due to fire and mutant threats early in Metro history, but the tunnel could not be blown up due to the threat of an underground water breach. Nor was it closed, as the hermetic doors had been dismantled even earlier, during the [Central Metro Command](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Central_Metro_Command) times. No one tried to resettle Botanichesky Sad, as something horrible dwelt in the Garden above it.

After [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom), as a teen, and some of his friends opened the hermetic doors at Botanichesky Sad while trying to see the outside world, the Dark Ones came down and began methodically assaulting VDNKh after setting up a base in Botanichesky Sad. At first the Dark Ones sent their envoys, but humans killed them all, slowly changing the Dark Ones' opinion of humanity. With no means of cutting the tunnel off, VDNKh in a few years turned into a war-torn place, filled with fear of constant assault from the Dark Ones.
