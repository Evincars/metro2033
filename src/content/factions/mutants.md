# Mutants

A generalized term used to describe the many non-human creatures living in the Metro tunnels and on the surface of the Moscow ruins. Most mutants are not organized, as they are mainly considered animals. However, they commonly do have pack structures and are known to attack other mutant species and any humans they come across.

This 'faction' is hostile to almost everyone, except stronger mutants or during times of extreme peril, such as when Artyom and a [watchman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchmen) pack worked together to defeat the mutant [bear](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bear). One of the few mutant species that could be sentient is the [librarians](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Librarians), who are known to set traps for [stalkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers), and even grow plants.
