# Huliaipole Republic

The **Huliaipole Republic** (Russian: Гуляй-польская республика) is an anarcho-communist faction of the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro). It is mentioned in *Track Marks*, appears in *[Dark Tunnels](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Tunnels_(Novel))* and *[Ghost Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ghost_Station)*, and is featured on the map of *[Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035)*.

> "Death to all those who stand in the way of freedom for working people."
> — Banner of the Makhnovshchyna

## History

The faction formed during the early 2020s after **Mikhail Voikov**, a former high-ranking general originally from the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line), had a major falling out with Secretary General [Andrey Moskvin](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Andrey_Moskvin) and his associates, whom he considered traitors to the revolutionary ideals. Mikhail and the followers loyal to him renounced the Red Line and its Stalinist ideology in favour of **anarcho-communism**, establishing a commune across three stations at the north end of the [Zamoskvoretskaya Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Zamoskvoretskaya_Line).

## Overview

Voikov and his followers migrated to Voykovskaya station, which they renamed **Huliaipole** — the main base of the faction. **Vodny Stadion** serves as their cultural centre, and **Rechnoy Vokzal** functions as the farm station. Together, these three stations form the anarcho-communist community of the Huliaipole Republic.

The republic is guided by Mikhail Voikov, who took the pseudonym **"Nestor"** after his comrades noted that the way he coordinated raids against the Red Line and [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) during the [Hansa – Red Line War](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hansa_-_Red_Line_War) was reminiscent of the Ukrainian revolutionary Nestor Makhno. Decisions in the republic are made through votes in a council system inspired by the historical Free Soviets.

The anarchists hold the philosophers **Pyotr Kropotkin** and **Mikhail Bakunin** in very high regard, and possess all of their books, obtained by sending [stalkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers) out to the [Great Library](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Library). **Anatoliy Tomski**, the protagonist of *[Dark Tunnels](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Tunnels_(Novel))*, hails from the republic; in the book he leads a squad of soldiers from Voykovskaya on a special mission given to them by Nestor.

## Military

The anarchists field a large number of soldiers and stalkers, as well as impressive weapons reserves. Despite the abundance of gangster "rabble" at Voykovskaya, Nestor easily rallies everyone together, instilling iron discipline through his oratorical speeches. The anarchists frequently organise military campaigns, most led by Nestor himself. As vehicles, the fellowship uses motorised railcars called **"carriages"** mounted with Kalashnikov light machine guns.

## Trivia

- Huliaipole takes its name from a town (Ukrainian: Гуляйполе) in the Zaporizhia Oblast of Ukraine — the birthplace of Nestor Makhno and for a time the capital of his Free Territory. Between 1917 and 1921, amid the wars in the region, Huliaipole changed hands 16 times.
- The red and black diagonal colours seen on the faction's flag are traditionally associated with anarcho-syndicalists and anarcho-communists.
