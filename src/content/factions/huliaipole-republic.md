# Huliaipole Republic

An anarcho-communist faction consisting of 3 stations at the northwest end of the Zamoskvoretskaya line, they are in conflict with Hanza and the Red Line. The protagonist of *[Dark Tunnels](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Tunnels_%28Novel%29)* comes from this faction.
