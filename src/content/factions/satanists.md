# Satanists

The **Satanists** are a community of malevolent [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Metro_System) inhabitants who believe that the Metro is a pathway to Hell.

## Overview

In *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel))*, a surviving witness describes how he was kidnapped at Belorusskaya and brought to the Satanists' station. He did not recognize the station, and its exact location is never revealed by Glukhovsky in the first novel. The station is described as vandalized — name-boards ripped off, blood-stained walls and floor — with a huge pit at the center of the platform, perhaps 30 meters deep, where slaves kidnapped from other stations dig ever further down in an attempt to reach Hell itself.

The Satanists believe the end of the world has already happened and that the Moscow Metro is the gate to the underworld — if they dig deep enough, they will reach Hell and meet Satan, the light-bearer, himself. Most Satanists are armed and maintain no real industry, brutally murdering slaves on a constant basis. It is suggested by one character that they may occupy Lyublino station, though given there is only one witness, the true location remains highly questionable.

## Revelations in later novels

In *Dark Tunnels*, the station is revealed to actually be [Timiryazevskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Timiryazevskaya), settled by the Satanists some time after it was abandoned by survivors of a rat attack. The faction is led by a manipulative middle-aged man known as **Claw** (Russian: Коготь, *Kogot*), who keeps most of his followers under control through the consumption of hallucinogenic mushrooms. Almost every other night the Satanists brew potent broths from these mushrooms and drink until they pass out across the station, leaving only a few sentries on guard. To maintain control, Claw also stages brutal ritual executions of prisoners deemed heretics.

The devil-worshippers keep prisoners dirty and malnourished, locked in metal cages, used solely as slave labor for digging the pit — believing that the "enemies of Satan," such as Christians, are meant to do the digging. They use a network of hidden tunnels to reach other stations. [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) secretly maintains limited relations with the Satanists through traders (primarily from Mendeleevskaya and Novoslobodskaya), as the cult has access to a mysterious source of fuel. In exchange, Hanza trades food, weapons, and ammunition, and will even hand over "undesirables" — drunks, prisoners, and non-Hanza citizens — into slavery, considering the Satanists "excellent business partners."

Timiryazevskaya is covered in pentagrams, drawings of Baphomet, and satanic curses written in Latin. Many denizens bear the same tattoos and wear necklaces of rat teeth, using primitive clothing and keeping poor hygiene — all except Claw, who always appears clean and well-dressed. The protagonist of *Dark Tunnels*, Anatoliy Tomski, noted that Claw behaved more like a bureaucrat or businessman than a deranged cult leader; new prisoners are given two options — dig the pit as slaves, or be tied up and burned alive in a ritual sacrifice, forced first to drink the mushroom broth to "cleanse them of their Earthly life."

Another prominent figure is a blind old man named **Charon** (Russian: Харон), named after the ferryman of Hades. According to the *Pipha-2* novel, from 2014 until March 2033 Charon was the sole leader of the Satanists, and the cult originally began in the dungeons of [Kitai-Gorod](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kitai-Gorod). After a bound slave girl escaped, the supporting caves collapsed and blinded Charon; his assistant Claw then became leader while Charon stepped down to become his partner in crime, and the cult migrated to Timiryazevskaya. Charon grows and gathers the hallucinogenic mushrooms, lures victims into the cult's hands, and — despite his blindness — claims to "see much farther and wider" thanks to constant mushroom use. He also proved immune to the telepathy of the mutant Mobat during an escape attempt.

The Satanists are constantly at war with the **Northern Emirate**, an Islamic society at Petrovsko-Razumovskaya station (the next station above Timiryazevskaya), which has declared a holy war against the "so-called sons of Shaitan." Lacking any real military beyond a mob of armed thugs, the Satanists have only survived thanks to the large arsenal traded to them by Hanza. Most are believed to have been eliminated by 2035, with Claw killed in early 2034 according to the *Rublyovka-2* novel.
