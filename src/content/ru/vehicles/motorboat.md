## Обзор

A fiberglass boat with an external engine, this vehicle and others like it are ideal for quickly getting around the flooded ruins of [Vladivostok](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vladivostok), being both fast and maneuverable.

## Интересные факты

- Motorboats are found with either their original steering wheels or replacement bicycle handlebars.
  - Regardless of what type of steering they have, all motorboats use the same driving animation as [Saul's Van](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Saul's_Van).
- Motorboats have headlights that can be turned on with the flashlight button. They will remain on if the player exits the vehicle.
