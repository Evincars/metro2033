**Within a Hair of Death** is a secret [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and a regular achievement/trophy in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux) that can be obtained in the level [Revolution](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolution).

## Обзор

This achievement is earned by escaping from the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line). It is a story achievement/trophy and will simply be unlocked upon the completion of [Revolution](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolution).
