**Survivor 2033 **is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Обзор

This achievement is awarded for completing the game in Survival Mode. Survival Mode is similar in playstyle to the original [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)), with less [ammunition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/ammunition), supplies, [filter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Filters) time, reload and aiming time, as well as the analogue [watch](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro-Made_Watch). This must be done in one playthrough.
