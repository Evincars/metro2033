**Quick Draw** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) and [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Обзор

This achievement is awarded for successfully defending [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) during the level [Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Level)) from the [nosalis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) attack by keeping the metal gratings intact. This can be fairly easy to earn, but requires skill and focus. - It's not as simple as shooting the nosalises attacking any of the 4 grates in the room before they break through them, as there are Nosalises that drop from the ceiling and target you with no help from the NPCs. The best strategy is to clear one Nosalis per grate, then deal with the one that drops as quick as you can, then clear another round of grates, then the two Nosalises that drop, then the rest of the ones at the grates.
