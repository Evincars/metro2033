**Autumn** is the tenth level overall level in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*, and the fourth and final of the seasonal intermission levels set solely aboard the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora). Spirits are low as [Anna's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_(Character)) condition worsens and [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) suggests they head to [Novosibirsk](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk), and the source of the experimental medicine.

## Обзор
Following the revelation that the hydroelectric dam will eventually fail and flood [the valley](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Taiga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) in radioactive sediment, the Aurora continues east and towards their temporary goal of Novosibirsk. Having gone through her mothers notes, [Katya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Katya) has discovered that an experimental military drug, [Renergan-F](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Renergan-F), was produced there. During one of Anna's attacks, Katya ushers [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) out of the room, to where a sympathetic [Krest](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Krest) is waiting. He beckons Artyom to the bridge, where the colonel will announce their next move.

Out of options, and rapidly running out of time, Miller proposes Novosibirsk as their next destination, however [Idiot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Idiot) has some bad news. Having studied the radiation maps, he learned that radiation levels are marked as green on the maps, and purple indicates errors, however the small print explains that areas outside or normal ranges are also marked as purple. Having previously thought Novosibirsk was relatively radiation free, it is now revealed that the city is most likely lethally radioactive. Miller proposes that only he and Artyom will venture into the city, as they are the most responsible for Anna and her condition. Despite the objections of the crew, Miller calls an end to the discussion and tasks them with searching the nearby [Railway museum](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Railway_museum) and the academic district of the city to the south.

Elsewhere on the train, Krest and [Yermak](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Yermak) discuss the railway museum, where Yermak reveals his vast knowledge on the railway, as well as the various exhibits there. One of the objectives for the rest of the crew, is an experimental snow plow that Yermak explains came from [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow) many years before the war. At the head of the train, [Sam](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam) is monitoring radiation levels, and talks to Artyom about his life in the [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro) and how Miller saved him from the hands of a lynch mob. At the rear of the train [Tokarev](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tokarev) is busy modifying two spartan suits for Miller and Artyom. Whilst there, he tells Artyom how he gained his nickname of Tokarev, and his run in with a bandit. If [Duke](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duke) and [Damir](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Damir) are aboard the Aurora, they will be seen in their cabin, and will assure Artyom that they will be there for him.

## Интересные факты
*If Artyom returns to Anna after meeting with Miller and the rest of the crew, there will be a postcard depicting the Pacific Ocean on her chest. Artyom must reach Anna before Katya returns to her in order to see this.
*In the looped scenery outside, along the track are two locations in which the Aurora passes some survivors. One of them is a dog, while the other is a human. Both appear to be alive. 
*If Artyom interacts with the radio, there will be a transmission in which [Kirill](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kirill_(Exodus)) tries to contact Colonel [Khlebnikov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khlebnikov).
