## Обзор

Olga (Russian: Ольга) is a high-ranking member of the [Children of the Forest](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Children_of_the_Forest), holding the rank of Instructor among the Pioneers. She is one of their best fighters, with only [Roman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Roman), their leader, being able to best her in combat. Despite her fighting prowess, Olga often acts as the voice of reason within the group and advocates for peaceful solutions. She wields a [Helsing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Helsing) crossbow and is a skilled survivalist in the dense forests of the Taiga region.

## Роль в Metro Exodus

Olga plays a crucial role during [The Taiga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Taiga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) chapter of Metro Exodus. She rescues [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) from drowning in a river after [Krest](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Krest)'s railcar becomes derailed by a landslide triggered by the [Forest Master](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bear), a massive bear-like mutant. Olga keeps Artyom's survival a secret from the other Children and tells him to stay out of sight. She also helps [Alyosha](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alyosha) escape after he is captured by the Children. Olga is encountered again at a church where the Children's revered former leader, known as [the Teacher](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Teacher_(Metro_Exodus)), is buried. A romantic connection develops between Olga and Alyosha during the events of the chapter. The player's actions toward the Children of the Forest directly affect both Olga and Alyosha's fates.
