## Обзор

Colonel Vyacheslav "Slava" Khlebnikov (Russian: Вячеслав "Слава" Хлебников) is a soldier of the [OSKOM](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/OSKOM) Rapid Response Unit who maintained order in the [Novosibirsk Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk_Metro). He is the father of Private [Kirill Khlebnikov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kirill_(Exodus)) and one of the last survivors of [Novosibirsk](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk). Originally a Lieutenant Colonel, he was promoted to full Colonel for successfully preventing rioters from capturing Sibirskaya station on June 5th, 2034. Khlebnikov died on November 20th, 2035, during the final collapse of the Novosibirsk Metro.

## Роль в Metro Exodus

Khlebnikov is mentioned in the main campaign of [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) and serves as the playable protagonist of [The Two Colonels](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Two_Colonels) DLC. In this expansion, players experience the final days of the Novosibirsk Metro through Khlebnikov's eyes. The DLC reveals the desperate situation in Novosibirsk, where the metro system was slowly dying due to extreme radiation on the surface (caused by a cobalt bomb) and dwindling resources. Khlebnikov struggles with the moral dilemma of whether to tell the populace the truth about their hopeless situation or maintain the official lies to prevent panic. His story intersects with [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller)'s -- the "two colonels" of the title -- as Miller arrives in Novosibirsk searching for [Renergan-F](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Renergan-F) to save [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna). Khlebnikov's personal logs and diary entries, discovered by Miller and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom), serve as the framing device for the DLC's narrative.
