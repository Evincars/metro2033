## Обзор

Nastya (Russian: Настя) is [Katya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Katya)'s daughter, born on April 6, 2029. The two originally hail from the southern Urals but moved west to the [Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Volga) region in search of a better life. Unfortunately, they fell under the control of [Silantius](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Silantius) and the [Church of the Water Tsar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Water_Tsar), a technophobic cult. Nastya hated the cult and frequently renounced their religion. She also strongly disliked Silantius, recalling how he once found her flashlight and smashed it in anger due to his technophobia. Despite the horrors of the post-apocalyptic world, Nastya remains a curious and imaginative child, fascinated by the world around her.

## Роль в Metro Exodus

Nastya and her mother Katya are rescued from the Church of the Water Tsar by [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) during the Volga chapter and join the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) crew. Aboard the train, Nastya brings a sense of innocence and wonder to the otherwise hardened group of Rangers and survivors. She finds beauty in unexpected things, even going so far as to call the flying mutants known as [Demons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Demon) "majestic and beautiful." Nastya's presence aboard the Aurora gives the crew something to protect and fight for beyond mere survival. Her stepfather becomes [Stepan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stepan), who marries Katya during the journey. Nastya accompanies the crew all the way to [Lake Baikal](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lake_Baikal), representing the hope for a new generation in a post-apocalyptic world.
