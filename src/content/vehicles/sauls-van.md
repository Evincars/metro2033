**Saul's Van** is a [vehicle](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicles) in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*. It serves as [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s main mode of transportation in the [Caspian Desert](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Caspian_Sea) and [Novosibirsk](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk).

## Overview

The van is first seen when [Saul](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Saul) and another [raider](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Munai-bailer) use it to intercept the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) upon arrival in the Caspian Desert, then proceed to drive off to inform [their leader](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Baron). A short time later, Artyom infiltrates their outpost, and after a confrontation with Saul, acquires the key to the van. Once acquired, Artyom will use it to traverse the desert, and drive to various points of interest. Following the events in the Caspian Desert, the van is loaded onto the Aurora and brought along on the crew's journey.

Saul's van is later modified and fitted with lead sheeting, which allows Artyom and [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) to drive through the radioactive ruins of Novosibirsk without getting critically irradiated. They are forced to abandon the van, however, when they find the road blocked by a tank. While Artyom heads to the research institute to look for a cure for [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_Miller), Miller returns to the van with [Kirill](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kirill_(Exodus)) and drives to the [SatCom Centre](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk_Satellite_Communications_Center) to find information regarding a habitable place for the Aurora crew. As Artyom manages to escape the institute, but is exhausted due to radiation poisoning, Miller rescues him and takes him to the van. Miller manages to drive out of the city before passing out, forcing Artyom to drive the rest of the way. Although Miller dies from radiation poisoning, Artyom and Kirill make it back to the Aurora, and the van is taken aboard again.

At some point after the Aurora's crew establish a new home at [Lake Baikal](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lake_Baikal), [Sam](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam) will take the van on a journey to [Vladivostok](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vladivostok). It breaks down along the way, however, forcing him to abandon it and travel on foot until he finds a tractor to drive the rest of the way.

## Description

In its original condition, Saul's van is rusting, missing several windows and one of its headlamps, and has bike handlebars as a makeshift steering wheel. Despite the vehicle's decrepit appearance, its engine is in good running condition. It is later given improvements, including; lead sheeting, new headlamps and windows, and a proper steering wheel.

## Trivia

- If Artyom attempts to enter any other vehicle, a lock symbol will show up. This is due to Artyom not having a key for them.
- Saul's van is based on the [UAZ-452](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/wikipedia:UAZ-452) (Russian: УАЗ-452), a van produced by the Russian automobile manufacturer, [Ulyanovsk Automobile Plant](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/wikipedia:Ulyanovsk_Automobile_Plant). The van is colloquially known as "Bukhanka" (Russian for "loaf"), due to an appearance that resembles a loaf of bread. This nickname is heard, at least in the Russian dub, when Artyom goes through Baron's stronghold, and it is referred to as such in the [Driver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Driver) and [Carmaheddon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Carmaheddon) [achievements](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies).
- The original colour scheme of the van (although hard to recognize due to its condition) is used by the [Emergency Gas Service](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/wikipedia:Emergency_Gas_Service) in [Russia](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Russia) and some of its neighbouring countries.
