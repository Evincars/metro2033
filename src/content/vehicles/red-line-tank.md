The **Red Line Tank** is an armoured vehicle used by the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro%3A_Last_Light)* to break the lines of entrenched enemies. It's the Red equivalent to the [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) [Panzer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Panzer).

## Overview
While the Red Line tank is referred to as a tank by [Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) and Reich soldiers, it's simply an armoured railcar with a turret affixed to the top. The tank is made out of a motorized flat-railbed with steam engine wheels. It has a rotating turret in the front and a [DShK 12.7mm machine gun](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DShK_12.7mm_Machinegun) nest on the rear. The main gun attached to the turret fires rockets, unlike the Panzer which fires artillery shells, and has closeable metal doors to protect the crew whilst reloading. Only two of these tanks were known to exist in the Metro.

The Red Line tank is, by all means a war machine that lack any form of elegancy, and not even an uniformity of different eras: the 18th century style main gun is mounted on a 19th century style steamed train chassis, and the bulk head imitating 20th century panzer III turret appearance. It is very likely that the Red Line tank used salvaged parts from destroyed Reich panzer, it would be cost efficient and good for morale, as showing off these parts like trophies marks Red Line triumphs over the Reich.

The tank itself could be roughly divided into three sections from the head to the tail: the engine compartment, the main gun platform, and the heavy machine gun nest. 

Judging by the outlook of it, the tank is driven by steam engine, and power is transmitted to the rear driving wheels via complexed piston-tie rod system. The engine itself cannot be directly observed from the outside, only a chimney to be found in the middle front position of the chassis. The position of the chimney would have an negative impact on the visibility of the forward field of view. A pair of large barrier breaking wheels are also mounted at the front, assumed to be capable of grinding through possible obstacles on the forward path.

The main gun is technically not mount on a "turret", but a “barbette”. The gun plat form is not fully enclosed, but is open at the top and the rear. For the frontal direction, which actually faces enemy however, two layers of armor shields are placed: the exterior layer is a pair of wedge-shaped arranged armor doors that only slide open when the gun is ready to fire, and close tight when the gun is reloading; the interior layer is a fixed notched curve-shaped gun shield. The main gun is a breech loading gun that uses rocket as ammunition. The rockets pile up at the rear end of the gun platform, and are loaded into the main gun with the aid of a ramrod.

At the rear of the tank is a small fortified platform with a mounted heavy machine gun, the gun nest protects the Red Line tank's hemisphere, and provides fire power reinforcement in aggressive missions. The two pairs of large diameter driving wheels are also mounted on this section of the tank, completely exposed and with out any forms of protection, creating a known weak point that could be used by the enemies to their advantage.

## Metro: Last Light
The Red's Tank is first seen during the [Battle of D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_D6), showing up after the Red Army's failed first wave of infantry. It enters D6 from the opposite platform to the Ranger's defensive line accompanied by light infantry. Artyom manages to immobilize the tank with a [Preved](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Preved) by shooting off the wheels' coupling rod, then the wheels proper. The tank is finally destroyed with a shot to the main gun just before it fired, igniting the ammunition, causing it to self-destruct.

A second tank was seen at the [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_%28Location%29), during a [battle](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Squad) between the Reich and the Red Line. The Red Line used it as a final attempt to destroy the Reich's defensive line held [Hans](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hans) and the shocktroopers. Hans destroys the tank with the [Medved](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Medved) after a number of shots, ending the Red Line's assault.
