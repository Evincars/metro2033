The **Red Line Armoured Train** is a functioning [vehicle](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicles) and special [weapon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons) of the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line), utilized as an incredibly powerful battering ram.

## Overview
Salvaged from an unknown tunnel or station and built up with a massive amount of steel, the armoured train stands as the single most dangerous weapon of war in possession of the Red Line. Despite this, it remains relatively inactive and unheard of for most of *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light)*. It serves as a set-piece item for the opening of the level "[Revolution](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolution)", where it's seen being constructed, and is mentioned in passing by [Andrew](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Andrew) in "[Regina](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Regina_%28Level%29)". However, it isn't used and not vital to the plot until the final wave of enemies in [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_%28Metro_Last_Light_Level%29). The armoured train is possibly the answer to the Rangers' fortification of D6 and the "Panzers" used by the Nazis. In its brief last appearance, it is the deciding weapon that cripples the [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) by destroying D6's platform - who had previously killed several dozen soldiers of the Red Army, a tank, as well as a shield and [flamethrower](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/flamethrower) unit. Though the train obviously destroyed in the [C'est la Vie](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/C%27est_la_Vie_%28Achievement%29) [ending](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Endings) of *Metro: Last Light*, what exactly happens to it in the [Redemption](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Redemption_%28Achievement%29) ending is unknown. However, it's possible that the train was rendered useless by destroying the platform.

The Red Line armoured train can be separated into two sections: the train head, and the engine compartment, judging by the outlooks of the unfinished armour train. Both sections of the armoured train are fully embodied in an armour shell, the bone structure of the armour shell is made of angle bar, wielded together, and the armour plates are attached to the bone by wielding and bolt tightening (for frontal armour only) . Besides the two sections' own armour casing, there is an extra top cover hanging above the connecting position of the two sections. The thickness of the armour plates varies from different positions, with the frontal armour undoubtedly being the thickest of them all. The armour plates in the lower front portion of the train head is stacked like shutter, leaving gaps between each plates allowing air inlet but forbids direction penetration lest for the diagonal upward incoming direction at close range.

For armament, the Red Line armoured train has chosen a main gun mounted on a turret with a basket, the turret does not seem to have any openings for observing or aiming at the current construction status. The gun itself, judging by the existence of muzzle brake and bore evacuator, could possibly be a pre-war weapon, but this cannot be confirmed by any direct proves.At both side of the train head there is a walk way for personal, the Red Line armoured train added two machine gun turrets to both walk ways, but until the Red Line launched the final assault toward D6, the machine guns were still not mounted on each side.

At the forehead of the armoured train there is a big search light protected by wire fences. On each side of the train head there is a group of search light consisted of 4 bulbs, the side search light cluster is partially covered by two rotatable plates on the top and at the bottom, whose roles may act as shade boards.

The Fourth Reich has also build its own armored train similar to the Red Line armored train. Judging by the looks, the frontal half of the Reich armored train is almost the copy of Red Line counterpart, with only a few detail differences at the main search light and the sign. The Reich armored train uses a turret similar to Reich panzer, and does not have an armored cover in the middle. Unlike its Red Line counterpart, the rear half of the Reich armored train is a standard train wagon, not an armor box, and does not contain anything that looks like engines inside, indicating it could be possibly used as passenger compartment. If the assumption is correct, then the driving system could only be integrated into the front half of the Reich armored train. The Reich armored train could be seen in the beginning of Metro Last Light game level "Separation". Though its construction is finished earlier than the Red Line counterpart (which is seen in Revolution level and only in half finished status), the Reich armored train never saw true actions in the following game experience.

A very similar armoured train called the [Cruiser](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Cruiser) is seen again in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*, this time operated by [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza). It intercepts the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora), as [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and the Rangers are escaping [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow). Artyom is forced to sneak aboard the train and plant an explosive in the boiler. He then jumps back aboard the Aurora and blows it up.

## Trivia
- In the level "[Separation](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Separation_%28Metro_Last_Light_Level%29)", the [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) has an armoured train which can be seen in most of the entire level, with their logo on the front replacing the red star.
- Despite being armed, the train never uses its turret gun. The Reds only use the train as a battering ram.
- It's unknown what type of engine and fuel the Red Line Armoured Train uses. The base of the train are standard Metro carriages, which are electrically-powered, but as electric 3rd rails in the Metro tunnels are not powered anymore, it cannot be using their original propulsion. Furthermore, the train might be too heavy to be powered by the original set of electromotors while still maintaining the great speed seen in *Metro: Last Light*, given all its additionally-mounted armour. It's possible the Reds added a combustion engine (salvaged from one of the many locomotives or armoured vehicles found on the surface) to power their train.
