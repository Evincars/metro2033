## Characteristics

Created after [World War III](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_III) by gunsmiths employed by [Tom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tom) in [Vladivostok](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vladivostok), incendiary 5.45x39mm was designed for use by the Sammy, an assault rifle created by the same gunsmiths. It is a highly potent round capable of killing most mutants and humans in a couple of shots, but the "hotter" load meant that it was capable of fouling the gas systems of older [Kalashes](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M)).

Because of this, the Sammy's magazines and parts were designed to not be interchangeable with other rifles, so Tom's men didn't ruin rifles by trying to run the incendiary rounds through them. In spite of this, these rounds are an invaluable resource for surviving in Vladivostok, and help [Sam](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam) make it through the ruined city.

## Trivia

- Incendiary 5.45x39mm functions identically to the [Military Grade Rounds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Grade_Rounds) found in the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro) during *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)* and *[Metro Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Redux)*. In these games, MGRs were able to be used in all standard rifles, from the [Bastard](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bastard) to the [Kalash 2012](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_2012), with no repercussions or restrictions other than availability.
- Despite the Sammy supposedly using proprietary magazines specifically designed to load these rounds, a standard Kalash mag would be able to fit them with no problems, as they are of the same dimensions as standard 5.45x39mm cartridges.
- Unlike Military Grade Rounds, the corpse of an enemy that was set on fire by incendiary 5.45x39mm will deal fire damage to the player.
