> — Metro Exodus in-game description

## Overview

Ball bearings are metal 15mm or 0.59 inch balls. In both *Metro 2033* and *Metro: Last Light*, they are found in long cartridges containing up to 15 balls. These can be purchased in most shops in the game. In *Metro 2033*, they have the best exchange rate, as 1 [MGR](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR) buys 8 pellets, though they hold little resale value, given how many need to be sold in exchange for one MGR. Trading MGR for ball bearings is only advised if the [Tikhar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) or [Hellbreath](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hellbreath) are [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s primary weapons.

In *Metro: Last Light*, the value of ball bearings has increased, as 10 ball bearings now cost 8 MGRs to buy and only 10 ball bearings can be exchanged for one MGR, instead of 16.

In *Metro Exodus*, ball bearings are available as the ammunition for the Tikhar, which is given to Artyom by [Tokarev](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tokarev) at the beginning of [Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)). An advantage of ball bearings is that they can be crafted in the field, using Artyom's [backpack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/backpack), and they are also the cheapest ammo type to craft. Otherwise, they are very uncommon, as nobody else, apart from the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) crew, uses the Tikhar, being a Metro-made weapon.

## Acquisition

Ball bearings are a rather uncommon type of ammunition, and are not as common as [shotgun shells](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/12x70_Buckshot) or [dirty rounds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dirty_5.45x39mm). While on lower difficulty in Metro 2033, each pickup will gain 15 ball bearings, on the highest difficultly, only 5 to 8 will be acquired. Furthermore, ball bearings are also the cheapest form of ammunition, as one military-grade round will net Artyom eight ball bearings at an [exchange kiosk](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/exchange_kiosk), so buying most ball bearing ammo is a viable strategy, especially since the guns that use ball bearings are high-damage weapons, that can kill anything in one shot with a headshot (as long as the weapon in pumped/charged enough). Given their rarity, if they are regularly used, purchasing as many as possible is advised.

## Use

Ball bearings are only used in the [Tihar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) and [Volt Driver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Volt_Driver) in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033)*, so it's best to carry a backup [Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M)) or [Uboinik](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Uboinik) in case the supply of pellets has been depleted.

They see even less use in *Metro: Last Light*, as the main campaign only has the Tikhar, so their use is diminished until one can be found. The first Tikhar available is found in the [Facility](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Facility) level, on the walkway above the gas room, with some rounds. Ball bearings are uncommon in *Metro: Last Light*, so purchasing as many as possible when at a station is advised, although Artyom can only carry up to 90 ball bearings. They are also used in Metro: Last Light's DLC, as ammunition for the Hellbreath (Volt Driver).
