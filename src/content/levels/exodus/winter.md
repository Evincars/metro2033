**Winter** is the third [level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Levels) in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*, and the first of the seasonal intermission levels set solely on the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora). This level is the shortest of the season levels and has the least amount of activities to do on the train.  

## Overview
The level begins with the fallout of the [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) actions in [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow). Having stopped 100 kilometers outside of the city, [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) briefs the crew on the real situation gripping [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow). [The War](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_III) is still ongoing, the [Invisible Watchers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Invisible_Watchers) do indeed exist and pull the strings of the [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro), and Moscow has been ordered by the remnants of the government to 'play dead' and maintain radio silence to avoid further bombing. 

[Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_(Character)) and [Stepan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stepan_(Metro_2035)) return from outside checking their [Geiger counters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Geiger_Counter), revealing the air is clean, breathable and radiation free. Anna then berates [her father](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) for knowingly allowing [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) to continue to risk his life searching for outside life whilst Miller had known the truth all along. He responds with saying that the [Invisible Watchers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Invisible_Watchers) only made themselves known to him after the [Battle for D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_for_D6), and threatened him with a strict "You talk, you die" policy. Miller then asks Artyom to search the airwaves for any mention of [the Ark](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/the_Ark) project, the government bunker in the Ural mountains housing the remnants of the government. Artyom manages to patch into a pre-recorded message from the Ark, giving instructions to any survivors to make their way to Mount Yamantau where the government will house any survivors of the war. An elated Miller proposes a toast and declares Yamantau their destination, with [Idiot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Idiot) suggesting naming the train [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora), in reference to the [Roman goddess of the dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurora_(mythology)), and to the [Naval cruiser of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_cruiser_Aurora) that started the [October revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_Revolution). 

[Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) then speaks to [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) privately, justifying his silence in regards to knowing about the outside world, saying that the [Watchers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Invisible_Watchers) needed him to train new recruits after the [Battle for D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_for_D6), and how he believes that losing his men, and losing his legs was justified if it meant that it would save the [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro) and [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow) itself. He likens the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) to a marathon runner, and warns Artyom not to trip them up during the journey. 

Meanwhile on the train, [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) and [Alyosha](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alyosha) discuss his injury, [Duke](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duke) is shoveling coal, [Tokarev](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tokarev) and [Stepan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stepan) are taking inventory and [Damir](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Damir), [Sam](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam) and [Idiot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Idiot) are stood at the head of the train, discussing philosophy among other things.
