# Echoes

> "We're almost at the Theater now. The Metro entrance should be pretty close. But however small the distance is, we'll have to cover it on the surface. And there, every step you take could well be your last."
> — Level description

**Echoes** is the ninth level of [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

## Overview

In this level, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov) must cross the dangerous surface, passing through the wreckage of [Flight 76715](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Flight_76715), in order to reach [Theatre Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Theatre_Station). This is the first level where the dramatic climate changes since the first game can be fully witnessed — the shift is clearly abnormal, as shown by Pavel's reaction to the rain. Inside the downed airliner, Artyom experiences a vivid flashback of the plane's final moments before the bombs fell.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- After sliding down the building and encountering the sudden rain, crawl into the sewage pipe on the left until you reach a room with a skeleton.
- Reach the skeleton by the tail of the crashed blue plane after the sinkhole.
- Enter the small caved-in room with ammo and filters after searching the spider-infested skeleton.
- Take the [Duplet](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duplet) from the service room before the stairs out of the subway.
- Let the [Watcher](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watcher) herd pass by without being spotted.
- See all of the hallucinations in the first passenger section of the plane.
- Listen to Pavel after saving him.

**Lose:**

- Shoot at the herd of watchmen.

## Trivia

- The name of the level refers to the plane flashback being an "echo from the past."
- The scene where Artyom and Pavel are surrounded by watchers at the station entrance mirrors a similar moment in the [novel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)).
- Flight 76715 was inbound from Majorca to a Moscow airport when the bombs exploded; the aircraft is not a real-world model but evokes Soviet-era airliners like the Tu-204 and Il-96.
