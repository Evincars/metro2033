# The Garden

**The Garden** is the twenty-ninth level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It is based on the real-world Alexander Garden in [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow).

> "We have to make it to Polis in time for the peace conference. There isn't going to be any peace... Just war. The last war! Now I know the answers to all the questions. Pavel's head proved to be a real treasury of Korbut's sinister plans."
> — Level description

## Overview

Making his way toward [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis) through the overgrown ruins of Alexander Garden, Artyom crosses flooded stores, shallow rivers, and root-choked passages teeming with mutants — [Shrimp](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shrimp), [Watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watcher), and a [bear](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bear).

The centerpiece is a confrontation with a giant mutant bear. After the fight, a pack of watchmen attacks the wounded animal, and Artyom can choose to kill them quickly to save her — earning a moral point and unlocking the [Forest Guardian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_Guardian) achievement — or let them finish her off. The level ends with the [Baby Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One) showing Khan and Artyom a vision of the hibernating Dark Ones inside [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6).

## Notes

- Near the start, a hole in the right-hand wall leads into a flooded store containing can alarms and two safes; one is locked, and its key lies further along the row of stores past a Demon feeding on a Stalker's corpse.
- Refraining from killing the watchmen along the shallow river path, and slipping past them into a root passage, earns a moral point and a glimpse of the mother bear and her cubs.
