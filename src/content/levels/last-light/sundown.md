# Sundown

**Sundown** is the seventeenth [level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Levels) in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place in the swamps near the Cathedral of Christ the Savior in [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow).

> If I'm lucky, our people will be waiting for me at the outpost in the abandoned church. I will tell them everything I know — about Pavel, about a test of some kind they're going to conduct — and will continue on my way to Oktyabrskaya. The Dark One is there.
>
> — Level description

## Overview

Sundown is an open, exploratory surface level. [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) must make his way across a flooded, mutant-infested swamp toward an outpost, picking his path between shores while managing his gas mask filters and avoiding deep water. The environment gradually darkens as time passes, and red flags mark the intended route for players who lose their way.

## Walkthrough

The swamp is dotted with landmarks — the player's spawn, ruined buildings, a storage yard, a gas station, a crashed airliner, a ferry, and lairs where [demons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Demon) and [shrimps](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shrimp) roam. Artyom picks his way between these shores, scavenging weapons and ammunition while avoiding the deep flooded stretches where mutants lurk, following the red flags toward the outpost and eventually the manhole that leads back underground with Simon.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- Explore all nine major shores of the swamp — the spawn, the ruined buildings at the bottom left, the demon at the bottom right, the storage yard, the ruined buildings to the left, the gas station, the fallen airplane, the demon at the top left, and the ferry. The point may be awarded seemingly at random while exploring, such as when entering the derelict truck at the gas station, the storage yard, or the ferry shore.

## Trivia

- Auto-save triggers every two minutes, plus the usual event and positional saves; the timer pauses while Artyom is under attack or drowning and resumes once he is safe.
- The sky darkens over time, taking around 20 minutes to reach full night.
- Several weapons can be found across the map, including a [Kalash 2012](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_2012) behind the gas station (behind a tripwire), a scoped [Valve](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Valve) in a watchtower, a heavily modified [Lolife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lolife) in a nearby garage (also trip-wired), and a [Shambler](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shambler) with long barrel and reflex sight in a destroyed building guarded by a sleeping male shrimp.
- A box of shotgun shells can be reached by climbing atop the crashed plane and walking to the end of the right wing.
- [Liana](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Liana) lurk inside the gas station and other indoor areas.
- Crossing the flooded parts of the map risks drowning or being eaten; a splash and bubbling sound signals deep water, useful for conserving filter time.
- When a [demon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Demon) and a male [shrimp](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shrimp) share an area, the demon's AI will kill the shrimp in a single hit if they collide, even when passive.
- If Artyom already wears a gas mask when opening the manhole with Simon, he puts a fresh one over it.
- The crashed plane is an Antonov An-24, a common regional airliner used in Russia.
