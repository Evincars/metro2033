# D6

**D6** is the thirty-first and final level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light), aside from the [endings](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Endings). It culminates in the [Battle of D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_D6), a last climactic firefight to protect [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)) from the [Red Line's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) special forces.

> "He is gone. He did what he could... And now he has gone to meet his kin. I can't judge him for that. The remainders of humanity are finishing each other off in their final fight - it is not his war. I hope he was able to forgive us... Me. For what we did to his brothers and sisters. His mother and father."
> — Level description

## Overview

After gearing up, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom), [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)), [Uhlman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ulman), [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller), and the [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) ride the Metro to meet the Red Line threat. Miller delivers a rousing speech, and the Spartans dig in for their last stand.

## Walkthrough

Before the battle, the player can visit the weapon vendor to customize a loadout for free — the vendor becomes unavailable once fighting starts. Maximizing personal firepower is recommended, with plentiful ammunition available throughout the level.

- The Reds open the assault by ramming a train full of explosive barrels through the gate, then swarm the breach. Artyom is wounded and dragged to cover by Uhlman, but can keep firing until the Reds fall back.
- A huge [armored railcar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line_Tank) then breaks through the far platform. Artyom must shoot the glowing red connecting rod to immobilize it, then the two red wheels to tip it over and expose the turret's ammunition, destroying it. A fully-customized [Preved](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Preved) sniper rifle lies nearby.
- At the final line of cover, a [Gatling](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gatling) — the only one in the main game — helps repel a wave that includes riot-shielded troops protecting a [flamethrower trooper](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Trooper).

The Rangers briefly believe they have won before more Reds arrive. Miller orders D6's self-destruct prepared. Another armored train smashes through, knocking Artyom out. He wakes surrounded by Reds, with [Korbut](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Korbut) gloating over a gravely wounded Miller, who orders Artyom to destroy the facility. Artyom crawls to the self-destruct panel — and what happens next depends on his accumulated [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points), determining which of the two [endings](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Endings) plays out.

## Trivia

- This is one of the few levels where the player can freely kill human enemies without negative moral points — though by this point the ending has already been decided, back when Artyom left Polis.
- Although Artyom arrives with a trainload of Rangers, only he, Khan, and Miller disembark; Miller notes the Reds are attacking from three sides and that only about 100 Rangers exist in total.
