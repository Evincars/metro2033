# Nightfall

**Nightfall** is the eighteenth [level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Levels) in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place on the [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow) surface, near [Oktyabrskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Oktyabrskaya).

> I made quite a lot of noise pushing through the marshes. If there really are our people in the Church, I will soon meet them.
>
> — Level description

## Overview

Continuing the surface trek begun in Sundown, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) crosses a ruined, flooded cityscape at night, making his way toward the church outpost where he hopes to find fellow Rangers. The level introduces the [night vision goggles](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Night_vision_goggles) and pits Artyom against scouting [watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watcher), man-made traps, and a lurking [bog shrimp](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shrimps#Bog_Shrimp).

## Walkthrough

From the raft landing, Artyom explores broken buildings and a destroyed shopping mall, gathering equipment and dodging watchmen. Approaching the mall triggers an ambush by a bog shrimp that rises from the water; escaping into the mall causes it to break off pursuit. The level culminates in a confrontation with the bog shrimp in front of the church before a Ranger helps Artyom across the water.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- Explore four areas of the map: the red bus to the right of the starting point; the room in the house ruins where a walkie-talkie plays [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_Miller)'s message (you must enter the room, not just the building); the area with the small bonfire in the far north; and the top of the escalators on the second floor of the destroyed mall.

## Trivia

- Auto-save triggers every two minutes plus the usual event and positional saves; the timer pauses while Artyom is under attack or drowning and resumes once he is safe.
- On the path to the mall, a bog shrimp emerges from the water — approaching makes it charge and dive back, while waiting makes it retreat on its own; you can simply run into the mall to lose it and save ammunition.
- After the bog shrimp ambush, the continuous spawning of watchmen in the previous area stops, letting you explore freely.
- An Easter egg names the shopping centre "ROISSYA VPERDE," a Runet meme corrupting the pro-Russia slogan "Russia forward" into a mocking phrase.
- A deactivated fusebox with a tripwire sits in the leftmost room of the broken building; activating it switches on searchlights that draw watchmen. The same room holds an [RPK](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/RPK_(RPK-74)) with night vision sight beside Anna's tape.
- This is the level where Artyom first finds night vision goggles, located in the house ruins, which are filled with creepy whispers.
- Be careful while fleeing the bog shrimp, as several points allow Artyom to fall into the water and die.

## Bugs

- If you reach the marshes (triggering the bog shrimp ambush) without alerting the watchmen in front of the mall, they remain passive and will not attack even after the shrimp flees.
- In the second-floor mall room with two [lurkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lurker) on the ceiling, you can glitch through the plank-covered left wall from outside and find them frozen if the climbing event has not triggered.
- A rare glitch in front of the church can drop Artyom through the ground with no landing.
- Rarely, the bog shrimp fails to spawn for the boss cutscene, leaving the fight uncompletable; the game autosaves just before, so reloading fixes it.
- In Redux, a Ranger may fail to follow his script after the fight, preventing him from helping Artyom cross the water and forcing a chapter restart.
