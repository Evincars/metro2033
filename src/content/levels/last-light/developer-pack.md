# Developer Pack

The **Developer Pack** is a level in the [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light) DLC of [the same name](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Developer_Pack_(DLC)) that includes the [AI Arena](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/AI_Arena), the [Metro Museum](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Museum) and the [Shooting Gallery](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shooting_Gallery).

## Overview

The level starts with the unknown player ascending an elevator. On the way, through a window, you will see a strange old woman staring at you. When you arrive at your destination, you will discover she has somehow arrived before you. If you turn your back on her and walk away, an ominous sound will play (this sound is heard multiple times throughout the main campaign) and she will appear in a different location doing something strange — such as sitting on an invisible chair, weeping on the ground, holding her broom as a gun, sweeping, or dancing seductively. There are two doors here: the left leading to the Shooting Gallery and the AI Arena, and the right to the Museum.

## Trivia

- In the entrance with the old woman, there are numerous rats that can be shot, similar to the [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Location)) shooting range.
- It is a mystery who the old woman is meant to be, if anyone. Likely, it is just to make the Developer Pack seem a bit creepier. She can be shot with [Helsing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Helsing) [arrows](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arrows) and [throwing knives](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives) that will stick in her body, and when she respawns elsewhere, they remain lodged in her.
- If you are killed by enemies in the AI Arena or Shooting Gallery, you will simply respawn with nothing lost. However, if you kill yourself (e.g. grenades, fire), you will die and the level retry screen will appear.
- Undead soldiers appear in the whole last two levels of challenge modes. Undead in the Shooting Gallery have weak HP, but in the AI Arena challenge mode their HP is quite large, so it is recommended to avoid them.
- Throughout the level, you can hear the sound of the Rhino roaring. The Rhino can be seen in the background in the model viewer in the top right.

## Achievements

- **Developer** — Spend time in the Developer Pack.
- **Specialist** — Complete challenges within the pack.
