# The Crossing

**The Crossing** is the twenty-fourth level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place around Vorobyovy Gory Station and the Luzhniki Metro Bridge in [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow).

> "Of course I could not kill him. And while I am alive and my heart is beating, I will protect him. Khan says we'll be able to use him to stop the war... Yes, he can make miracles happen... But I don't want to use him. His war was over when I targeted the missiles at his home, his family."
> — Level description

## Overview

The level is based on the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro)'s [Vorobyovy Gory](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vorobyovy_Gory) station, which sits on the Luzhniki Metro Bridge. Carrying the [Baby Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One), Artyom crosses the frozen river beneath the bridge, passing wrecked vehicles, a half-submerged ship, and packs of [watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchmen) before reaching a train.

Along the way the Baby Dark One appears several times — asking to wear human clothing and drawing Artyom's attention to the dead — creating quiet moments amid the danger.

## Trivia

- The largest demon in the game is found in this level.
- If Artyom changes his filter while carrying the Dark One child after the train crash, a second left arm appears out of thin air to perform the swap.
- After entering a building filled with watchmen growls, approaching the farthest corpse (which carries a [claymore](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Claymore_Mine)) and turning around causes three watchmen to spawn; walking backward toward the corpses avoids the spawn while still grabbing the item.
