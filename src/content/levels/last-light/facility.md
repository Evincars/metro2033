# Facility

> "Pavel's almost certainly going to hang. Who knows what would have happened to me, had he not released me? Red, blue, yellow — what difference does it make? He risked his life for me. You don't get that much in the Metro. I can't just walk away from him..."
> — Level description

**Facility** is the seventh level of [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

## Overview

In this level, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) must sneak or fight his way through a [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) farming facility in order to save [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov) from being hanged.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- After exiting the air vent, watch the scene where a Nazi bullies a fellow soldier; wait until the brutalized soldier leaves the room to earn the point.
- Do not kill the brutalized soldier hiding in the meat locker.
- Listen to two Nazis discussing a stashed custom weapon, then follow the one heading to the locker room; knocking him out and taking the weapon also earns the **A Present** achievement.
- Listen to the conversation between two Nazis near the electric circuit that controls the lights.
- Enter the air vent in the room beyond the bathtubs.
- In the room with water turbines, listen to the full conversation between two Nazis discussing Pavel's hanging.
- Reach the sewer without raising an alarm (doing so without killing anyone unlocks the **Invisible Savior** achievement).

**Lose:**

- Kill the Nazi hiding in the meat locker.

## Trivia

- Two Nazis struggling with the electrical circuit complain about how much easier it would be with the mutant electrician — the man beaten to death with a shovel in [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)).
- The modified [Lolife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lolife) hidden in a Nazi locker is said to have been taken from a slain Polis Ranger; the owner claims he "got it off one of those pesky Polis Rangers we whacked last week." The Rangers Rost and Scalp were killed around that time.
