# Venice

**Venice** is the sixteenth [level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Levels) in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

> The subterranean Venice... An island on the dark waters of underground rivers. You always hear rough stories circulating about this place. I hear it is all one huge den of thieves. But there is something I know for sure: Pavel reached Venice and if I'm not late he's still here. Finding the Dark One is still my main mission, but now I see I must also uncover Korbut's plans so that I can warn the order about them...
>
> — Level description

## Overview

[Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom), having just been saved by a [local fisherman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fedor), arrives at [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Location)) and sets out to find [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov). The events surrounding Pavel's own arrival are explored in the chapter "[Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Chronicles_Pack_DLC_Level))" from the Chronicles Pack.

## Walkthrough

Venice is a sprawling market town built on the water, full of merchants, beggars, bars and a brothel. Artyom explores the docks and market alleys, spending [Military Grade Rounds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR) at shops and diversions, before tracking Pavel into a storage area guarded by gangsters and pressing on toward the surface.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- Give a bullet to a beggar near the market alley where two men are trying to push a pig in a crate onto a boat (giving a second bullet does not grant a second point).
- Give a bullet to a nearby second beggar, who will play his guitar for you (unavailable in Redux).
- Give a bullet to a third beggar in the alley where two children are drawing on the wall.
- Buy and drink the bartender's special, "The Bullet."
- Drinking three times triggers a blackout; when Artyom wakes the bar is wrecked. Paying the bartender 100 MGR for the damage earns a point (and the drinking also unlocks the **[Cheers!](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Cheers!)** achievement in Redux).
- Pay 10 MGR to use the shooting range and shoot five rats within the time limit.
- Complete all the shooting gallery challenges to win a teddy bear, then return it to the crying child being comforted by his mother.
- Complete the level without killing any bandits in the storage area.

**Lose:**

- Taking the 20 MGR aboard the boat of a sleeping fisherman at the left corner of the dock (the point is lost even if you grab the rounds without stepping onto the boat).
- Blacking out and wrecking the bar costs a point unless you pay the bartender 100 MGR afterward, which results in a net gain.

## Trivia

- In the first dock area, a girl and her father are fishing; further ahead another man fishing from a boat is eventually pulled into the water if you watch long enough.
- When drinking at the bar, a woman to the right appears more beautiful with each drink, shifting from an older woman to a red-haired girl; after the blackout she is seen on the floor beside Artyom, reverted to her original look.
- A hookah pipe in the stripper's room can be smoked, blurring the screen with psychedelic colours; smoking three times makes Artyom pass out and wake by the river, after which the pipe is gone.
- In the storage area, three gangsters gathered around an oil-drum fire tell the same story heard at the bar in Polis, implying they are the same characters.
- The Chronicles Pack "Pavel" level reveals that after escaping Artyom and [Simon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Simon_(Venice)), Pavel is captured by the Venice bandits, later escaping to join a Red caravan heading to Revolution Square.
