# Regina

**Regina** is the thirteenth [level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Levels) in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

> I was able to send a report back to the Order via my old acquaintance: Andrew the Blacksmith. At least Miller will know I'm alive, that I haven't completed my mission but I haven't resigned from it either.
>
> — Level description

## Overview

In this level, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) ventures through a dangerous tunnel on his way to [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice), riding [Andrew](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Andrew)'s masterpiece — the armoured railcar [Regina](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Regina_(Vehicle)).

## Walkthrough

Artyom drives Regina out of Andrew's home and through a long, eerie stretch of tunnel, using the railcar to smash wooden barricades and push abandoned metro cars ahead of him. Along the way he can dismount to explore side passages — spider nests, partly submerged rooms, a snowy dead-end tunnel patrolled by a [watcher](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watcher), a haunted makeshift hospital filled with whispering ghosts, and a sewer crawling with watchmen — before pressing on toward Venice.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- After boarding Regina and leaving Andrew's home, enter the first doorway on the left and step into the area with the lamp on the desk.
- Further along on the left, enter the door beneath a light with a mask and filter beside it, pass through the spider nest, and reach the glowing red room with a light switch at the end.
- Continue on and take another left-hand doorway into a partly submerged area; enter the room at the far right containing ammo, [filters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Filters), and a skeleton.
- After opening the airlock into a [gas mask](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gas_mask) area, dismount, throw the switch on the left to change the tracks, then use Regina to smash the wooden barricade.
- Open the crate on the floor near a skeleton on the left, behind the wooden barricade.
- Before the snowy tunnel, take the left-hand passage to an abandoned train where a [watcher](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watcher) ambushes you, and follow it to the dead end with a skeleton.
- Just before the metro cars you must push, take the right-hand passage where a gas mask hangs on the wall with a filter.
- While pushing the metro cars, enter the left-hand passage into a former makeshift hospital full of shadows and whispering ghosts, reaching its end (this area also holds a [Valve](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Valve)).
- Still pushing the cars, enter the sewer area on the right guarded by watchmen and reach the end near the skeleton wearing a gas mask.

## Notes

- In the frozen railway tunnel, a room on the right holds a safe key on a body near the (inaccessible) security entrance; the matching safe is in a nearby office.
- On Ranger Hardcore, with the HUD disabled, the prompt to start the vehicle is hidden — the correct input is the jump key (default: space).
