# Pavel

> "My luck's been exceptionally rotten lately... I managed to get captured by the Nazis, fail to recruit a Ranger into our ranks and then almost get killed by the same ranger — all in a row... I did carry out my mission in Venice, but got caught again... Am I growing old, or what? I wonder, where am I now and how long is it going to take to get to the Red Line..."
> — Level Description

**Pavel** is the first level in the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack) DLC for [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). The player plays as [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov) who, after being captured and subsequently escaping, clears a [Bandit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits_(Faction))-controlled section of [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Location)).

## Overview

After escaping from [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Simon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Simon_(Venice)) in Venice, Pavel is recaptured by bandits who reside there. When the level starts, one of them has Pavel tied to a post, torturing him. He tells Pavel they are in a power plant that supplies Venice with energy, created by human labor, and the bandit leader has demanded Pavel join the slaves. The bandit prepares to give Pavel an injection that will render him unable to do anything but push the generator, but Pavel slips his hands over the post and attacks the bandit, jamming the syringe in his neck instead before throwing him down a drainpipe.

Free, Pavel retrieves a [flashlight](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Flashlight) and a [knife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Knife), and makes it to the bandits' main camp. Travelling through their lair, while clearing the place of bandits and emptying their safes, Pavel makes it to another part of Venice where the bandits had not reached. Here, he meets a contact in the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line), [Yanek](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Yanek), posing as a homeless man, who tells Pavel that a cart is waiting to take him to the Red Line. The level ends here, though Pavel made it back to [Korbut](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Korbut_(Level)), who immediately dispatched him to Red Square, as in the main campaign.

## Trivia

- The ammunition in this level is extremely scarce, so a stealth approach is recommended.
- If the player looks down after escaping, several men turning cranks are seen below, turning the aforementioned generators. Thankfully, Pavel avoided this fate.
- If you add a bullet into the empty [Revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver), the bandit will unintentionally shoot the other bandit, believing that he himself killed him.
- The bandit who is drunk in the shower room will offer Pavel a drink from his bottle. If you shoot the bottle, it will kill him.
- Two bandits in the non-hostile section of Venice talk about [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) from the first game, and how he is not actually dead. His death in the [novel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)) series is still canonical, however.
- The band in the hall mistakes you for their drummer, Valera. It appears Valera is the dead body seen at the bottom of the stairs before Pavel opens the door.
- The fortune teller reveals Pavel's future, alluding that he has "two paths" and will either be saved or damned by Artyom.
- Many of the bandits are wearing Reich helmets and armor. In the Redux version, the gangsters instead appear in typical bandit attire with pieces of Reich armor worn over their clothes.

## Bugs

- Pavel can use his flashlight during the intro sequence, despite being chained up and without one.
- There are a great many spelling and punctuation errors present in the Chronicles Pack, most of them found in Pavel.
- There are a number of odd clipping and collision areas throughout the level; some crates are bugged and the player can clip through them.
- In the room before the nightclub, sometimes the enemies will not react to the player, and attempting to knock one out can lock up the game graphics, requiring a level restart.
