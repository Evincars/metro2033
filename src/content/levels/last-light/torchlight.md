# Torchlight

> "I dragged Pavel out of the noose, and he says he's going to take me as close to Polis as possible. Then I'll contact the Order and report on everything that has happened. The path to the legendary Theater lies through the catacombs. I've never been here before, and without my new friend I'd probably be completely lost. I hope he knows the way."
> — Level description

**Torchlight** is the eighth level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

## Overview

The level begins just outside the airlock where [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov)'s execution was scheduled to take place. As [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and Pavel move through the tunnel, they overhear a [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) officer ordering the lights cut through the announcement system. Forced into the infested tunnels, the pair must fight their way through hordes of mutated [Spiderbugs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spiderbug), whose weakness to bright light becomes the central mechanic of the level — the player uses Artyom's flashlight and torches to drive them back.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- Burn away the cobwebs hiding a small nook of ammo and a medkit just before the elevator at the start of the spider tunnels.
- Enter the elevator with Pavel.
- Fend off the spiders with the flashlight while in the elevator.
- Quickly save Pavel from a spider attack after exiting the elevator.
- Stay close to Pavel while he carries a torch, until he drops it.

**Lose:**

- Leave the flashlight off during the elevator ride.
- Leave the flashlight off and fail to help Pavel when he is pinned by a spider after the elevator.

## Trivia

- The title of the level refers to the spiders' sensitivity to bright lights.
