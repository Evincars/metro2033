# Anna

> "And here we are... the Exhibition. Close to your home station... You know, I barely escaped those missiles. I was searching for Hunter until the very last moment. My father didn't know, of course."
> — Level Description

**Anna** is the third level in the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack) DLC for [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). In this level, [Anna Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_Miller) assists [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) as he makes his way through the [botanical gardens](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Botanicheskiy_Sad). This mission is the main campaign mission, [Ashes](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashes), from Anna's perspective. She provides overwatch while Artyom hunts for the Dark One, and searches for Artyom after the latter is captured by [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) [stalkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers).

## Overview

Starting part way into Ashes, after Anna and Artyom have left the Metro, the two split up, Artyom giving a friendly salute to Anna before heading off into the gardens. Anna climbs to the top of the Exhibition front gate to obtain a decent sniping spot. Noting that there is still a fire burning in the gardens, Artyom moves closer to the crater the missile left. However, Anna sees movement to his left — a group of [watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchmen) attack him. Together, they defeat the watchmen, and Anna compliments Artyom's fighting skills before he moves on.

Moving to a better position, the two return to searching for the Dark One. Anna spots it and fires, though it seemingly avoids the bullet. Cursing, she sends Artyom after him; Artyom chases the Dark One through the Gardens, Anna repeatedly trying and failing to shoot it, before it is cornered. She finally manages to hit it, and Artyom approaches — however, the creature jumps up and knocks Artyom out. The two disappear from view.

Alarmed, Anna rushes down a zip-line to reach him, but is confronted by a group of Reich stalkers that block her path. Anna fights past them and moves deeper into the gardens, reaching the area where she last saw Artyom. However, he and the Dark One have gone. She presses on, hearing an engine nearby. Readying her [Preved](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Preved), she reaches the top of a ridge, ready to save Artyom — but she is too late, and sees an [armoured truck](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armoured_truck) speeding away with Artyom in tow. Failing her mission to kill the Dark One and losing her partner, Anna returns to [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)) in shame.

## Trivia

- This level is the shortest in the Chronicles Pack, and one of the shortest in the whole game.
- Although it's possible to sneak past the Stalkers, the snipers on top of the bridge are good spotters and will likely see Anna, forcing her into combat.
- This level is unique in that it's the only section in either game where the player character is a woman. Accordingly, the sounds for swapping gas masks, running out of air, and death all sound different. However, when knocking down an enemy, Artyom's voice is used instead.
- This is the only section in either game where Artyom is an NPC (except the endings and cutscenes). To retain the silent protagonist feel, Artyom is dressed in generic Stalker gear, never says a word, and is only seen from afar.
- The Reich stalkers encountered are searching for the location of D6, though at this stage it's still just a rumor to the rest of the Metro.
- If Anna is out of [12.7x108mm](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/12.7x108mm) ammo, the Dark One will be knocked down by himself at the end of the chase without Anna shooting him.

## Bugs

- After the Dark One is knocked down, Anna can breathe without her gas mask until the end of the chapter. The filter timer countdown also stops or is not working when wearing the gas mask.
