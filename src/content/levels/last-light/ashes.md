# Ashes

**Ashes** is the third level of [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). In this mission, [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) travel to the [Botanical Garden](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Botanical_Garden) in search of the [last](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dark_One) of the [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones). The level is later revisited from Anna's perspective during the **Anna** level in the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack) DLC.

> "Just a year ago I stood on top of the TV tower and saw missiles rain on the Botanical Gardens, turning flesh into ashes, smelting metal and glass. Nobody and nothing could survive that inferno. Yet Khan did find a Dark One there. Now my mission is to find and eliminate it. To finish what I started."
> — Level description

## Overview

The level begins with Anna and Artyom leaving the [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)) tunnels aboard the [Automatic Train](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Automatic_Train), arriving at a service station. They climb a narrow staircase littered with skeletons and emerge into a sewer lined with graves. As they proceed, Anna is startled by a [lurker](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lurker); a quick kill earns Artyom her praise. The pair reach a ladder leading to the surface.

Emerging on a cliff overlooking the Botanical Garden, the two split up — Artyom continues on the ground while Anna climbs the Exhibition front gate to set up a sniping position. Approaching the crater, Anna remarks that Artyom did a thorough job incinerating the place, then spots movement she cannot identify. Artyom moves closer and is set upon by [watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchmen). With Anna's covering fire, Artyom survives several waves of the pack.

Moving deeper into the gardens, Anna sights the [Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One) and fires, but misses, leaving Artyom to give chase. The pursuit ends with the Dark One trapped and wounded by one of Anna's shots. Artyom grabs the child, and the contact forges a psychic link between them, knocking both Artyom and the Dark One unconscious.

In the closing cutscene, [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) stalkers discover the unconscious Artyom. As he stirs, they knock him out again and take him prisoner.

## Trivia

- The underground stairwell is covered in Russian inscriptions left by dying survivors, recording names and desperate final messages.
- Real Moscow landmarks appear on the level, including the [Monument to the Conquerors of Space](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_to_the_Conquerors_of_Space), the All-Russia Exhibition Centre's main entrance and Central Pavilion, Hotel Cosmos, and the [Ostankino Tower](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ostankino_Tower).
- A crashed Mi-24 "Hind" helicopter can be seen to the left upon reaching the surface.
