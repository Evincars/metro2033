# Khan

**Khan** is the twenty-second level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

> In some other time and place, what happened could be life changing. But there is no time but present and no place but the Metro for us. And we must do everything we can to save it. I have to hurry and catch up with the Dark One, Anna has to tell her father of what Korbut and Moskvin are doing. Anything else is a luxury we can't afford right now.
>
> — Level description

## Overview

Artyom is led by the enigmatic [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)) through a stretch of haunted tunnels and sewers toward the [River of Fate](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate). Khan speaks of the Metro as a living, breathing thing, and the journey is marked by supernatural encounters — restless skeletons, ringing phones, and the spreading Darkness that forces the pair to navigate by lighter.

## Walkthrough

Following Khan past three giant ventilation fans and through drainage pipes and cobwebbed chambers, Artyom witnesses a series of eerie phenomena — skeletons that stir to life, a phone ringing in the sewers, and a dream-like sequence — before finally reaching the River of Fate that will carry the story onward.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- Answer the phone in the sewers. (Ignoring the call instead awards the **[Nobody's Home](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nobody%27s_Home)** achievement, which is absent from the [Redux version](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).)
- Reach the [River of Fate](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate).

## Trivia

- After the area where the phone rings, Artyom continues through a drainage pipe with Khan, who says to go left. Heading straight instead reveals two skeletons that come to life and stare at you for a while.
- Directly after waking from the dream sequence, Khan says "Back to the desert of the real," a direct reference to The Matrix.
- Entering the door in the cobweb room activates a skeleton resting its head on its hand, with a unique animation of it trying to get comfortable after being disturbed.
- Khan is notable in this level for using his lighter after the Darkness occupies the tunnel.

## Notes

- In Redux, after emerging from the river and bypassing a set of tin-can alarms, a safe key can be found under a corpse's hand; the matching safe is next to the dead end of the nearby tracks.

## Achievements

- **[Nobody's Home](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nobody%27s_Home)** — Bronze Trophy — Ignore the phone call in the Anomaly (not available in Redux).
