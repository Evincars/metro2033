# Contagion

**Contagion** is the twentieth level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light), where [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) must navigate the burning ruins of [Oktyabrskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Oktyabrskaya) in order to save [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_Miller).

> All the roads lead to Oktyabrskaya. The Dark One is there. Anna is there now, too. In the hands of my enemies. The hands of the traitor, Lesnitsky.
>
> — Level description

## Overview

In this level, Artyom must make his way through Oktyabrskaya Station as the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) burns it to the ground. The station has been struck by a mysterious "[disease](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Virus)," and Red soldiers are executing infected citizens and torching the living quarters. Artyom picks his way through fire, gas-masked passages, and grieving survivors toward the traitor [Lesnitsky](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lesnitsky), who is holding Anna hostage.

## Walkthrough

Artyom moves through the station as Red Line soldiers set everything ablaze, climbing to a second floor to drop behind a locked gate, passing docked railcars, irradiated living quarters, and a market area. Along the way he overhears the Reds discussing the plague and their brutal quarantine, witnesses the execution of an infected soldier, and finally confronts Lesnitsky, who holds a knife to Anna's throat.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- Complete the level without killing anyone (the point is awarded on entering the red door leading to Lesnitsky).
- Deal with the two Red Line soldiers executing citizens at the beginning, before the shot man dies.
- Listen to the shot man's explanation and confusion over the Red's attack.
- Alternatively, let the Red Line soldiers execute the civilians but listen to their discussion afterward (nets 1 instead of 2).
- In the room to the left of the railcar where an enemy loads boxes, slip through the partially blocked hole in the back wall into the abandoned tunnel and open the ammo cache at the waterline across from the hole.
- Listen to and watch the full execution of the [wounded Red soldier](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rzhavin) who contracted the disease.
- Collect at least 14 [MGRs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Grade_Rounds) from the station's six stashes (18 total available): 3 in the docked pink railcar, 2 in the lamp-lit room on the second floor of the living quarters, 3 in the underground passage with glowing radioactive substances below the living quarters, 2 near a guitar in the torch-lit market stall, 4 on the second floor above the market, and 4 in the room with the sewing machine on the second floor of the train compartments.
- After hearing Lesnitsky speak with another soldier and passing through the shaft, listen to the Red Line officer going over dead citizens at the top of the stairs in the room where plants grow.
- Remove your mask when Lesnitsky holds his knife at Anna's throat, before he counts to five (you must be wearing a gas mask before opening the door, or no point is given).

**Lose:**

- Attacking Lesnitsky while he holds his knife at Anna's throat.

## Notes

- A locked safe sits upstairs near the end of the level where the last two Reds are behind a locked gate; its key is on a shelf in a small room adjacent to the iron gate on the lower level. The safe holds a filter and some regular ammunition.

## Trivia

- In the train compartments area, a Red soldier struggles to open a wooden door; if you knock him out or kill him first, Artyom can open it effortlessly.
- Entering the final area without a gas mask changes Lesnitsky's dialogue, though the outcome is the same — he throws Anna to you and flees — but no moral point is given.

## Bugs

- This is one of the levels with the unlimited gas mask filter glitch: entering a gas-mask area (marked by a blue door with a gas lamp) without a mask and pressing the filter-change key stops the suffocation without consuming filters.
- Reloading a checkpoint after the struggling soldier opens the wooden door can leave it appearing closed while still allowing you to glitch through it.

## Achievements

- **[Savior](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Savior)** — Silver Trophy — Remove your mask when Lesnitsky demands it.
