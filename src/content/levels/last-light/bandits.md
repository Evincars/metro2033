# Bandits

**Bandits** is the fourteenth [level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Levels) in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place in the tunnels between Revolution Square and [Tretyakovskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Location)).

> Pavel's group is advancing towards Tretyakovskaya, also known as Venice. They have a few hours' lead on me, and are moving via frequently used tunnels... But I will catch up. I can't afford not to.
>
> — Level description

## Overview

While travelling the tunnels using [Regina](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Regina_(Vehicle)), [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) stumbles upon a group of [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) [refugees](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Refugees) and subsequently agrees to help them with their problem — the [bandits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits) preying on the tunnels.

## Walkthrough

After the railcar crashes, Artyom continues on foot through irradiated stretches of tunnel, passing a refugee caravan and abandoned metro cars where bandits are attacking helpless travellers. He works his way through torch-lit passages toward the main bandit camp, where women and children are being held prisoner. Freeing them triggers a final stand: Artyom must hold out against a Nosalis attack until the ferry arrives to carry him onward toward Venice.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- When Artyom first awakes, turn around and enter where Regina crashed to discover a skeleton and a [Shambler](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Uboinik_(Shambler)) with an extended barrel.
- After meeting the refugee caravan, wait and listen to all their conversations until a woman describes how her husband was drafted and then captured by Nazis.
- In the abandoned metro cars off the first right-hand tunnel, kill or knock out the two bandits before they murder the woman crying for help.
- Just before the main bandit camp, continue down the left-hand tunnel to a carriage full of corpses.
- Rescue the women and children held in the bandit camp; the point is earned when the bandit threatening them in the final room is killed.

## Trivia

- After freeing the captive women and children, they run to the red door behind Artyom.
- In the main bandit camp, a large room with three bandits contains a bottle of vodka; drinking it makes the screen "drunk" and slows time slightly.
- In the railcar area, only one refugee can be saved no matter how quickly the bandits are dealt with; the other runs into the radioactive water and drowns if not executed first.
- A great deal of [MGR](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR) can be earned by collecting enemy weapons and selling them at the shop at the start of the level.
- Off the right side of the abandoned caravan, a boarded-up room blocked by a train carriage hides a corpse carrying MGRs — but a nosalis ambushes anyone who searches the body.

## Notes

- In the first part of the bandit camp, a safe key sits on a seat toward the back of the first passenger car; the matching safe is on the opposite side at the end of the passage.
- During the final Nosalis stand, it is more effective to run laps and conserve ammunition, since the ferry arrives after a set time has passed rather than after a certain number of kills.
- The radioactive puddle on the tracks after the fork lowers Artyom's health but cannot actually kill him.
