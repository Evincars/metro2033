# Revolution

**Revolution** is the twelfth level of [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).

> Now I am in REAL spider-infested catacombs... where the General secretary Moskvin does not look like the head spider, that title clearly belongs to General Korbut... I sure would like to know what's on his mind. What did Lesnitsky bring him from D6? What's going to destroy the enemies of the Revolution? Only questions, and no answers. The only thing that is clear: Pavel has information on the Dark One, which makes him my current target.
>
> — Level description

## Overview

In this level, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) must sneak or fight his way out of a [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line)-held [station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ploshchad_Revolutsii). Held prisoner deep inside enemy territory, he begins in a storage room and works his way down through the station's lower levels, past patrolling guards, work crews, and machine-gun emplacements, toward the tunnels beyond.

The level is built heavily around stealth. Artyom can slip past the Reds unnoticed, listening in on their conversations, or he can take them down silently. Completing the chapter without killing anyone is both possible and rewarded.

## Walkthrough

Artyom moves down through metal detectors and stairwells into the station's storage and service areas. Along the way he passes guards reporting in over the intercom, soldiers gossiping about the earlier "[Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level))" battle, crane operators moving radioactive materials, and maintenance men running service checks. A large ventilation fan must be shut down from a nearby control room before Artyom can climb through it and continue toward a machine-gun platform and the [armored train](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line_Armored_Train) yard.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- Go downstairs from the starting storage room to listen to the guard reporting in on the intercom.
- Listen to the two soldiers in the storage area talking about the [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level)) mission from [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033).
- Listen to the crane operators discuss moving radioactive materials.
- Do not disturb the men doing the service check; keep following them until they finish and talk about a smoke break.
- After the service check room and the big fan, listen to the conversation between two men right after climbing down the ladder.
- At the machine gun platform, listen to the conversation between the machine gunner and a soldier.
- Complete the level without killing any humans (points are still given even if alarms are triggered).

**Lose:**

- Kill the civilian crane operator near the [armored train](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line_Armored_Train).

## Trivia

- An in-game joke can be found in the area after Artyom goes through the giant fan. In one corner near the exit there is a box; opening it reveals a smaller box, then another, then a final small box containing a single [MGR](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR).
- Another joke is hidden in the second room (the one with the piano) after the storage room: a fake bomb sits inside a locker beside the doorway. The bomb can actually explode if hit with a shotgun blast, several handgun bullets, or assault rifle bursts. The same is true of the bomb in the initial area, just before heading downstairs.
- The two Reds who talk about the events of "[Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level))" speculate about how both sides were wiped out, giving explanations that include a single man (Artyom) and a mythical tunnel demon called the "Dark Rail Tracer." In the canon storyline Artyom killed everyone in that level, though in-game the choice is left to the player.

## Notes

- In the initial area, a safe key can be found in the downstairs locker room on a table next to the stairs. Climbing back up and edging along the ledge near the Reds' fuel-canister bomb leads to a gate hiding a Lolife with a long barrel and red dot, plus the matching safe full of MGRs.
- In the room where a Red is exercising, another safe key sits on a small dresser next to the sofa; the matching safe is in the storage room where a Red tells the "Dark Rail Tracer" story.
- A third safe key is in one of the switchboard compartments in the generator room, with its safe in the control room where the fan brakes are pushed.
- A fourth safe key rests on the crate where soldiers are gambling near the crane; once their game ends they disperse and the key can be taken. The matching safe sits atop a nearby shelf reached by a ladder near the crane.

- **[Invisible Soldier](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Invisible_Soldier)** — Silver Trophy — Complete the Revolution level without killing and without raising the alarm.
