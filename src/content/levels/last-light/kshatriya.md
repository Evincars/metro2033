# Kshatriya

**Kshatriya** is the second level added in the [Faction Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faction_Pack), and is not only the longest of the three but the single longest level in the [Metro video game series](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Videogame_Series), at least up until [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus). Unlike most levels, which have [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) travelling from point A to B or battling a foe, Kshatriya uses new mechanics such as open world scavenging to emulate the act of [stalking](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers) — venturing up to the surface to retrieve artifacts of the old world.

## Overview

Kshatriya puts the player in the boots of a supposed [Ranger Trainee](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Trainee) from [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)), attempting to prove himself worthy of the warrior class of Polis: the Kshatriya. He begins by meeting up with the [Kshatriya Team](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_Kshatriya) that helps him scavenge the area of the [Great Library](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Library) above. Following a short conversation with the base's arms dealer, [Kuzmich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kuzmich), the Trainee meets with [Shaman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shaman), the artifacts collector, who instructs the trainee to hand in any artifacts found on the surface in exchange for varying levels of [cash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR) rewards.

Following this, the trainee may leave for the sewers that connect with the surface. Along the way the trainee meets [Bar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bar) and [Su](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Su), twin Stalkers of his team who provide occasional help in combat and tips on how to survive the level. After the meeting, the player is left to their own devices to explore the slowly opening world of Kshatriya.

Gameplay in Kshatriya is drastically different than that of the main campaign of Metro: Last Light. Aside from finding all 30 artifacts listed on Shaman's electronic drawing-board, including the "Map of [Secret Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location))," there is no goal, so the player is free to explore the large map at will. Certain parts of Kshatriya must be unlocked by exploration to proceed further. The sewers, seemingly a hallway or two, rapidly become a crossroads leading to various parts of the surface and the Great Library.

Saving progress must be done manually rather than automatically. To save, the player must return to the Stalker base and use the door to the right of Kuzmich's wares to enter Polis station, where the Trainee rests with progress saved. The player's backpack only fits 5 artifacts, so a player wishing to finish quickly will return to the station at least six times to hand over findings to Shaman.

Unique to Kshatriya are semi-randomized encounters, mostly with enemies but also with artifact placement, that lend the level well to multiple replays. Passing through the sewer at any time has the chance of spawning [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) from a myriad of places that can quickly kill the trainee if unprepared. Kshatriya is completed after the trainee ventures deep into the archives of the library, beyond the fearsome [librarians](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Librarians) within, and returns with the secret metro map.

## Gameplay

- The nosalises encountered underground can be a fair source of income, as they can be killed with 1-2 shotgun blasts and yield 1-5 [MGRs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR). As they are found underground, you do not need to waste precious filters fighting them.
- Filters become more expensive every time you purchase one, but their price resets after a time. The base price is 20 MGRs, and each consecutive filter costs 5 MGRs more.
- Power has been reconnected to the sewer system and Library underground, and lightbulbs can be used to brighten these areas. Lightbulbs can be collected from a box in the airlock leading into the sewer, but only 5 lights can be restored before more are needed.

## Equipment

The trainee starts out with a [Bastard](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bastard) carbine and an [Ashot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashot) shotgun-pistol. The trader offers to sell various guns including the [Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash), [Duplet](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duplet), [Revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver) and [Lolife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lolife), along with ammunition and equipment such as [throwing knives](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives), [claymore mines](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Claymore_Mine), [sticky explosives](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Grenade) and [incendiary grenades](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Incendiary_Grenade). Two suits of armor are available for purchase, but only one can be worn at a time:

- **Radiation armor** costs 200 MGRs and protects from radiation, lowering the rate at which filters are used up in radioactive areas.
- **Heavy armor** costs 225 MGRs and protects from the attacks of monsters.

[Night vision goggles](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Vision_Goggles) can be found on the body of one of the trainee's predecessors.

## Trivia

- [Kshatriya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kshatriya), from Kshatra (rule or authority), is one of the four varnas (social orders) in Hinduism, constituting the ruling and military elite. In the Metro universe, the Kshatriya is one of the four classes Polis citizens may elect to fall into when they become of age — the warrior class and combat elite of Polis.
- Despite being on a trek to prove himself as a warrior, Kshatriya can be 100% completed without firing a single bullet.
- Only the secret subway map artifact is required to finish the mission; collecting all 30 is optional.
- The library is slightly different from the Metro 2033 version, and the weather is stormy instead of snowy.
- This mission introduces a new type of shotgun ammunition, the incendiary Dragonbreath shells, which have a red shell instead of the blue regular buckshot shell.
