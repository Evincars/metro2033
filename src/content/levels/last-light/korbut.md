# Korbut

> "That was so stupid of me. I bought that talk of friendship, the stupid musketeer saying... just you wait... I will get even. I wonder, though, why did Pavel go through the trouble of trapping me?"
> — Level description

**Korbut** is the eleventh level of [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

## Overview

In this level, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) is held captive and interrogated by the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) secretary general [Maxim Moskvin](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Maxim_Moskvin) and general [Korbut](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Korbut_(Character)). Pavel's earlier friendship is revealed to have been a ruse to trap Artyom, and the level unfolds largely as a captive sequence during the interrogation.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- Listen in on the conversation between Korbut and Moskvin.

## Trivia

- The start of the level is based on the [Ploshchad Revolutsii](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ploshchad_Revolutsii) station.
- A background speech by [Moskvin](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Maxim_Moskvin) is the only time in either game that the [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) is referred to as "The Fourth Reich," though it is hard to hear over Korbut's dialogue.
- [Artyom's mother](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom%27s_Mother) is briefly seen in a flashback, being attacked by a pack of rats.
