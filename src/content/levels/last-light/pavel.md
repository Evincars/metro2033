# Pavel

**Pavel** is the fourth level of [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

> "The creature I met in the burnt-out remains of the Gardens looks like a Dark One... and it was able to enter my mind, dragging the innermost out of me... But it was also just a baby, and I am sure that... it recognized me. And got scared. It also left me knocked out for some time... Just enough for me to get captured..."
> — Level description

## Overview

[Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) escapes a [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) concentration camp along with a [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) soldier named [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov). The pair make their way through the prison complex, and to complete the mission Artyom must free the other captives — pulling a lever to release the prisoners rewards a **moral point**.

The level is built around stealth. As Pavel and the inmates warn, the prison block will be flooded with poison gas if the guards go into full alert, killing everyone inside. It is the only non-DLC mission that strictly requires a stealthy approach; every other level can be played loud or quiet.

## Trivia

- On Ranger difficulty it is possible to recover the weapons Artyom selected back in the [Sparta](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sparta_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)) level, found near the guard post on a periodic table on the wall — though they are empty of ammunition.
- A man executed with a shovel in this level is later mentioned in [Facility](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Facility) by two Nazi workers, who lament that the dead man was an electrician who could have fixed the farm's poor lighting.
- One of the Nazis mentions receiving 200 [Military Grade Rounds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Grade_Rounds) for selling the [Baby Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One) to a Hansa trader — the first hint of the child's fate after Artyom's capture. Bandits in [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Level)) later reveal the trader came from [Oktyabrskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Oktyabrskaya).
