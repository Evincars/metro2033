# Red Square

**Red Square** is the twenty-eighth level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

> "I know only one way into Polis from here - through the Red Square. The Order keeps an outpost in the Saint Basil's Cathedral. I'll get in touch with our men and tell them all I know. And my goal would be just a step away from there. It will all be over soon."
> — Level description

## Overview

Artyom crosses Red Square toward [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis), passing through St. Basil's Cathedral, [Lenin's Mausoleum](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lenin%27s_Mausoleum), and a radioactive storm haunted by the [Damned Souls](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ghosts_(Phenomenon)). His objectives are to meet up with the other [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) at the Kremlin and to deal with the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) — and, ultimately, to decide the fate of the traitor [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov).

Guided by the [Baby Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One), Artyom must stay close through the storm or die within seconds to the radioactive rain. Choosing to save Pavel from the damned souls earns a moral point — the Baby Dark One remarking that he now understands forgiveness — while leaving him behind loses one.

## Trivia

- The room holding the second diary entry is the Tomb of Vladimir Lenin; a corpse stands in for Lenin in the glass coffin, and a silenced [VSV](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/VSV_(VSK-94)) with laser and IR sights can be found there.
- This is the first and only level to feature the Damned Souls.
- With some luck it is possible to loot a unique Kalash from a communist soldier on the second floor, distinguished by a green laser sight instead of the usual red.
