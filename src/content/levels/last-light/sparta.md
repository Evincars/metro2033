# Sparta

**Sparta** is the second level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light) and the first level that can be played — albeit with limited controls.

## Overview

The level opens with a man, presumably [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom), sitting at a guardpost with other sentries outside [Exhibition station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)), echoing the beginning of the [original novel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)). A nearby soldier reminisces about where he lived before the war when screams break out at the next outpost deeper in the tunnel, and everything falls dark and silent.

Artyom and his comrades stand ready as three [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones) materialize from the darkness alongside several [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalises). As Artyom guns down the creatures, he is horrified to realize he was hallucinating — the "monsters" were in fact his fellow soldiers. As a Dark One reaches down to touch him, Artyom is jolted awake.

He wakes in his bed at [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)), roused by [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)). It was all a dream. Khan reveals that he has spotted a surviving Dark One in the [Botanical Garden](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Botanical_garden) and insists they must find it. He urges Artyom to seek [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller)'s approval.

## Gearing Up

Artyom heads to the armoury to equip himself. Along the way he can listen to [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) talking for **moral points**, play instruments, and overhear talk of a missing Spartan, [Lesnitsky](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lesnitsky), who was guarding a stash of [chemical weapons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Virus). At the armoury he receives a [gas mask](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gas_mask), [medkit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Medkit), some [MGRs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR), a free choice of weapons to customize, and a chance to test them on the shooting range.

## The Mission

At the meeting, Khan informs Miller of the surviving Dark One and asks to take Artyom to locate it. Miller agrees but instead sends his own daughter, the sniper [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna), to kill the lone [Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One). Khan objects and is escorted out by guards.

Anna teases Artyom, calling him a "rabbit," unconvinced he is a capable soldier. The two leave the facility on a monorail bound for the gardens — the very route Artyom and Miller took a year earlier to launch the missile strike against the Dark Ones.

## Trivia

- The station where Miller is found is labeled "China-Town" on the map, referring to [Kitay-Gorod](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kitay-gorod), which translates as "China Town."
- Several areas first encountered in Metro 2033 are revisited in this level.
