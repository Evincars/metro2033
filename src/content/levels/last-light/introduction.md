# Introduction

**Introduction** is the first level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light), serving solely as a cutscene with [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) as the narrator.

## Overview

The level contains no gameplay. Instead, it establishes the tone of the game through Artyom's opening monologue, played over imagery of the world before and after the nuclear war.

Artyom recalls a childhood trip with his mother and two friends, **Vitali** and **Eugine**, to the [Botanical Gardens](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Botanical_Garden). He remembers taking the escalator to the surface, the open sky, the wind, and an ice cream his mother bought him — the last he would ever taste.

That day the bombs fell. Humanity fled underground into the Metro to survive the apocalypse, while the surface was left ruined and irradiated. Artyom laments that he can remember countless small, unimportant details of that day, yet he can no longer picture the one thing he wishes he could: his mother's face. She died shortly after the war began.

The monologue frames the survivors as people hiding from the wrath of a vengeful world, clinging to life on a used-up Earth, and sets the stage for Artyom's story in the Metro.
