# Tower Pack

The **Tower Pack** is the single longest level, and with [few](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Biomass_(Level)) [contenders](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Squad), the hardest level to date in the [Metro Video Game Series](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Video_Game_Series), and is the centerpiece of [Last Light's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) [Tower Pack DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tower_Pack_DLC). The player must fight their way through a combat simulator, battling humans and [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants) with all of Metro's [weaponry](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons).

## Overview

Gameplay for the Tower Pack is fairly simple; essentially, the goal is for the player to play defensively in a small area, outlasting all the enemies that appear in increasingly more difficult waves. Beginning each level, the player must choose their preferred weapons (all free of charge) for the next encounter, and, for three of the five levels, choose additional difficulty modifiers that might affect play. [Military grade rounds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR), granted for each kill and technical kill, have a special function as the currency for purchasing in-level bonuses — either an ammunition resupply or AI soldiers who assist you in combat.

Score is an important part of the Tower Pack, if one is interested in their performance and the online global leaderboards. Score can be increased by the difficulty you are playing on, difficulty modifiers, as well as in-level performance such as keeping additional gates sealed and performing technical kills. Technical kills include:

- **Distance kills** (roughly over 20 meters).
- **Headshots** (on human opponents only).
- **Double-Kills** (two kills within a second or two apart).
- **Multikills** (three or more kills within a second or two apart).

## Level 1 — Enter the Reich

The level is summed up best as a hall, the main feature being a long trench with several balconies leading across it, and a staircase leading down to a shallow pool. The area becomes host to a battle between the Captain and the simulated forces of the [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich). Enemies first spawn from the archways at the far end of the hall, but the Reich soldiers try to activate gate switches, which create more spawn areas. Activating a gate makes a loud alarm and takes roughly five seconds, but can be prevented if the player kills the soldier activating it.

**Round 1** features a large group of Nazis attacking with [Bastards](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bastard). **Round 2** begins similarly but at the half-way point introduces armored soldiers with surprisingly accurate [Shamblers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shambler), remarkably deadly at close-range. Planting Claymore Mines on the upper left balcony and the front right staircase protects the gate switches, while taking cover on the left side of the spawn bridge with a Kalash creates an effective firing line.

## Level 2 — Nosalis Nightmare

For many players, this second level is a massive roadblock. The map is another hall with a large opening area that splits into a wet lower ground and a huge balcony. The enemies are [Nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalises) — far more fearsome than their [2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) counterparts, intensely damaging and with an unmatched attack rate. This level adds the **Darkness** modifier, which removes ambient lighting, and **Huge**, which adds another round.

Round 1 is quite merciful, with Nosalises slowly spawning. Round 2 adds Nosalises attacking the front gate and introduces irradiated green Nosalises with roughly 1.5x more health. Round 3 spikes in difficulty with the inclusion of [winged Nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis#Winged_Nosalis), which produce an aggravating sonic screech that slows the player. Rounds 4 and 5 escalate spawn rates further. Weapon selection is crucial: the [Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M)) is overall the best all-rounder, the [Revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver) excels in early Ranger-difficulty waves, and the [Bastard](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bastard) benefits from displaying its ammunition prominently.

## Level 3 — Defenders

The third level shares a lot with the second, with common, green, and winged Nosalises returning at even higher spawn rates. The map is a long hallway resembling a large sewer cistern, with two gates to protect. Crucially, the player can spend military grade rounds to purchase AI soldiers. This stage features the **Huge** modifier and a new **Horde** modifier that increases the number of enemies by about 1.2x–1.5x.

When purchasing soldiers, get as many at once as possible, as they cover each other well. Walking slightly ahead of the soldiers is a better strategy than hiding behind them, as Nosalises target the player more, and the AI concentrates their fire around the player. The [Valve](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Valve) is a natural long-range choice that one-shot-kills any Nosalis, while the [Kalash 2012](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_2012) is the best choice for close range combat.

## Level 4 — Blood-Red Square

A breather from Nosalises, level 4 returns to Nazis in a very different arena shaped like a large square, not unlike a boxing ring. A high rectangular platform sits in the middle, with the enemy spawn immediately in front. All rounds keep a moderate difficulty, making it one of the easiest levels of the Tower. Round 3 introduces snipers armed with [Preveds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Preved) whose damage output is enormous. Weapon selection is not mandatory for survival here; the [Hellbreath](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hellbreath_(Volt_Driver)) is a good choice, exerting huge damage while firing quickly. Like the first level, level 4 is entirely about prevention, using claymores and frequent AI ally purchases.

## Level 5 — Last Stand

The final level of the tower is only three rounds long, but the last hurdle is not an easy one. The level is among the smallest of the pack — a large half circle with three gates, a gentle slope, and a minefield in the back. These features limit sniping and keep combat tight against Nosalises and [Librarians](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Librarians). Winged Nosalises do not return. Human AI partners return with a vengeance, as the heavy troopers here take an intense beating before dying and one-shot Nosalises on most difficulties.

Round 1 is the most difficult of the three, as it lacks AI partners. Round 2 adds Nosalises attacking the gates. The **Final Round** differs from every other round in that it has three waves: the first introduces the nigh-invincible Librarians, the second throws the number of Nosalises into overdrive, and the climax has all three gates blaring at once with Librarians smashing them down. The [Gatling](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gatling) is the perfect tool to handle the horde, but it must be hand-wound between waves to maintain its high rate of fire.

## Victory

After completing the final level, the captain exits the simulator and is greeted by a large and smiling audience. The grand total score of the player's efforts is shown on another electronic board. [Chook](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chook) and [Geek](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Geek) celebrate the captain's success, stating that there is no way the plug for the project could ever be pulled now.

## Trivia

- The hub room of the tower pack has a visible XYZ origin node, common in software depicting 3D spaces.
- Players fond of Valve's [Half-Life](https://half-life.fandom.com/wiki/Half-Life_series) series may find the last round in level 5 particularly reminiscent of a scene in the second additional episode of Half-Life 2.
- There is a known glitch where rapidly toggling your mask on and off will cause a round to be considered complete — useful for trophy hunters seeking the [Test Complete](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Test_Complete) achievement.
