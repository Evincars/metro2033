# Bridge

**Bridge** is the twenty-fifth level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place on the Luzhniki Metro Bridge in [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow).

> "Whatever my intentions concerning him might be, the little Dark One just does not seem to care. He keeps following me as if I never was going after him, as if we were tied together by fate..."
> — Level description

## Overview

[Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and the [Baby Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One) must travel through the city using a bridge. The level is based on the [Vorobyovy Gory](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vorobyovy_Gory) station of the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro), which sits on the Luzhniki Metro Bridge — though the surroundings of the bridge differ from their real-world counterpart.

As Artyom crosses, the Little Dark One shares further visions, and waves of mutants attack. The level can be completed without killing any mutant, which also unlocks the [Rain Man](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rain_Man) achievement.

## Notes

- Inside a small room off the bridge, Artyom can pick up a [Valve](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Valve) fitted with a 4x scope and laser sight.
- In the area beneath the bridge there is a safe key near a body draped over a partition; the matching safe is in a small room just before the escalators, and a silenced [VSV](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/VSV_(VSK-94)) can be found on one of the nearby bodies.
- Artyom can remove his gas mask while riding the zipline without taking any suffocation damage.
