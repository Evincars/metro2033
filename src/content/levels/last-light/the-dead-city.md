# The Dead City

**The Dead City** is the twenty-seventh level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place in the ruins of [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow). In [Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Redux) the level is renamed "City of Phantoms".

> "He who sows the wind, shall reap the storm. A peace conference is being held at Polis. Laughable, the war has already started. All trying to stop it could just as well try and stop a tornado with their bare hands. I have to reach Polis to denounce the liars. And face the storm."
> — Level description

## Overview

The Dead City (Город Призраков — "City of Ghosts" in Russian) was once a thriving residential district of Moscow. When the nuclear bombs fell, the people caught in the blast were trapped as [ghosts](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ghosts_(Phenomenon)). Throughout the level Artyom encounters these phenomena — not hostile, but disorienting and unsettling — and, if he is in the right place at the right time, witnesses visions of the events preceding the nukes. Locating all of these visions counts toward the [Back to the Past](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Back_to_the_Past) achievement.

Scattered recordings offer insight into the area, most left behind by a team of [Stalkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers) who explored the ruins; only their abandoned camps remain. Many of these recordings refer to a strange, foghorn-like noise — heard at the end of the level in the darkened tunnel — which one [Ranger's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) final message warns future adventurers to avoid.

The only living inhabitants of the Dead City are mutants — chiefly [Watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchmen) and [Demons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Demons), the latter having nested there — but many can be avoided by following the [Baby Dark One's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One) guidance. The main adversary here is simply fear.

## Trivia

- The Demon nesting on the rooftop is the easiest one in the game to kill with the [Helsing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Helsing), making it ideal for the "Van Helsing" achievement, and a silenced [Kalash 2012](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_2012) can be found at her nest.
- Late in the level, while traversing the [darkness](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ghosts), stopping and turning around at the right moment reveals a wall of reaching human figures that quickly vanishes — foreshadowing the damned souls of the next level.
- In a flooded basement near a playground, the ghost of a crying child appears but cannot be looked at directly, stepping away and reappearing elsewhere if approached.
