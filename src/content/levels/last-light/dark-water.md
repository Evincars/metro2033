# Dark Water

**Dark Water** is the fifteenth [level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Levels) in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place in a flooded tunnel between Revolution Square and [Tretyakovskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Location)).

> The snow on the surface starts melting... The familiar railcars are now joined with boats... and fishermen. One of them appeared just in the nick of time. Soon we'll be docked in Venice. Pavel is most probably already there.
>
> — Level description

## Overview

After being rescued, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) is taken to [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Location)) through the flooded tunnels by the fisherman [Fedor](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fedor). This short, on-rails chapter plays out from Fedor's [boat](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/River_Boat) as it drifts through the dark, half-submerged passages.

## Walkthrough

As the boat moves through the flooded tunnels, the pair are set upon by [Shrimps](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shrimp). Artyom defends the vessel until Fedor throws a box of grenades to scatter the swarm, and the level ends once they escape the creatures and near the docks of Venice.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- One point is earned at the end of the level after escaping the [Shrimps](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shrimp).

## Glitches

- After a box of grenades is thrown to the group of Shrimps, the fisherman may vanish; the player can no longer see him, but the dialogue continues as usual.
- After the grenades are thrown, the resulting wave of water may push the player through the world geometry.
