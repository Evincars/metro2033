# Undercity

**Undercity** is the nineteenth [level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Levels) in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place in the catacombs of the [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow) underground.

> For some reason I was genuinely glad to see Anna, my pain in the ass partner... She finally stopped those wicked jokes of hers. Looks like she's ashamed of having left her partner at the battlefield.
>
> — Level description

## Overview

Arguably one of the eeriest and most unnerving levels in the game, alongside [Regina](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Regina_(Level)), [Nightfall](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nightfall), and [Dead City](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dead_City_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)). [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) must venture through the mysterious catacombs beneath [Sparta base](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sparta_Base_(Location)).

The undercity is a series of enormous, naturally formed caverns connected to the church's basement and catacombs. Though they bear signs of human habitation going back hundreds of years, they had been untouched for at least a century until the [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) rediscovered them after camping in the church above. Expeditions revealed a route to the Metro tunnels, but the Rangers avoid the caverns whenever possible: the area is home to a nest of [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis), and strange things are known to happen there.

There are an enormous number of nosalises here, made troublesome by the game's ammo-carrying limit. Ammo can be bought at the start of the level, and stealth with throwing knives works in some places; the [Saiga-12](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Saiga-12) and [RPK-74](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/RPK-74) are good choices for heavier firepower. A boss battle with the [rhino](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rhino) awaits near the end, and [claymores](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Claymore_Mine) are highly effective against her — killing her in two hits on Ranger modes.

The level also introduces the [Darkness](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness), a rarely encountered phenomenon similar to the "Dark Star" [anomaly](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anomaly_(Phenomenon)). Unlike the Dark Star, the Darkness is formless and invisible, known only by its effect on electrical light: it disables Artyom's flashlight and night vision goggles. His only portable light is his [lighter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lighter), used to ignite torches along the way, though night vision scopes remain unaffected. Throughout the level Artyom hears distant gunshots and sees muzzle flashes; on reaching each location he finds mutilated corpses and dead nosalises, many of them recently killed men in [Lesnitsky](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lesnitsky)'s uniforms, their gear ready to be taken.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- Just before the catacombs, play the tape on the body near a table reaching for a recorder — the Ranger's last words about the Reds taking Anna into the catacombs.
- After entering the catacombs, move forward past the lightable torches in the dark; when the church bell rings, several bloody ghosts briefly appear ahead — wait for them to vanish.
- One point is gained automatically as Artyom is lowered deeper into the catacombs on the elevator, just before the nosalises attack.
- After being knocked off the elevator, witness the nosalis pack moving through the tunnels; a second point is earned if they are allowed to pass unharmed.
- Follow the nosalis pack's path to a small Christian shrine of Eastern Orthodox icons and candle stands.
- After descending the small lift and meeting the winged nosalises, approach the skeleton with a custom quadruple-barreled duplet near the spiderwebs.

**Lose:**

- Shooting at the nosalis pack after getting knocked off the elevator deducts a point.

## Notes

- In the final lift area before the [nosalis rhino](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis_Rhino), it is more effective to run laps or take the high ground to the right and conserve ammo, since the pack eventually flees after a set time. A spot just right of the elevator lets you climb out of reach.
- In the first rhino encounter you need not attack her at all — let her destroy the pillars and she will run away, saving ammo. She must be killed in the second encounter, where the [claymore mine](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Claymore_Mine) is very effective.

## Bugs

- Walking back into the fire and debris blocking the original spawn after the church door is blown open can repeat the door-knocking sound and music change.
- Sometimes the nosalis rhino freezes after the cutscene, standing still and knocking Artyom back without animation; reloading the checkpoint fixes it.
- Among the nosalises eating corpses is a handle-less [duplet](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duplet) that functions normally despite the missing part.
- Hugging the lever behind the handle when calling the lift can prevent the ambush nosalises from spawning, skipping the fight.
- For unknown reasons, the game may crash to desktop when quitting or reloading a checkpoint at certain points; the only fix is to play the level start to finish without quitting or reloading. This affects only the Undercity level.
