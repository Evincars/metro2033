# The Chase

**The Chase** is the twenty-third level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place in the tunnel between Oktyabrskaya and Park Kultury.

> "I never believed him - and that is no surprise - but Khan's words were true. This strange river took me to the past, the moment I sent out the signal and destroyed all the Dark Ones. And then it dropped me off at the place where I can find the last survivor of that race - a helpless and harmless child... Khan called him the Last Angel."
> — Level description

## Overview

In this level, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)) chase down a [Hansa](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hansa) train — which has been hijacked by the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) — in order to rescue the [Baby Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One).

Near the end of the level the train inexplicably explodes, throwing Artyom and the Baby Dark One toward the surface. It is later revealed in the "[Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Chronicles_Pack_Level))" level of the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack) that [Ulman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ulman) and a group of [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) were the ones who blew up the train.

## Trivia

- During the flashback with Artyom's mother, the character models of the background people are reused from various NPCs in the story, including [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_(Character)) and [Leonid](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Leonid).
- In pre-release gameplay, the train was originally taken over by the [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) rather than Red Line troops, suggesting the story may at one point have featured the Nazis as the main antagonists.
