# Separation

> "We pulled off the impossible... Now I have to inform the Order that the Dark One is missing. Which means to acknowledge that I've failed my mission, but now, when he's down in the Metro I won't be able to find him alone. I have to get to Polis, the Order's HQ. Pavel will show me the way."
> — Level description

**Separation** is the sixth level of [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

## Overview

[Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov) is captured by [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) soldiers in this level, and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) must either sneak or fight his way through a Reich outpost to save him. The stealth approach isn't particularly hard as long as the player sticks to the shadows, shoots out or otherwise extinguishes sources of light, looks for alternate routes past well-lit areas, and eliminates Nazis who wander off into the dark.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- Complete the level without killing any humans, or simply spare the man reading on his bed after he surrenders.

**Lose:**

- After exiting the first area of Nazis, kill the man reading on his bed after he surrenders.

## Trivia

- A Reich soldier wearing headphones can be found jamming out to "The Market," a track from the *Metro 2033* soundtrack. With his eyes closed, he will never spot Artyom unless unsuppressed gunshots are fired or Artyom touches him.
- In the main outpost area, an armoured train fitted with a [Panzer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Panzer) turret sits on the elevated tracks.
- The surrendering Nazi soldier is seen reading *The Evolution of Species* by Charles Darwin.
- The [Gatling](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gatling) was featured in E3 demos of this level but cut from the final version. In those demos Khan assisted Artyom's escape, whereas the final game has Artyom rescue Pavel from hanging.
