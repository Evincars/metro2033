# Bolshoi

> "Our short visit to Dead Moscow, the phantom of the past, is over. Pavel and I helped each other out again and are returning down to the Metro as real partners now. You can't survive the wastelands any other way. But now ahead of us lies a populated station, the Theater. It's very close to Polis. If Pavel manages to lead me through the Red Line guard posts, I'll be home in less than an hour."
> — Level description

**Bolshoi** is the tenth level of [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

## Overview

Having survived the surface, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov) descend into the populated [Theatre Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Theatre_Station), presumably under Red Line control. The level is a slower, story-focused walk through a lively station full of refugees, beggars, performers, dancers and a live theatre show, giving the player many opportunities to explore and gather **moral points** by observing station life. A shopkeeper here sells weapons, letting Artyom re-arm before pressing on toward Polis.

## Moral Points

Most points come from stopping to watch and listen — a couple arguing about army height requirements, refugees pleading with the guards, giving a bullet to the theatre critic-turned-beggar, bar patrons debating the value of theatre, stalkers informing Katja of her husband's death, Mischa entertaining children with shadow puppets, and watching the full theatre show (which also earns the **Patron of the Arts** achievement).

## Trivia

- The level is based on [Teatralnaya Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Theatre_Station) of the Moscow Metro.
- Looking in the dressing-room mirror shows Pavel and the dancers but no reflection of Artyom, likely to preserve his anonymity.
- The speech after the "watchman trainer" show references a "Hero of the Exhibition" who used a "mushroom" to destroy the Dark Ones — a nod to Artyom's deeds in *Metro 2033*, unaware that the hero is standing among them.
