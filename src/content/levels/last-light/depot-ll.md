# Depot

**Depot** is the twenty-sixth level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place in an abandoned train depot in [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow).

> "We are coming to Polis. Together. Come what may. The Little One did not want to leave. Something is holding him... I don't yet get what. Nevertheless, I'm glad it is, because he is helpful."
> — Level description

## Overview

[Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) must make his way through the surface section of the Metro train [depot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Depot_(Location)), with the help of the [Baby Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One), on his way to [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis). The depot is heavily garrisoned by the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line), with roughly forty Reds staged across a series of ambush areas, many of which hide passages beneath the fighting floors.

A key moment comes when Artyom confronts the traitor [Lesnitsky](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lesnitsky): knocking him unconscious earns a moral point, while killing him loses one.

## Notes

- This is the last shop in the game; afterward the player can spend MGR ammunition freely without worrying about future purchases.
- Overheard Red Line conversations reveal the soldiers are afraid of Artyom, recalling how he wiped out a squad of Nazis aboard the [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) train.
- If the player spares Lesnitsky, his gas mask can be found discarded on the floor nearby — realistically implying he dies regardless of the choice, most likely a developer oversight.
