# Quarantine

**Quarantine** is the twenty-first level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

> The pieces of the puzzle are falling together, and the big picture is horrifying and unbelievable. I will tell the other about this, but I still can't make myself believe it. The Reds have infected Oktyabrskaya, a peaceful station, denizens of which never suspected anything, with an unknown deadly virus. And then their mop-up squads entered it under the pretense of saving the station from the epidemic and eliminated everyone they could — women, children, old people, to prevent the infection from spreading... and if all this is, then me and Anna could be infected, too. We've been saved from being killed, but we don't know if we're going to survive for long.
>
> — Level description

## Overview

Reeling from the massacre at Oktyabrskaya, Artyom moves through a makeshift quarantine zone where doctors and guards struggle to contain [The Virus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Virus) — revealed here to be a Red Line bioweapon. He passes the sick and dying, an abandoned section of the station, and eventually meets [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)), who negotiates passage through a [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) checkpoint and armory.

## Walkthrough

The level is largely a somber, story-driven passage. Artyom is first locked in a sealed room where a doctor explains the virus is a bioweapon, then moves through quarantine wards full of infected patients, grieving families, and overwhelmed medics. After meeting Khan, he passes a Hanza-controlled station and armory, stocking up on filters and ammunition before continuing on.

## Diary Entries

- After being locked in a sealed room where a doctor explains that [The Virus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Virus) is a bioweapon, head around the desk on the right; the note is on the desk in front of you.
- After meeting [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)), instead of following him left toward the armory door, head down the right hall to find the note on a desk.

## Moral Points

**Gain:**

- In the first section, listen to all three conversations: the sick father and daughter; the pacing doctor and yellow-suited guard by the stairs; and a doctor comforting an infected soldier and children.
- In the next section, listen to all four conversations: the amputated doctor with a girl; a doctor and soldier checking a body near the cassette player; two doctors scanning a patient; and a man on a gurney insisting he only has a cold (this last one begins only after the doctor and colonel finish in the decontamination room).
- Pick up four of the five [MGR](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Grade_Rounds) stashes in the abandoned part of the station (which also earns the **[Forgotten](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Forgotten)** achievement). The first is behind the entry door once Artyom heads down the stairs and it closes; the second in a train-car window behind the sink with a quarantine skull sign; the third in a train-car window left of the path before the long corridor with fire barrels; the fourth in a broken window on the right side of that corridor; and the last in a window on the right at the start of the corridor section with growing mushrooms.
- After meeting Khan, help the man asking the guard to let him retrieve insulin from the quarantine zone — give him bullets after he is refused.
- In the last section, listen to all four conversations: a ranger and a Hanza commander discussing the [Reds'](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) attack; a refugee struggling to pass the Hanza station without papers; a Hanza sergeant and worker talking about the quarantine near the second diary note; and three Hanza soldiers in the armory discussing the Polis rangers (this begins only after Khan finishes talking about stocking up filters and ammo).

## Trivia

- In the English version, numerous untranslated, unsubtitled lines can be heard; the first is a woman praying over her husband, and others overlap as refugees speak just before one is shot for approaching the quarantine gates.
- [Ghostly whispers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ghosts_(Phenomenon)) can be heard by standing at the quarantine fence across from the sealed door at the start of the abandoned section, near the water fountains.

## Bugs

- To buy or customize a [Helsing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Helsing) here, do so **before** buying any Helsing bolts. A bug resets the Helsing ammunition counter to zero every time its customization menu is opened, and opening it will "forget" any bolts picked up in earlier levels.
