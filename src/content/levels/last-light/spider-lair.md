# Spider Lair

> "During the years that passed after the War, we stalkers left no stone unturned in the accessible regions of the city in our search for resources. That's why the news of the D6, a colossal secret bunker found in a pristine state had such an impact. All the Metro is dreaming of secret military facilities not marked on any maps now."
> — Level Description

**Spider Lair** is a single player level in [Last Light's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light) [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Season_Pass), the [Developer Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Developer_Pack_(DLC)), where you, as an [unnamed](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalker_(Spider_Lair)) [Stalker](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers), are forced to navigate through an old missile silo full of [Spiderbugs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spiderbugs), armed with little else but a [Flamethrower](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Flamethrower) and an [Ashot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashot).

## Overview

The level begins with the player, a [young stalker](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalker_(Spider_Lair)), sitting with two companions, [Simon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Simon_(Spider_Lair)) and [Petrovich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Petrovich), in the [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Location)) bar. They discuss the location of another bunker complex, similar to [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)) — how others who went there never returned, but Simon comments that they will survive. The group drink, and Petrovich offers a light but produces his Flamethrower from under the table, making Simon jump. They laugh and say they will be safe with the flamethrower.

Two days later, the Stalker awakens upside-down, suspended in Spiderbug webbing among many corpses. After freeing himself and dropping to the floor, he scrambles about looking for an exit and his companions. He eventually finds Simon, already long dead, and takes his [Head Torch](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Flashlight) and knife just before a Spiderbug attacks. Moving deeper into the facility, he finds Petrovich's corpse and his flamethrower, which proves incredibly effective against the Spiderbugs and their nests.

After ascending the old missile silos, he finally activates an elevator and makes it to the surface. However, as he does, a pack of [Watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchmen) ambush him, one pouncing as the level ends. It is unknown if he survived.

## Achievements

- **Through the Fire** — Complete the SPIDER LAIR level.
- **Arachnophobia** — Kill 20 spiders with your flame thrower.
