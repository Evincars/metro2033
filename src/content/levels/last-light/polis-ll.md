# Polis

**Polis** is the thirtieth level in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It takes place in [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)).

> "He is not alone... He's not the last! I understand his excitement and his wish to immediately meet the others. To free them and wake them up... but... He has agreed to wait a bit longer. First he's going to try and help us. If we make it to the conference, the Dark One will be able to open up the thoughts of Moskvin - or Korbut himself! Perhaps... make them stop this madness! This is our last chance."
> — Level description

## Overview

Polis is a non-combat level that concludes the story before [The Battle for D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Battle_for_D6). Walking with [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) and the [Baby Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One), [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) attends the [Polis Peace Conference](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_Peace_Conference), where the Dark One helps reveal the plans of [Moskvin](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moskvin) to the assembled factions.

## Trivia

- After the conference, when Artyom enters the [Automatic Train](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Automatic_Train) and sees [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna), the Baby Dark One gives a clue as to which [ending](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Endings) the player will receive based on their accumulated Moral Points — one line pointing toward the [C'est la Vie](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/C%27est_la_Vie_(Achievement)) ending, another toward the [Redemption](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Redemption_(Achievement)) ending.
- In the decontamination chamber, the Baby Dark One waves to a [Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) outside, who returns a friendly salute, before flailing in surprise as the decontamination gases are sprayed in.
- Although the [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) is the smallest of the three major factions, they have the most supporters gathered outside the Council Hall; the scarce [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) presence is explained by most of their forces being mobilized for the invasion of D6.
