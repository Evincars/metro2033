# Khan

> "It never ends, this stuff... You complete a mission and give the base a radio hail on your way back to let them know you're coming, they send you on another milk run. Sometimes I wish radio was never invented... Anyway, this time it seems like fun — I've got to stop a Hansa train captured by the Reds. It's been quite some time since I had stuff to blow up, so never mind if I do!"
> — Level Description

**Khan** is the second level in the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack) DLC for [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). The player plays as [Ulman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ulman), assisting [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)) in first stopping a [Hansa](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hansa) train occupied by the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) — the same one found in "[The Chase](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Chase_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))" level.

## Overview

Ulman temporarily relives the attack on Polyanka by [Nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalises), through Khan's eyes (the player plays as a young Khan), in which Khan was forced to close the gate to Polyanka, dooming his friends there. The level takes place at the same time as "[The Crossing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Crossing_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))," as we can see Ulman causing the explosion which knocks [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) out at the end of "The Chase." It also explains that, after Chase, Khan went directly to [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)) to warn [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller), waiting for Artyom at the [Garden](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Garden_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))'s entrance to Polis.

## Trivia

- This level reveals why the Red Line hijacked train exploded when Artyom was trying to find the Baby Dark One. It was Ulman's attempt to stop the train, which he was eager and enthusiastic to carry out.
- This is the only level where you can play two different characters (Ulman and Khan).
- When playing as Khan, he reloads his weapon twice as fast.
- It is entirely possible to sneak past the mutants to complete Khan's task.
- Regardless of how much time is left on the bomb's timer, it always blows up a few seconds after Khan is attacked by the Nosalis.
- Before entering the train with Khan at the beginning, entering the metal grille door to the left with a pile of corpses shows a hallucination of two children standing in a pose similar to the twins from Stanley Kubrick's film The Shining.
- In [Metro Awakening](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Awakening), it is revealed that the fateful incident at Polyanka happened in the year 2018. It is also revealed by Khan that a few days before the incident he had a premonition, but refrained from alerting anyone for fear of ridicule; afterward he is struck with guilt and becomes depressed for not saving anyone.
