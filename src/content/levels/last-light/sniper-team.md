# Sniper Team

> "Another raid against the Nazis. I have to clear out a Nazi outpost and infiltrate the Chernaya station. One of our scouts is coming with me this time. But he has a mission of his own. So we'll be working separately most of the time."
> — Level Description

**Sniper Team** is a mission in the [Faction Pack DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faction_Pack_DLC) for [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light), where the player, a [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) [sniper](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line_Sniper), along with a [spotter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line_Spotter), infiltrates a heavily guarded [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) outpost on the surface under the cover of a radioactive storm. The mission is heavily focused on stealth, and alerting the Nazis results in automatic failure.

## Equipment

The sniper is equipped with a [Clapper](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Clapper_(VSSK_Vychlop)) and a [Lolife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lolife) with a silencer, foregrip, stock, extended magazine, laser sight, autofire adapter, and reflex sight. The player cannot pick up any other weapons from killed enemies, and the only ammunition they can pick up is [.44 Magnum](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/.44_Magnum) or [12.7x108mm](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/12.7x108mm) rounds.

As the whole area is heavily irradiated, both the sniper and the Nazis must wear gas masks at all times. Initially, the player starts with a slightly cracked [gas mask](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gas_mask) with 10 minutes of extra [filters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Filters), though most Nazis carry spares that can be picked up. The sniper also carries two [throwing knives](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives), with more found along the way, and a [medkit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Medkit).

## Trivia

- This level is set in the same area as the [Outpost](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Outpost_(Level)) level from [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033), with new accessible areas and minor tweaks. As it's now spring, the ice has melted and new structures such as watch towers have been erected.
- This is the only level where stealth is essential; in other missions being detected means combat is tougher.
- The stealthy knock out attack has been disabled for this mission. Since masks are needed for survival on the surface, knocking enemies out would kill them anyway as they would be unable to change their filters.
- Many Nazis break off from patrol to visit their buddies — if they find their friends dead, they will run to the alarm and you will fail the mission, so make sure bodies are hidden in shadow.
- Turn off every light you can. Even the fires can usually be extinguished, but make sure there aren't any Nazis nearby, as they will be alerted by the mystery fireman.
- The conversations the Nazis have are interesting. Some mention the events of the [Pavel level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Level)) with the prison break. The Nazis in the camp appear to all be ex-prison guards who were fired and sent to the surface as punishment for allowing the prisoners to escape.
- The lightning outside can illuminate you for a brief moment, which could alert the Nazis. Alternatively, you could use this to lead single Nazis away to be killed.

## Bugs

- Some objects (like the flaming barrel on the roof) will appear to hover over the ground.
- In the Redux version, if your checkpoint was just getting off a ladder, there is a possibility you will be stuck in place, resulting in restarting the level. Initiating a filter replacement after dismounting the ladder may result in a softlock state; to prevent this, unequip your gas mask and reequip it once you are off the ladder.
