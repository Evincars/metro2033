# Ghosts

> Khan's appearance caught me by surprise, although, while fighting the bandits, I did sense someone helping me from the shadows. Khan assured me that Bourbon's fate was not tied to my own. But I regretted his death.
> — Level description

> Nobody ever walks here. Neither people nor beasts. Even the rats are absent...
> — Khan

**Ghosts** is the first level in which you are accompanied by [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)), who takes you through the haunted tunnels of the metro system.

**Chapter:** 3 "Khan"
**Objective:** Reach Cursed Station with Khan.

## Overview

No combat happens in Ghosts (expect some 3 or 4 [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalises) at the end), as it is essentially a filler to give the player some background on the storyline, specifically Khan, the [ghosts](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ghosts_(Phenomenon)), and the [Anomalies](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anomalies). The level is also used to create an eerie atmosphere for the player to experience.

Khan reveals some history of this particular part of [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro). A great battle was waged there, where he was the only survivor; his fallen comrades were fated to live in a constant limbo as ghosts, never to pass on, always having to replay the final moments of their lives. [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and Khan encounter his old squad near the end of the level, where they're all standing ready to battle, blocking the path for Khan and Artyom. Khan chants a form of Latin, which causes the ghosts of his squad to part to the sides, allowing Artyom and Khan to pass. Khan asks Artyom to not ask about what he had just done, as he states that it was very personal to him.

## Trivia

- More often than not the bodies of the ghostly victims are shown near to where each ghost resides. The remains of a woman's ghost, a running man's ghost, and the skull of a child can be found here, as well as the last car of the train.
- The nosalis that attacks the child is also a ghost, which suggests it also died soon after.
- Khan's hymn is in Sanskrit or rough Latin. He chants "Casto noi lapide asterium manto, alaum raum om", which is an ancient mantra for dismissing negative energy from a body.
- This level is only one of the few levels ("Chase", "Ghosts", and "Cursed" have it) to feature ghosts, even though they are mentioned quite frequently. It's speculated that the Ghosts were to originally play a bigger role in the game, but were most likely removed from later levels.
- The so-called "terrible event" are in fact multiple events because several different ghosts can be seen in the tunnels, such as: a group of men guarding a barricade (Khan's former group), a small boy running from a nosalis, a woman crying over a dead man, a metro train moving through the tunnels (or, at least, the lights, shadows, and noise), and a few other ghosts in certain areas. These events are most likely unrelated because it wouldn't make any sense for a group of men to erect a barricade when they knew a train would eventually arrive. But the moving train preceded the men's construction of the barricade, because before the disaster happened, the train was moving in the tunnel and got caught in the destruction.
- In this level, you encounter a Ghost Train, which predates the ghosts residing at this level right up to when the nuclear war occurred. The Ghosts on this train were most likely some of the first Ghosts in the Metro. This train was likely travelling when the disaster happened.
  - If you toss a throwing knife at the Ghost Train, there will be a blood splatter as if you have hit an enemy and the knife will be gone.
  - The player will be killed by the Ghost Train if they move too slow.
- If Artyom moves in front of Khan while the small boy is running away from the nosalis, it will jump and kill him.
- If the player looks at their stopwatch while in this level, they will notice that it has stopped ticking but will still keep time. This is most likely due to the fact that the watch keeps the time on the player's console/PC and it would thus be easier to simply stop the ticking.
- The exact location of this tunnel is between Sukharevskaya (Dry) and Turgenevskaya (Cursed Station).
- In [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux), this level and "[Anomaly](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anomaly_(Level))" are combined into one. It also features an added visual effect; if the player listens to the singing pipes for too long, shadowy hands reach out and try to grab them, reminiscent of the [Damned Souls](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Damned_Souls) from [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).
- In the Redux version of the game, Khan's Kalash is a standard Kalash.
