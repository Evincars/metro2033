# Trolley Combat

> When I came to my senses, I lost all hope, Nazi prisoners were used for slave labor or target practice.
> — Level description

**Trolley Combat** is the first level after [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) encounters [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Ranger)) and [Ulman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ulman). The two rangers save Artyom from being executed by two Nazis. Ulman silently kills the two Nazis with his knife. The level itself is a shootout between the Nazi checkpoint guards and Artyom.

**Chapter:** 4 "War"
**Objective:** Break through the fascist territory with Pavel to Black Station and meet with Ulman.

## Overview

After being saved by Ulman and Pavel, Artyom shows the two rangers [Hunter's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Ranger)) dogtags, implying his association with the ranger faction. They immediately tell Artyom that he needs to go see [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller), the leader of the rangers. Ulman leaves the group to continue their "mission" by himself, as Pavel and Artyom proceed on an armored trolley toward [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)) through a Nazi outpost. Artyom mans the machine gun turret while Pavel drives. As they enter the outpost, Pavel and Artyom try to deceive the officer by passing off as Nazi soldiers delivering ammo. They are unsuccessful, and this leads to a Nazi alert and a chase through the tunnels, throughout which lie various obstacles including guard stations and a few enemy [turret carts](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Railcar) that Artyom must destroy.

Near the end of the level, a [Panzer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Panzer) shows up on a parallel track and smashes a train off the rails, resulting in the destruction of a steel support beam. Artyom must then proceed by shooting down the remaining weakened support in order to bring down the track and the Panzer before the tank destroys Artyom's trolley.

## Trivia

- There's a [NVG](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Vision_Goggles) in the room behind you if you didn't pick up the ones from the previous level.
- The player's machine gun can fire as fast as one can pull the trigger. This does affect the overheating as well, however. Holding the trigger is not recommended, as the gun will overheat very quickly.
- If the driver of an enemy trolley is killed, the trolley will immediately explode; this is quite risky because Artyom may be shot to pieces by the gunner if the player is concentrating on the driver.
- If the gunner of an enemy trolley is killed, the driver will constantly ram the back of Artyom's trolley.
- The last enemy trolley cannot be destroyed; it destroys itself by crashing, so after killing the gunner, stop shooting at it and restore your [machine gun's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DShK_12.7mm_Machinegun) temperature for the Panzer.
- Pavel will refer to the Panzer as a bulldozer when the battle starts, referring to its quite intimidating *path clearing* capabilities.
- If you wait a while at the closed gate where Pavel seems to fall asleep, you can hear him breathing heavily and sometimes snoring. After honking, Pavel will wake up with a grunt.
- You don't fight the Panzer in the [redux version](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux); however one is still seen in the level.
- The first two stations that you fight your way through are considered [Red](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) territory in *Metro: Last Light*, leading one to believe that the Reds took them during the time gap between the two games.
- There is a [Tihar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) with NV Sight & Laser Sight in the same room with the safe, which is to the left as Pavel turns right after the Trolley section.
