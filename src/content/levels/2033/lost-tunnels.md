# Lost Tunnels

> "As I entered the abandoned tunnels with Bourbon, I wondered if I'd been wrong to trust him. But the station gates were still sealed, and there was no telling when I'll have another chance to leave Riga."
> — Level description

**Objective:** Follow and protect Bourbon.

**Lost Tunnel** is a short/medium length action level that starts after [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) meets [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) and leaves [Riga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_Station_(Location)) with him. This is the first level featuring [lurkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lurkers) (not including the one in "[Chase](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chase_(Level))") as well as human enemies — the [bandits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits_(Faction)).
