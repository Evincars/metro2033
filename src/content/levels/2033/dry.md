# Dry

> As we went underground, it struck me that my city was now foreign and hostile place. I felt safe here, in the darkness of the Metro more so, when Bourbon claimed to have friends at Dry Station.
> — Level description

**Dry** is the first [level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Levels) where Artyom encounters [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)). It's also the level where [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) is killed.

**Chapter:** 3 "Khan"
**Place:** Sukharevskaya
**Objectives:**

- Try to help Bourbon.
- Reach Cursed Station with Khan.

## Overview

Dry takes place in Sukharevskaya station, which has been fortified into a [bandit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits) base. [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and Bourbon first reach this area through the ventilation shafts. Immediately after entering the station, Bourbon is captured by the bandits and taken to their leader. Artyom then either fights or sneaks his way through the base to where Bourbon is being held captive.

The moment he walks through the door, Bourbon takes advantage of the confusion to take the bandit leader's [revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver), and subsequently kills the leader. Before dying, the bandit leader manages to kill Bourbon with his [Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M)). Khan then appears from the above vent and offers to help Artyom along his path.

## Trivia

- Apparently the bandits at Sukharevskaya, or at least half of them, are possibly former communists from the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line), as evident by the music typical of the Reds that can be heard playing in Sukharevskaya. Also, in the Redux version of Metro 2033, the Bandit leader looks like a Red Line commander or officer.
- The rebar sticking out of the sandbag barricades in this level will damage Artyom if he stands or walks too close to them. These are the only rebar barricades in the game that do this.
- If you climb up to the main balcony, you can change the song that is playing in the station from a record player there. Doing this will alert the bandits nearby.
- Near the end of the level, to the left before the door where Bourbon is found, is a box maze. Inside, there is a guard sitting with a hookah. Artyom can either knock down or kill the guard. Smoking the hookah makes Artyom more resilient to damage and also gains a [moral point](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points). Smoking will produce a visual effect and scramble your movement controls for a short time.
- There is a second hookah near the left-side barricade, one room away from the first guard Khan snipes.
- Although many [arrows](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arrows) can be found, the [Helsing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Helsing) isn't available for sale until later in the game. However, there is one hidden in the earlier chapter "Dead City 1", which can be put to good use here.
- If choosing a stealth approach to the mission, Khan will kill certain guards silently if Artyom remains idle and looks at the guard long enough. The guards may make sounds but this will not alert their friends. Khan also starts firing when guards get suspicious of an intruder. You'll know he is helping you when you hear the firing sound of a Helsing. The arrows Khan fires can be picked up.
- Even if Artyom kills everybody inside the station, bandits will still appear when reaching the door cutscene at the end.
- There are a few [traps](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Traps) and alarms set up in the side passages. Triggering these or making enough noise by bumping into clutter will alert the bandits.
- Near the arrow range at the back of the station, a prison can be found under a grate. The man inside is the caravan master that the bandits at the Riga station camp mentioned.
- In Moscow, this station is named Sukharevskaya (Kaluzhsko-Rizhskaya Line).
- On the left side of the vents, behind a barricade, is a man complaining of stomach trouble. He said that one of his friends forced him to eat something that caused his stomach problems. He will soon run towards the vents to use the restroom, leaving a good time for stealth kills.
- At the start of the level where you are in the vent, there is a Kalash and [Shambler](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shambler) in the back of the vent where Bourbon's bag is. The Kalash is Bourbon's and it seems he left it for Artyom, thus (perhaps unintentionally) fulfilling his promise of giving him his AK.
