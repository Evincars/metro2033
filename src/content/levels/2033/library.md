# Library

> Upon entering the Great Library, I was in awe. It seemed like a fairy tale castle, and as in the old stories, it's treasures were guarded by monsters.
>
> — Level description

The level **Library** is an action-based level, taking place within the [Moscow State Library](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_State_Library). After fighting through the alley behind the library, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) meets [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) and [Danila](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Danila) outside the library's front doors. Inside the library is the information needed to get to [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)).

## Overview

As an escort mission, there is very little that the player has to do on their own. The level starts when Artyom and Miller enter the library and Danila secures the entrance. Once they reach the main hall, a [demon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Demon) attacks Danila. Making their way through the main hall, Danila and Miller block the main hall door while the demon tries to smash it down. After finding the other door conspicuously closed and making his way around the hall, Artyom manages to climb into an adjacent room and unlock the door for the [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers).

After another, less lethal, encounter with the demon and another temporary separation with his teammates, Artyom and the Rangers make their way to the main hall. Distracted by a fleeing [Librarian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Librarian), and unable to fend off the demon again, Danila is injured while Artyom is forced to advance into the ruined library alone.

## Trivia

- While sifting through some of the books in the library, Danila mentions "Roadside Picnic, hmm... something familiar...". *Roadside Picnic* was the book the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. video games were loosely based on, as well as the inspiration for the Metro books according to [Dmitry Glukhovsky](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dmitry_Glukhovsky).
- The interior of the library is slightly different in Metro 2033 than in Metro: Last Light. Metro 2033 Redux reuses the Last Light layout, but the Redux version is cobweb covered, hinting at spiderbug activity.
- Almost all frozen corpses found in this level are [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) soldiers; the small "C" insignia can be seen on their arms.
  - It's odd that Polis would allow anyone other than their own Kshatriya to get that close to the Library. Seeing as how Polis are the main suppliers of books in the Metro, they wouldn't want any 'competition' from rival factions.
  - Alternatively, it is possible that some of those Reich Stalkers did venture too close to the Library and were shot by Polis sentries.
  - However, in Metro 2033 Redux, all the dead Reich Stalker models are replaced by generic Hanza/Red Line ones.
- After a demon attack in the Main Reading Hall, Miller mentions that dawn is coming, although it's clearly day outside. Screenshots from the beta version show that both "Alley" and "Library" happened around either dawn or dusk.
