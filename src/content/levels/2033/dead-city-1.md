# Dead City 1

> "When we emerged into the ruins of Moscow, I felt both fear and sorrow. But, after a life underground, I also saw strange beauty in the dark skies and frozen landscape. 'So, that's the dead city,' grunted Bourbon. 'Welcome home, Artyom.'"
> — Level description

The level **Dead City 1** is a long level which starts right after [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) leaves the [Market station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_Station_(Location)) with [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon). This is the first mission, chronologically, where Artyom is travelling across the surface. The level finishes just after the second [Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_One) is spotted.

**Objectives:**

- Find the entrance to Dry Station
- I'll have to go around to meet up with Bourbon behind the hotel.

## Overview

Artyom walks around half a kilometer (0.3 miles) on the surface from [Prospekt Mira](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_Station_(Location)) to [Sukharevskaya (Dry Station)](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dry_Station_(Location)), passing through the ruined surroundings of Prospekt Mira Avenue in downtown [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow).

Despite being visually imposing, the actual level consists only of the Prospect Mira (Market) station vestibule, three crumbling buildings, two courtyards and a collapsed and flooded intersection.

Moscow was never bombed directly, thanks in no small part to Moscow Missile Defense systems, but without human maintenance, a lot of buildings began falling apart due to exposure to the elements and eventually filled the streets of the city with impassable piles of debris.

The buildings mentioned above contained a bank, a grocery store, and numerous offices respectively. An interesting fact is that the bank vault appears to be thoroughly looted while the office supplies and equipment remain virtually untouched. Be careful while exploring — traps can be found in some buildings.

## Trivia

- There is a [Tihar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) with a Reflex sight that can be found after your first Dark One encounter, after navigating the office & toilet area of the 2nd floor. Simply drop down from the elevator where the Dark One vanished to find the weapon near some loot. You can drop down a level further to exit the floor, but beware that there are 2 Watchers waiting below.
- Before entering the fifth building with the traps, you can go around the corner to the left end, walk up a piece of rebar, to find a [Helsing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Helsing).
- There is a tape recorder containing a message from [Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Ranger)), roughly detailing the Dark Ones' attacks at [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)). Hunter's message can only be played once. This area is likely to be the outpost for Hunter.
- From this level alone you can clearly see that the Dark Ones only want peace. In the playground sequence, it's possible that the Dark One created this illusion to symbolize that it wants the world to be like this again, as well as to try and observe the actions of Artyom.
- If the player opens fire during the playground vision, they will lose moral points and the Dark One will remark *"He only wants to kill. He doesn't understand."*
- It's possible to kill all of the [demons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Demons) on this level and get an [Inquisitor](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Inquisitor) achievement. A stash with sticky grenades is conveniently placed in the truck on the intersection for just such an occasion.
- In the [Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux) version, "Dead City 1" and "Dead City 2" have merged into a single level. A previously unexplorable area is host to a single Watchman protecting its cubs, and the sun slowly sets during this level, giving way to nightfall and a whole new aesthetic.
- In the building with the tape recorder, Artyom can hear footsteps and the sound of keys rustling and locking or unlocking a door. This either adds to the haunted feel of some buildings or suggests there are others on the surface.
- There is an achievement for finding all the Ranger Stashes in the level.

## Bug

- In the same building with Hunter's tape recording, at the very end of the flight of stairs, you can drop down to a pile of water that leads to your death even if there logically shouldn't be a pool of water there.

## Dark One Sightings

It's possible to spot several Dark Ones on this level. The first one can be briefly seen in the offices, while the second one will cause a flashback when Artyom passes through the playground, and finally the third one is checking a corpse in the gated passage to the courtyard of the building Bourbon told Artyom to head towards.
