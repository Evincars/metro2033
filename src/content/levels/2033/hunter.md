# Hunter

> "Life was never easy in the tunnels, but it was our home. There was comfort in its routines, in seeing the same people day after day. But since the mutant attacks has escalated, fear ruled the station. I had just turned twenty... and could never had imagined what would follow on the morning that my stepfather's friend, Hunter, arrived at the barricade..."
> — Level description

**Hunter** is a short level which establishes the setting and story as well as gives the player a chance at some more combat. Hunter is the second level of the Prologue chapter.

## Overview

The level takes place at [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) and depicts the events of the beginning of [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s crucial mission. This level gives the player an opportunity to look at different parts of Artyom's home station and to listen in on some conversations of the metro inhabitants that can give the player a better insight into the game. The player will be able to acquire a standard [revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver) as they need to defend the station from a mutant attack.

This level starts with Artyom asleep in his bunker. [Alex](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alex) wakes him up, and the two go through the station to the airlock. Shortly, [Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Ranger)) arrives. After dealing with a nosalis attack, the trio are informed that the station has been attacked, and they rush to the spot. Afterwards, Hunter gives Artyom his badge, saying that if he does not return, Artyom will need to travel to [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)) and report to Miller and the Council about the current situation at Exhibition, and the threat that closes in on them.

## Trivia

- Although present, the player is unable to equip the [trench knife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Trench_Knife); it is only available for use once the mutants start to attack the station.
- The only weapons that can be used are a standard revolver and the trench knife.
- The revolver doesn't need to be acquired to beat the level, the [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) will spawn regardless.
- If the player doesn't take the postcard from Hunter's hand, he will keep it beside him, where it can be picked up again.

## Related Achievements

- **Quick Draw** (30 game points) — On the level "Hunter", kill the nosalises before they break through the ventilation grilles.
- **First Blood** (5 game points) — What doesn't kill you, makes you stronger.
