# Lost Catacombs

> "Market Station was just a little further, but Bourbon warned me that our path went through the 'lost catacombs'..."
> — Level description

**Objective:** Follow and protect Bourbon.

**The Lost Catacombs** is a short level in which [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) escape the [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalises) trailing after them from the [bridge](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bridge_(Metro_2033_Level)).

## Overview

The place that they escape to is a graveyard with bodies, and the things that await them there may be worse than nosalises. After a strange [supernatural door](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Door) attempts to kill them, a [Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_One) reaches through the door and saves them. The journey remaining to Market Station is a short one but still overshadowed by the lurking nosalises. Bourbon eventually leads Artyom to the door of [Market Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_Station_(Location)).

As the trailing nosalises finally reach the duo, Hansa's armored railed car begins to mobilize. As the last nosalises are fended off, the guards turn their weapons towards Bourbon and Artyom, as it becomes apparent that Bourbon has racked up debt with them in the past. Artyom and Bourbon are eventually guided by the light and safety of Hansa, albeit at gunpoint.

## Trivia

- Strangely, Bourbon doesn't need to wear his [mask](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gas_Mask) when in the graveyard, while you need to.
- If you shoot the [Hansa](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hansa) armoured railcar, the gunner will respond by returning fire and killing Bourbon in the process. If this happens, you will fail the level.
- The player should loot the bodies for supplies at the beginning of the level as quickly as they can. They will not be able to return after the supernatural occurrence.
