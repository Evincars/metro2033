# Riga

> "Riga Station was only my first stop on my journey, but the caravan went no further. So, before parting ways, we drank to celebrate our survival. The vodka didn't drown my fear of travelling on to Polis alone. But I was about to find myself with an unexpected and notorious partner."
> — Level description

**Riga** is the second closest [Metro station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Metro_System) to [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) after Alexeyevskaya and the first one [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) travels to. [Riga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_Station_(Location)) is on security lockdown when the player first gets there due to a recent [anomaly](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anomalies) and bandits in the tunnels nearby.

**Objectives:**

- Find a way out of the station and continue to Polis.
- Someone wants to meet me here at Riga station. I should go look for him.
- Get out of Riga with Bourbon.

## Overview

Riga is a very weathered and tarnished settlement. Many of its citizens seem to have little to nothing. It has a small market with a few shops, a bar, and a small open barrack where people most likely sleep. It is, in essence, an underground slum. There are people begging Artyom for MGRs; he can give them out if he wants, but he will only gain the Positive Moral Point once.

Artyom encounters a young boy, willing to take you to see [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) for 1 [MGR](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR). If the player accepts, he leads Artyom to Bourbon, and the player obtains a Positive Moral Point. If he refuses, the player obtains a Negative Moral Point upon sitting down with Bourbon; however, before this, the player can still return to ask the boy to lead him to Bourbon.

[Nikki](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nikki) is also encountered here. She is a prostitute who offers herself to Artyom for one bullet. If the player accepts, she leads him back to her shack, where she tells Artyom to close his eyes. When Artyom opens them, a large man punches him in the face and steals all of his MGR.

## Trivia

- Artyom can hear one of the stories about Sevastopolskaya station, which is located behind a locked door, after a left turn from the child offering to lead you to Bourbon.
- Bourbon tells Artyom that he'll give him his "[AK](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M))" when he reaches his destination. This is the only time the game uses a real-life gun name. In the novels, Artyom agrees to escort Bourbon in exchange for 2 magazines (60 rounds), and enough food to last the journey. The weapon carried by Bourbon is most likely an [AKS-74u](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/AKSU_(AKS-74U)).
- A glitch may occur after the player agrees when Bourbon asks if he is ready. When Bourbon walks away with the player very close behind him, the "talk icon" may appear; when clicked, Bourbon will repeat his question "Are you ready?" This time the player cannot answer, causing a bug that only allows the player to look 45 degrees left and right from where Bourbon is.
- It's advised not to upgrade your Revolver at the store — you find a Revolver with an extended barrel attachment at the end of "Lost Tunnel" anyway.
- Telling Bourbon you are ready to leave will start "[Lost Tunnel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Tunnel)", locking you out of the shops and often leaving you strapped for ammo.
- In Metro 2033 Redux, you can't buy filters, but they are visible in the shop.

## Easter Eggs

There is a guard at Riga who breaks the fourth wall by offering to grant three wishes, if you try to talk to him enough times. There is also a beggar who says "May the force be with you..." (a reference to Star Wars) should you give him a bullet.

## Related Achievements

- **Generous** (10 game points) — Help the poor, a coin for the kid, medicine for the sick. You help everyone you see.
- **Realist** (10 game points) — A coin for a hungry kid? Get a job.
- **Sherlock** (20 game points) — Found all MGR ammo, hidden throughout stations.
