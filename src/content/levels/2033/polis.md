# Polis

> It's hard to describe my feelings at that moment.. exhausted, yes, but joyful - I had made it! Once I delivered Hunter's message, the rescue of my home station could be decided by those stronger and wiser than I. And my task would be over.
>
> — Level description

**Polis** is the largest and most organized settlement in the [Metro system](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro).

It is guarded around the clock by [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers), as well as the city Militia. The overarching power of Polis is the Polis Council. [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) makes his way to Polis to try to convince the Council to send Rangers and soldiers to help save his home settlement, [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)).

## Overview

Artyom first reaches Polis with [Ulman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ulman), after traveling by railcar from the Black Station. The two are greeted by several Rangers guarding the entrance in the dark, using night vision goggles and laser sights. When the Rangers ask who they are, Ulman sarcastically replies, imitating a German speaking broken Russian: "We're Nazis, come to take your station!".

After docking the railcar, Ulman takes Artyom to see [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller). Impressed by the courage Artyom has shown to get to this point, and with Hunter's message, Miller agrees to help Artyom by taking him to the Polis Council, where the fate of Exhibition will be discussed. While Miller is in the next room, organizing the Council meeting, Artyom has a chance to look around. Although he cannot leave the room and explore Polis, Artyom has the option to purchase some new gear (this will also be the last place to buy things, so feel free to spend all of your MGR unless you want to keep more heavy hitting rounds for the next few missions).

After a short period of time, Miller comes back and brings Artyom to the Polis Council. After four hours of debate, the Council is about as productive as real politicians, and declines sending aid to Exhibition. Miller, furious that Polis didn't agree with him, decides to go against the Council's word and take his Rangers to find a way to save Exhibition, and the rest of the Metro.

Miller tells Artyom about several operational missile silos his Rangers have discovered. However, they're controlled from a legendary military bunker called [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)); the location of which was lost after the nuclear disaster. He explains to Artyom that the location to D6 is likely still stored in the secret Archives, deep in the [Moscow Library](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_State_Library).

Miller tasks Artyom with meeting with him at the Library, where they will find the location to D6 and attempt to save Exhibition and the rest of the Metro system.

## Trivia

- This will be the only place to purchase the [Kalash 2012](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_2012) (Silenced, Scoped). The next time you will be able to obtain this gun is from Miller on top of the Tower. You can obtain a standard Kalash 2012 for free in Sparta, however.
- It's best to purchase new weapons, heavy armor, and as much ammo here as possible, as there is only one more settlement ahead: [Sparta](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sparta_Base_(Location)), and everything there is completely free. If you need the special ammo like arrows or ball bearings, now is the time to get them.
- This is the last level in *Metro 2033* where you can trade/buy MGRs.
- It appears that Polis has salvaged old Metro train cars and repaired them to working order, as seen off to Artyom's left when he and Ulman first enter Polis. The trains are supposedly used to transport people and goods between the 4 stations of Polis.
- There are working computers in the reception area (the room with the shops) as well as in Miller's office.
- Even though Polis is the biggest station seen in the game, it's the smallest you interact with since the rest of the time there are just cutscenes after you meet Miller.
- As you enter Polis there is a large tank-like railcart on a parallel railway line, possibly a modified gun turret used to defend against [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) Panzers.
