# Alley

> The council's decision shocked me, but Miller's plans gave me hope. Once more, I climbed up to the bones of Moscow, to search for the secret of D6 and a way to awaken the horde of missiles...
>
> — Level description

**Alley** is a short level that bridges the events that take place in [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Metro_2033_Level)) and the [Library](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Library_(Metro_2033_Level)). Alley takes place on the surface and the only enemies are watchers, [demons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Demons), or the occasional falling debris.

## Overview

Alley is a very short level with two routes you can take, separated by a large gorge. The first has the player staying on the left side, and the other involves crossing the irradiated water via pipes at the beginning of the level. Either way the ending location is the same. If the gas mask was taken off in Polis, [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) should put it on as soon as possible because he will be at the surface as soon as the level starts.

It is recommended that the player moves quickly through this level as to avoid demons and additional watchers, but not moving too fast as to miss any of the pickups in the form of [filters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Filters), MGRs, medkits, various types of ammunition, and a [revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver). Curious players are rewarded for searching unconventional spaces, though no moral points will be awarded. Near the end of the level, Artyom will meet up with [Danila](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Danila) and [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) as they enter the library.

## Trivia

- Inside the second derelict bus in this level, footsteps from a nearby building, sounds of people screaming, and cars passing by can be heard. As in many other locations in the post-war Moscow, even here the [souls](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ghosts_(Phenomenon)) of citizens killed during the nuclear exchange apparently survived.
- If you stayed all the way to the left after exiting the station, then walk through the ice and go into the sewer hole, you'll hear ghostly laughters of a child and a Watcher ghost silhouette will go past you at the front.
