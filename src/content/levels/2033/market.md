# Market

> "I've never seen a Market like this—you could buy almost anything. But, Bourbon owed money to Hanza sentries, and was anxious to leave."
> — Level description

**Market** is a short level in which [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) must buy their way through [Market station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_Station_(Location)), and leave through the [surface](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Surface) in one piece.

**Objectives:**

- Follow Bourbon
- Go to weapons store
- Go up to the surface with Bourbon

## Overview

Market begins with the uncomfortably private negotiations Bourbon has with "[Boss](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Boss)". When Bourbon comes back after an apparent bribe, the two are admitted into the station. Apparently under some sort of time pressure, Bourbon decides to temporarily part from Artyom and asks him to go buy himself some more air [filters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Filters). The player can spend as much time as they want, investigating the [currency exchange](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exchange_Kiosk) and [weapon vendor](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_Vendors) with no trouble, but must eventually take a look at the filters.

Whether or not they decide to buy more filters, the player can return to Bourbon, who has recently paid off some debts owed to [Mike](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mike). As they make their way to the airlock, [Igor](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Igor) approaches Bourbon, who seemingly needs to pay yet another bribe. Instead of the passing of bullets, however, the two shake hands, as Igor was supposedly joking about the bribe. Igor then orders his men to open the airlock to the surface.

Artyom and Bourbon promptly exit through to the frozen remains of Moscow as [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) guards take notice of Bourbon's escape. Making it out just in time, the door closes and the two are free.

## Trivia

- Even though Bourbon says "you have around 5 minutes", you can actually spend as much time as you want in the station. You will only have to leave when you meet up with him again and follow him to the station's front gate.
- One of the conversations you can hear is a reference to something Bourbon told Artyom in Chapter 5 of the [Metro 2033 novel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)), about a convoy that ties itself with rope in the fog, and then the last guy disappears from the rearguard, leaving an arm behind tied to the rope.
- In the beginning, two guards have a conversation that is directly taken from the novel Metro 2033, that being the guard not shooting first even if it was a rat, for protocol.
- In [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow), this station is named Prospekt Mira (Kaluzhsko-Rizhskaya Line).
- The pipe in the bar can be smoked, and for a short time the player's sight and hearing will be distorted and the controls slow until Artyom becomes sober.
- You can drink the vodka in the bar for free; the hangover effect will last for a short period. In Redux, you have to pay 5 [MGR](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR)'s to drink.
- You can get hurt and killed by the rotating gate if you stand in it while an NPC passes through. It's also possible that if you stand in the fire of Igor's guard post, you will die too, making this the only level that the player perishes without combat.
- At the end of the station near the Hanza border, you can hear two men talking about the people that got lost with no trail in the tunnels between Suharevskaya, Turgenevskaya, and Kitay-Gorod, and mentioning the [stalkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers) who got hypnotized by the stars above the [Kremlin](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kremlin). These are references to the novel.
- It's ironic how Artyom mentions that Market is the biggest trading station he has ever seen, yet the player can only buy some weapons ([Revolvers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver), [Automatic Shotgun](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Automatic_Shotgun_(Uboinik)) and a [Tihar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) specifically), but no [armor](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armor), [Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M)), [Kalash 2012](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_2012), etc.
