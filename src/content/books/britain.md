# Britain

***Britain*** (Russian: **Британия**) is a novel that is part of the [Universe of Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Universe_of_Metro_2033_(Book_Series)), a long-running series of short stories, novellas and novels written by different authors, all set in the world of [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Series). *Britain*, written by English author Grant McMaster, was originally published in September of 2011. Although the book was originally written in English, *Britain* is only available in Russian and has not been translated into any other European languages.

## Synopsis
The novel is set in the United Kingdom. The main character is [Euan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Euan) , who lives in the Glasgow subway with his wife and children. One day his family along with other station residents are kidnaped by slave traders. Ewen decides to hunt down these slavers while seting out through the British lands devastated by the Last War and a nuclear winter.

## Trivia

- The first novel in the [Universe of Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Universe_of_Metro_2033_(Book_Series)) to take place in Western Europe.
- There is a comic book *Britain: Prologue*, written by Grant McMaster and drawn by Ben Hollis, based on the corresponding part of the book.
