# To See the Sun

***To See the Sun*** (Russian: **Увидеть солнце**) is a novel that is part of the [Universe of Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Universe_of_Metro_2033_(Book_Series)), a long-running series of short stories, novellas, and novels written by different authors, all set in the world of [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Series). *To See the Sun*, written by Russian author Sergey Moskvin, was originally published in January of 2011. Although there is currently no English version of the book, *To See the Sun* has been translated into one other European language - German. The novel was translated to German by Matthias Dondl and published in Germany in January of 2015.

The novel takes place in [Novosibirsk](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk) and the [Novosibirsk Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk_Metro).

## Synopsis
Something terrible breaks free and hangs over the city of Novosibirsk, becoming a threat not just to the city itself, but to the entire planet. The protagonist of the story, Sergei Kasarin of the Novosibirsk Metro, has to take advantage of - as the name of the book tells - the only chance that humanity has left to see the sun again.

As Kasarin and his fellow travellers manage to establish, the threat takes the form of genetically modified black mold; this strange substance absorbs, kills, and recycles any living organisms it comes into contact with. The main characters of the book try to prevent the complete death of Novosibirsk as a result of the expanding influence of this deadly mold.

## Trivia
- *To See the Sun* was the first novel in the [Universe of Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Universe_of_Metro_2033_(Book_Series)) series that takes place in Siberia.
- It was also the first book in the series whose cover depicts a woman.
