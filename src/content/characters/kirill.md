# Kirill

> "Mutants don't knock first, open the damn gate!" — Kirill

Kirill (Russian: Кирилл) is a resident of [Exhibition Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)), and a soldier in the [VDNKh Commonwealth](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/VDNKh_Commonwealth).

## Overview

Kirill appears to be a higher ranking guard. He's seen as one of the three guards at the main entrance to the station. [Alex](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alex) tells him to take his men and defend the tunnels, as he, along with [Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Ranger)) and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom), would defend the main doors. He is seen alive after the [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones) attack on the defense post.

## In the Novel

In the [novel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)), Kirill is a character who is living at VDNKh with Artyom. When Artyom begins his journey Kirill accompanies him on the rail cart. Near the end of the novel when Artyom revisits his home station, he greets Kirill, who survived the relentless attacks of the Dark Ones whilst Artyom was journeying to [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)).
