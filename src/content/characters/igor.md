# Igor

Igor is the head of a security checkpoint on a par with [Boss](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Boss) at the Prospect Mira radial station and works directly under [Mike](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mike_(Character)).

## Overview

Igor is briefly seen on the [Market](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_(Level)) level. He attempts to extort additional payment from [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon), but quickly gives up and lets him and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) pass the gate.

## Trivia

- For an unknown reason his patronym changes from dub to dub. He is referred to as Vladimirovich in English and as Sergeevich in Russian.
- He is the only character to wear a helmet from the Alpha-version of Metro 2033.
