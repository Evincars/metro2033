## Overview

Andrei Ivanovich Yermak (Russian: Андрей Иванович Ермак) is one of [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow)'s few surviving metro personnel who serves as the helmsman of the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) armored train. Before the war, Yermak worked as a metro engineer on the Ring Line, a job he found boring. He often dreamed of quitting to work on a real railroad and see Russia, much to the displeasure of his wife Tatiana. When the bombs fell, his wife and son were killed, but his daughter survived with him as they were in the Metro at the time. Yermak eventually signed up to run surface trains for [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza), which is how he came to be at the railway depot where [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) were held captive.

## Role in Metro Exodus

Yermak plays a pivotal role at the very beginning of Metro Exodus. He helps Artyom rescue Anna from the Hanza base at the railway depot. During their escape, the trio destroys the radio jammer console, revealing that the outside world is not as dead as Moscow's leadership had claimed. Together with Artyom and Anna, Yermak hijacks the Hanza steam locomotive that becomes the Aurora. As the train's helmsman, he is responsible for operating and driving the Aurora throughout the crew's journey across Russia. The oldest member of the crew, Yermak brings decades of railway experience that proves essential for navigating the deteriorating rail infrastructure of post-apocalyptic Russia. He is one of the non-determinant crew members who remains with the team throughout the entire journey to [Lake Baikal](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lake_Baikal).
