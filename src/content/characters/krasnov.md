# Krasnov

Captain Aleksandr Krasnov (Russian: Александр Краснов) is a [Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Rangers) working at [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)).

## Overview

Krasnov only has a small amount of dialogue in the game and only appears once. He speaks to [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Ulman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ulman) when they reach Polis. He only asks where Artyom is from, but then Ulman interrupts, explaining the situation that [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) is facing.
