# Sergey Sergeyevich

Sergey Sergeyevich (Russian: Сергей Сергеевич) is a resident of [Armory Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_Station_(Location)) and an officer of the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line).

## Overview

Sergey is overheard in the level "[Armory](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_(Metro_2033_Level))", in which [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) hears him accepting a child into his fighting force. A communist soldier brings the child forward, claiming that he is a "neighborhood kid", and embraces their "communist ideals". Sergey issues him a uniform and rifle.
