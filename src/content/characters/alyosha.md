## Overview

Alyosha (Russian: Алёша) is a [Spartan Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers) who specializes as a scout aboard the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) armored train. Before joining the Rangers, Alyosha spent several years working as a guard in [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)), but eventually left in search of adventure. Few in the Order can match his expertise when it comes to survival in the irradiated Russian wilderness. He is one of the crew members who joined [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom), [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna), and [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) on the journey eastward after the [Battle for D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_for_D6).

## Role in Metro Exodus

Alyosha serves as one of the Aurora crew's scouts throughout the journey across Russia. He plays a particularly significant role during [The Taiga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Taiga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) chapter, where he and Artyom are sent to explore the forested region around a dam and hydroelectric power station. During this mission, he is captured by the [Children of the Forest](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Children_of_the_Forest) but is helped to escape by [Olga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Olga). Alyosha develops a romantic interest in Olga during this chapter. Depending on the player's actions -- whether Artyom avoids killing the Children of the Forest members -- Alyosha either returns to the Aurora unharmed or is shot in the spine by the Children, which leaves him paralyzed from the waist down. His fate is one of the player-determinant outcomes that affects the game's ending.
