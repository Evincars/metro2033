# Boss

"Boss" is a [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) Guard stationed at [Market Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_Station_(Location)), and is briefly seen at the start of the level "[Market](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_(Level))".

## Overview

Boss appears to be the head of a security team at the radial Prospect Mira station checkpoint. His dialogue with [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) implies that they've met before on less than pleasant occasions. Boss eagerly agrees to accept a bribe and lets the duo enter the station without any fuss.

## Trivia

- Oddly enough during the first encounter with Boss, he will pulse with light identifying him as an item or objective.
- The name of "Boss" has not been confirmed to be the character's real name, it is simply what Bourbon refers to him as.
