# Nikolay

> "The Dark Ones will kill us all!" — Nikolay

Nikolay (Russian: Николай) is a resident of [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) station and is briefly seen in the level "[Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Level))".

## Overview

Nikolay is very briefly seen in the level "Hunter". He appears to be a doctor of some kind, and speaks to [Alex](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alex) about the [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones). He can be found at the bottom of the stairs just after Alex wakes [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and they leave his room. He appears to be afraid of the dark ones and thinks they will kill everyone.
