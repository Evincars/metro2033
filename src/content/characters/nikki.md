# Nikki

Nikki (also called Snezhana or Снежана in the Russian version) is a con-artist in [Rizhskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_Station_(Location)), encountered in the [Riga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_(Metro_2033_Level)) level of [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)).

## Overview

Nikki is first encountered when [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) is drinking vodka with [Eugine](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Eugine) and [Boris](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Boris_(Exhibition)), sitting at a table to the right, then walking past them. She can be found later at the end of the black street, in the area where there is a doll on the floor near a fire drum. She only shows up after Artyom talks to [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) and answered "I'm not ready" to his question.

When Nikki asks for a [bullet](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/5.45x39mm_(Disambiguation)), Artyom has the choice to pay. If Artyom does, she leads him to her room, then askes him to close his eyes and wait. Artyom does so, but opens them just in time to see a big man in front of him, who headbutts him, knocking him out. Artyom later wakes up with another man standing by him, chastising him for going with Nikki, who stole all his [MGRs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Grade_Rounds).

## Nikki's Conversation

A transcript of the conversation between Nikki and Artyom. Note that, although the basic phrases are similar, they may differ slightly from player to player:

Nikki: "Hey, baby. You like what you see?" (Artyom goes to her) "Is that a gun in your pocket? Or are you just happy to see me?" (Artyom offers a 5.45mm bullet) "Ok, come with me, sweetie." (Artyom follows her) "Prepare for a big surprise... big boy." (Artyom enters her room) "Now close your eyes and wait for a big suprise!"

Artyom covers his eyes, just seeing Nikki bending over. He opens his eyes, but the lights have been turned off. A tall man is standing in front of Artyom, and headbutts him in the face. The screen turns black and Artyom wakes up, with a man standing over him.

Unknown Man: "You dumbass! Everyone knows not to go with Nikki. Her ass really packs a punch, no? Ha-ha-ha! Looks like your pockets were emptied. That's what you get."

## Trivia

- If Artyom goes back to Nikki's room, nothing can be found. Nikki, the big man, and the MGRs are never seen again.
- Nikki is the only female character in Metro 2033 who Artyom can really interact with, and the only female NPC with a unique model.
- In [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux), Nikki's attire was changed, and her character model was recycled from the [stripper](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stripper) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).
