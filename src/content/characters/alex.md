# Alex

> "Even when you don't see them - the Dark Ones are there. Fear - that's their weapon, that's what made the nosalises run through the tunnel like rats. The Dark Ones are not simple mutants - they are Homo Novus, the next step in evolution. You've heard about 'survival of the fittest'? Guess what? We lost." — Alex to Hunter after defending the hospital from nosalises

Alexei "Alex" Sukhoi (Russian: Алексей Сухой) is [Artyom's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) adoptive father and mentor and a high ranking official in the [VDNKh Commonwealth](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/VDNKh_Commonwealth) and head of [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) by 2033.

## Background

Alex is responsible for saving Artyom during the nuclear war, as stated in the game; "I was just a boy when they saved me". In the novel, however, while Artyom is still a baby, his [mother](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom%27s_Mother) hands him over to Sukhoi while their [home station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Timiryazevskaya) is being attacked by a horde of rats. Sukhoi and some others manage to escape via [railcar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Railcar), but everyone else still in the station was killed.

Despite this, Artyom doesn't refer to Alex/Sukhoi as "father" but as "uncle". In the novel Sukhoi is not married or in any relationship, to which Artyom considers him "the old metro wolf". It may be because of this that he is depicted as getting nervous upon seeing a mother and a child, since he never got married or had kids himself. Instead he wanted himself to be a good father figure for Artyom, who he thought should have the future he could never have. At the end of the novel, Sukhoi was in charge of the survivors at VDNKh.

## Appearances

### Metro 2033

Alex only makes a few brief appearances in the [game](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Videogame)); Artyom fights alongside him and [Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Ranger)) against a horde of [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) at the gates of Exhibition Station. Also, there is an optional encounter in his office before Artyom leaves the station, nearing the end of the level "[Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Metro_2033_Level))". Talking to him the second time before leaving Exhibition awards positive [morality points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Point).

He is protective of Artyom and is unhappy with Artyom leaving Exhibition to go to [Riga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_Station_(Location)). As one of the leaders of Exhibition, it is his responsibility to ensure that the station survives. How he came to be one of these authorities is unknown, but it's generally implied that Alex was elected as he is popular among most of Exhibition's citizens.

### Metro 2035

Alex also has a minor role in [Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035). Near the end of the story, he helps Artyom and [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_Miller) escape [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) while 2 men from Hanza threaten him to turn them over.

## Trivia

- Alex's face was modeled after Anatoliy Koba, a [4A Games](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/4A_Games) employee.
- Alex may have well been in the Russian military or in the Rangers himself, considering his friendship with Hunter.
- Sukhoi (Сухой) literally translates to "dry" in Russian. Much like Hunter, this is a nickname.
