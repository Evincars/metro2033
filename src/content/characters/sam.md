## Overview

Lance Corporal Samuel "Sam" Taylor (Russian: Сэмюел "Сэм" Тэйлор) is a former [United States Marine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Marine_Corps) who serves as [Colonel Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller)'s personal bodyguard and a member of [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s crew aboard the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) armored train. Born in San Diego, California, Sam was stationed at the US Embassy in Moscow as part of a Marine Security Guard detachment when the nuclear war began. Stranded in Russia, he eventually found his way to the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Metro_System) and joined the [Spartan Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers). He first appears in [Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035) and returns in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Role in Metro Exodus

Sam serves as a reliable and optimistic member of the Aurora crew throughout the journey. His American heritage gives him a unique perspective among the predominantly Russian crew, and his hope of one day returning to the United States is a recurring theme. He assists in several key missions, including helping to rescue [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) from the gas-filled bunker at the [Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Volga). Sam is also the protagonist of the [Sam's Story](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam%27s_Story) DLC, which takes place after the events of Metro Exodus. In this expansion, Sam travels to the ruins of [Vladivostok](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vladivostok) in hopes of finding a way across the Pacific Ocean to reunite with his father in the United States.
