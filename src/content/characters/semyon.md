# Semyon

Semyon (Russian: Семён), also called Simon, is a resident of [Market Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_Station_(Location)), and is briefly seen in the level "[Lost Catacombs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Catacombs_(Level))".

## Overview

Semyon is briefly seen in the level "Lost Catacombs", in which he is guarding the main gate to Market Station. When [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) arrive, [Mikhalych](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mikhalych), a fellow guard, calls to Semyon to start up the cart. Moments later, a cart rolls out with a heavy machine gun and begins firing at the [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) attacking Bourbon and Artyom. The player should not fire at Semyon and the car else they will fire back, which will inevitably result in Bourbon's death.
