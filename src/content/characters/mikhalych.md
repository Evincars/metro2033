# Mikhalych

Mikhalych (Russian: Михалыч) is a resident of [Market Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_Station_(Location)), member of [Hansa](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hansa) Guards, and is briefly seen in the level "[Lost Catacombs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Catacombs_(Level))".

## Overview

Mikhalych is briefly seen in the level "Lost Catacombs", in which he is guarding the main gate to Market Station. When [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) arrive, Mikhalych calls to [Semyon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Semyon), another guard, to start up the cart. Moments later, a cart rolls out with a heavy machine gun and begins firing at the [Nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) attacking Bourbon and Artyom. After the Nosalises have been dealt with, Mikhalych tells Bourbon to wait for his return before he and Semyon ride out into the tunnels.

## Trivia

- "Михалыч" is a highly personal way to address someone, because it's a form of the patronym Mikhailovich (son of Mikhail), normally used among highly familiar people in a very unofficial circle.
- It's unknown exactly what Bourbon did to Mikhalych, but it must have been serious because Bourbon said "We stepped in shit now!" when he saw him.
