## Overview

Igor Dukov (Russian: Игорь Князев), better known as Duke (Russian: Князь), is a young [Spartan Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers) who joins [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s crew aboard the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) armored train. One of the few survivors of the [Battle for D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_for_D6), Duke is among the youngest members of the crew and serves as a scout. Eager and enthusiastic, he is always the first to volunteer for any mission, no matter the danger. He first appears in [Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035) and returns in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Role in Metro Exodus

Duke serves as a scout aboard the Aurora during the crew's journey eastward across post-apocalyptic Russia. His youthful enthusiasm and bravery make him a valuable asset to the team, though his eagerness sometimes puts him in danger. During the [Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Volga) chapter, Duke plays a significant role, and his fate is one of the player-determinant outcomes in the game. Depending on [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s actions -- whether he uses stealth or violence against the local [Church of the Water Tsar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Water_Tsar) cultists -- Duke either survives or is killed during the crew's escape from the region. His survival or death directly impacts the game's ending, as he is one of the crew members whose fate determines whether Artyom receives enough blood donations to survive his radiation poisoning. Duke also has a girlfriend named Svetlana waiting for him back in Moscow.
