# Peter

Peter is one of the guards of [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)).

## Overview

Peter makes a very brief appearance at the beginning of the level "[Chase](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chase_(Level))", while [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) is traveling on a [rail-car](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Railcar) between Exhibition and [Riga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_Station_(Location)). He informs Artyom and the rest of the caravan that the tunnel ahead has collapsed, and that they would have to take the service tunnel to Riga, which is a dangerous unguarded route in addition to being inhabited by [ghosts](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ghosts_(Phenomenon)).
