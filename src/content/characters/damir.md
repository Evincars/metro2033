## Overview

Damir (Russian: Дамир) is a [Spartan Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers) who serves as the Order's medic aboard the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) armored train. Born in [Kazakhstan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kazakhstan) before the war, he is one of the few non-ethnically Slavic members of the Rangers, being half-Russian and half-Kazakh. Despite sometimes feeling like an outsider because of his heritage, Damir remains fiercely loyal to [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s group. As the crew's medic, he is deeply empathetic and ensures that everyone aboard receives the medical attention they need.

## Role in Metro Exodus

Damir plays a supporting role throughout the Aurora's journey, tending to the crew's injuries and health needs. His heritage becomes particularly significant during [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) chapter, where the crew encounters a region near his ancestral homeland. Here, Damir connects with the local population who are enslaved by the [Munai-bailer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Munai-bailer) and their leader, [the Baron](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Baron). Depending on [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s actions in the Caspian -- whether he helps the enslaved locals and [Giul](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Giul)'s resistance or ignores their plight -- Damir either stays with the crew or chooses to remain behind to help his people. His decision is one of the player-determinant outcomes that affects the game's ending. He also assists Artyom and [Sam](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam) in rescuing [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) from the poisonous gas-filled bunker at the Volga.
