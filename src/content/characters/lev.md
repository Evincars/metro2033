# Lev

Lev (Russian: Лев) is a resident of [Armory Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_Station_(Location)) and a soldier of the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line). He is briefly heard in the level "[Front Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Front_Line_(Level))".

## Overview

Lev is overheard in the level "Front Line", having a conversation with his comrades while Artyom is hiding under the train. He asks why he was given a cyanide capsule and, upon hearing that he is to use it if he is captured to end his own life, Lev exclaims that suicide is an affront to the God of his faith, and that he wouldn't be able to go through with the action.
