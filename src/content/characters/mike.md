# Mike

Mike is a corrupt [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) guard living in [Market Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_Station_(Location)).

## Overview

If one doesn't have proper documents or travels illegally, he is the person to talk to. [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) arranges a free leave from the Market station with him, for some [MGR](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR) of course. What happened between Bourbon, Mike and the other Hanza guards is a mystery. He is not to be confused with [Mikolavich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mikolavich_(Character)); another similarly named guard at the same station.
