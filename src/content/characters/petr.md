# Petr

> "No visitors!" — Petr

Petr (alternatively Pyotr, Russian: Пётр) is a resident of [Exhibition station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)), and is briefly seen in the level "[Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Level))".

## Overview

Petr is very briefly seen in the level Hunter, in which he is guarding the door to the hospital. When [Alex](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alex) and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) knock on the door, he attempts to send them away. However, Alex persists and Petr opens up the hatch. Once he realizes who it is, he lets both men through.

He is most likely based off of Pyotr Andrevich, one of the head guards at VNDKH in the novel.
