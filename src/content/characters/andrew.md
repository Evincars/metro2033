# Andrew

> "Your seat won't be first class, but you don't have to pay the fare with your own blood either."
> — Andrew

Andrew (Russian: Андрей, pronounced Andryey) "The Blacksmith", "Smithy", or "The Smith" is first encountered at the [Armory](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_(Level)). He saves [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s life by hiding him from some [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) soldiers.

## Overview

Andrew, nicknamed "Andrew the Blacksmith", is as his name suggests, a talented Blacksmith. Not much is known about his background except that he has never liked the [Communists](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line), and often acts out on these feelings. He lived in the [Smithy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_(Level)) for a long time, helping [refugees](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Refugees) escape the Red Line. While living there, he built a reputation as being one of the most skilled engineers in the whole Metro. Andrew has mastered skills from Machining, to Auto-Body. From creating the [Abzats](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Automatic_Shotgun_(Abzats)), to building on the [Regina](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Regina_(Vehicle)), he is an all around Technician.

In 2034, however, he abandons the [Smithy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_Station_(Location)) as result of the Reds cracking down hard on spies. He then moves to a small house in the tunnel connecting the Red Line to [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Location)). He still helps people escape the Communists from here though.

## Appearances

### Metro 2033

Before Artyom leaves [Cursed Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Station_(Location)), [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)) tasks him with finding Andrew within the Armory. However, it is Andrew who finds him first after saving Artyom's life. After Artyom has finished his business within the Armory, he can then meet up with Andrew at the end of the station. Andrew smuggles Artyom out of the Armory and into the [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level)). After this point, Andrew is no longer heard from or seen again.

### Metro: Last Light

Andrew makes another appearance in Metro: Last Light, as a runaway from the Reds, and helps smuggle refugees out of the Red Line. Artyom stumbles across Andrew's home in the tunnels outside Revolution Square after he escapes the station, where Andrew nearly kills Artyom with a [Gatling](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gatling) before he recognizes him. He assists Artyom by unveiling a railcar, [Regina](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Regina_(Vehicle)), which he uses through most of the tunnels.

### Metro Exodus

It's revealed in a journal entry written by Artyom under Miller that Andrew installed [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller)'s prosthetic legs after he lost his during the [Battle for D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_for_D6).

## Trivia

- Andrew has a poster of the book [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)).
- Andrew is the creator of the [Abzats Heavy Automatic Shotgun](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Automatic_Shotgun).
- At Andrew's home in Metro: Last Light, the original soundtrack from Metro 2033 can be heard.
- He is the only NPC in the main game to carry the Gatling. [Hans](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hans), a heavy trooper in the [Heavy Squad](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Squad) also carries one. The [Hellbreath](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hellbreath_(Volt_Driver)) Hans uses in "[Heavy Squad](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Squad_(Faction_Pack_DLC_Level))" is presumably stolen from him.
- In both games, he is also known as Andrew the Blacksmith, or sometimes Smithy.
- His alternative name is spelled Andrei.
- In Metro 2033, Andrew has a significant amount of hair, despite the hood he wears hiding most of it. However, in Metro: Last Light, he seems to have become fully bald. This could signify the amount of time between 2033 and its sequel, or possibly just be an oversight during development.
- In game, he seems to mention "first class" a few times while describing transport vehicles. It's implied that he was an engineer before the Great War. It's very likely that he has military background due to his extensive knowledge of gunsmithing.
- While never appearing in Metro Exodus, he is mentioned in a journal entry under Miller as the one who made his prosthetics.
