## Overview

The Bigun is a homemade semi-automatic, breachloading, revolving pepper-box shotgun, made entirely from old bicycle parts, hence its name. The only available [attachment](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Attachments) is the laser sight (useless as it points left and upwards instead of in the middle of the screen). Due to programming constraints, [4A](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/4A) have said that they were unable to add it to the existing campaign and previous DLC. It isn't dissimilar to the [Uboinik](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Uboinik_(Shambler)), both being six-round revolving shotguns; however, it fires faster than the Uboinik, but seems to have less range and damage per shot (because it lacks any other damage and range-increasing modifiers such as a long barrel)

In the Developer Pack, it is found in the [level of the same name](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Developer_Pack_(Level)) for use in the [Shooting Gallery](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shooting_Gallery) and [AI Arena](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/AI_Arena). Its use in the shooting gallery is somewhat diminished (as with every other shotgun) as most targets in the challenges are too far away to shoot effectively, though it can be useful in fending off [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/mutants) in the AI Arena, but a [Saiga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Saiga-12) may be a better choice.

It is also featured in the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack) DLC, in the [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Chronicles_Pack_Level)) level, although it is only usable when [Ulman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ulman) has a vision of a young [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)) in [Polyanka](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polyanka), and the player controls the latter. However, its use is somewhat diminished given the short section of the level, the ammunition constraints and the fact that is is somewhat weaker than the Shambler that can be picked up from your fallen companion. It also lacks any sights (aside from a laser sight), whereas the Shambler has a reflex sight. But it is a unique weapon that fits perfectly with Khan's strange nature.

## Upgrades and Customisation

Bigun is one the least customizable weapons in the game, as it has only one [attachment](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/attachment) available, which is a laser sight.

## Trivia

- The gun is based on the winning design from [a contest](http://www.gameplanet.co.nz/pc/news/g51aea2c410d15/Winner-of-Gameplanets-Metro-Last-Light-PC-competition-announced/) held on [Gameplanet](http://www.gameplanet.com.au/).
- The Bigun, being fashioned out of bicycle parts, has a seatpost and seat as a stock, a bicycle main gear as the holder for the firing pins, a smaller chain guide as the hammer, frame tubes as the barrels, a lower frame assembly as the grip/forearm and a handlebar with a handbrake as the trigger.
- When idle, the character will ring the bell on the side of the weapon.
- This is the one of the three multi-barrel weapons in game, the others being the [Medved](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Medved) and [Gatling](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gatling). All are available in *Metro: Last Light* and the Redux version.
- When analysed and compared with all the other weapons in *Metro: Last Light*, it seems likely that the weapon would retail for around 60 [MGR](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR), as it boasts improved performance over the [Duplet](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duplet), but is slightly worse than the [Shambler](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shambler).
  - It is the perfect halfway point between the slow but powerful Duplet and the automatic Shambler, capable of performing well against any mutants from close range. At long range, however, it's less practical than other shotguns when the attachments system is factored in.

## Bugs

- In *[Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux)* and *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)*, if the Bigun is equipped with a laser sight, the laser dot will be misaligned, pointing a little left and up from the centre of the screen. This makes the attachment a fairly useless purchase, given the clear iron sights present on the weapon.
