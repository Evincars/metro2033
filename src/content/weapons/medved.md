The **Medved** (Russian: **Медвед**, a deliberate misspelling of the word meaning "bear") is a powerful Metro-made triple-barreled  grenade launcher that can fire up to three contact detonated [grenades](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Grenade) in quick succession, making it ideal for taking out light armour or groups of soldiers.

## Overview

The Medved was originally set to appear in the *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_(Videogame))* main campaign as an under-barrel attachment for the [Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M)). However, it isn't included in the final campaign. It's featured in [two](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faction_Pack) of [Last Light's DLCs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Developer_Pack_(DLC)), first seen in use by the [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Reich) Heavy, [Hans](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hans). This version has three barrels, unlike the underbarrel's single barrel. It consists of three pieces of pipe with a spring mechanism to launch grenades and a battery that created an electricity arc to light the fuse. The Medved has no proper sighting system or aim assist, so the player may have to experiment to determine the trajectory of the grenades.

## Upgrades and Customization

No [attachments](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/attachments) are available for this weapon. This trait is shared with the [flamethrower](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/flamethrower).

## Obtainment

- in Metro 2033 Redux, the underbarrel version of the Medved was originally going to be sold by the merchant in the [Defense](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Child_(Metro_2033_Level)) level, but it is no longer possible without actually having the launcher first, to obtain the launcher itself, one would need to use cheats to noclip his way into an unreachable room with 2 Red soldiers, one of them will be carrying a [Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M)) with the Medved underbarrel grenade launcher attached to it, knocking him out will make him drop the weapon, and then it will be able to be picked up, however, the gun cannot be modified with any other attachements in any merchant that is not the one found in the Defense level, its only possible to modify it further with that specific merchant, if the gun is modified at any other merchant, then the underbarrel grenade launcher dissapears completely, a guide to do this can be found [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VPXpMyfPicE).
- The Medved can be obtained during the course of the [Heavy Squad](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Squad) mission, where Hans' comrade [Diesel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Diesel) will give him the weapon, seeing as he himself preferred to use his rifle instead. It's also available in the [Developer Pack DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Developer_Pack_DLC), as are all other weapons in the game.
  - Hans can carry up to 15 grenades for his Medved, though he cannot throw any by hand. This is different from the Medved found in the Developer Pack, as the iteration available in that mission shoots regular grenades from the player's grenade slot, and the player can't carry more than 5 grenades.

## Trivia

- "Medved" ("медвед") is misprinted word "Medved'" ("медведь") - bear. It is part of a russian internet meme ["preved"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preved). Another weapon whose name refers to this meme is the [Preved](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Preved).
- An electric sound can be heard while launching the grenade, possibly an indication that the grenade is armed.
- It is unknown what makes these grenades contact detonated, as they appear visually the same as the normal thrown variety. It's possible they are made from a breakable container, like the incendiary grenades.
  - This is confused further by Redux, where it's shown to use standard grenades that when thrown have their normal 2-3 second fuse, if not used in the Medved.
- The Medved is loosely based on the GP-30 and GP-25 underbarrel grenade launchers in terms of operation and loading, while its stock was taken wholesale from a [Dragunov sniper rifle](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/wikipedia:Dragunov_sniper_rifle).
- In *[Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux)*, the original underbarrel attachment is seen on a guard's [Saiga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Saiga-12).
- If left idle, the character will try to start a motor of some sort that is on the Medved (probably the device that arms the grenades when they are shot), sees that he failed, then flick at the motor (which causes it to abruptly start), and then put his hand back on the gun.
- In Kshatriya, an under-barrel grenade launcher of some sort can be seen on the kalash of one of the twins, along with a bayonet. They are not used, nor can they be obtained.
