The **Stallion** (Russian: **Стэллион**) is a semi-automatic pistol (machine pistol with the appropriate attachments) that was imported into Russia by [Tom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tom)'s company before [World War III](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_III). It is an American-made single-action semi-automatic (fully automatic with the appropriate attachment)  pistol that has been heavily modified to suit the needs of a survivor in the ruined city of [Vladivostok](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vladivostok).

## Overview
A pre-War American handgun produced by the New Richmond Arsenal in Virginia that originally used the rare (outside of the USA) .45 ACP cartridge, the Stallion and weapons like it were an uncommon sight in [Russia](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Russia) even before [World War III](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_III).

These weapons were imported into Russia by an American gun company, with Tom acting as the company's local representative. Together with [Klim](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Klim), who was hired locally to provide security for the warehouses, Tom survived the War and took advantage of the large stockpile of firearms now at his disposal to gain a position of power in [Vladivostok](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vladivostok).

They have since been rechambered for the [.44 Magnum](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/.44_Magnum) cartridges common amongst [Tom's Army](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tom's_Army), Russian survivors, and have become a relatively common sight in Vladivostok. [Sam](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam) is provided with a Stallion and a [Sammy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sammy) by [Danila](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Danila_(Vladivostok)), Tom's personal gunsmith, after he agrees to track down the [Captain](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Captain_(Vladivostok)) for Tom and his men.

## Variants and Customization
The Stallion has the five standard upgrade slots most weapons have, with three options available for each one of them. Unlike other handguns, however, it can not be converted into a carbine/rifle, It's possible to turn it into a fully automatic machine pistol with high capacity drum magazine, which is devastating at close range.

## Trivia
- The Stallion is an explicit reference to the Colt logo.
- The coin pressed into the Stallion's light grip is a 1943 [Mercury dime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury%20dime).
- It is based on the Colt M1911 pistol in M1911A1 variant, one of the most successful 20th century pistols designed by [John Browning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Browning).
- The bottom part of the Stallion's barrel has a rail system to mount gadgets similar to 21st century variants of the M1911 with an underbarrel picatinny rail such as the M45A1 CQBP version MEU(SOC) pistols.
- It might be possible it's inspired by the [LAR Grizzly Win Mag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LAR_Grizzly_Win_Mag), a modified M1911 pistol conceived, invented, designed, engineered and developed in the 1980s by the sole inventor, Perry Arnett, who licensed his patent for an interchangeable caliber semi-automatic pistol to L.A.R. Manufacturing Inc. Perry Arnett's designs were initially flawed and were improved upon by Heinz Augat (former owner and founder of L.A.R. Manufacturing Inc.). As the LAR Grizzly Win Mags can be chambered with different calibers such as the .44 Magnum.
- Despite the modifications descriptions, the Stallion's magazine capacity is 7, 15, and 28 with each respective mod. The figure given in their description seems to assume that there is a round already chambered, as similar to the [Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M)) and [Bastard](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bastard) if a Stallion is reloaded before the magazine is completely emptied the chambered round will not be removed.
