# Metro Redux

**Metro Redux** is a post-apocalyptic first-person shooter, and a two-part remaster of *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game))* and *[Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light)* for the PS4 and Xbox One consoles. It was developed by [4A Games](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/4A_Games) and published by [Deep Sliver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Koch_Media). The enhanced versions of both games can be bought together as a bundle or separately. This was also Metro 2033's debut on a PlayStation console. The bundle later came out on the Nintendo Switch as well.

## Overview
*Metro Redux* features remakes of both games in an improved [4A Engine](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/4A_Engine). While *[Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux)* includes general improvements, *[Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux)* has been completely remade in the *Last Light* engine and updated to include the same features as *Last Light*. *Metro Redux* has been released for the PS4 and the Xbox One, as well as PC, in August 2014. Almost six years later, it was made available on the Switch in February 2020.

## Major Changes
- [Ranger Mode](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties%23Ranger_Difficulties_(DLC)) is included in both games.
- Support for Linux and SteamOS on the PC.
- Nvidia Physx effects have been re-built with the 3.X SDK.
- More realistic weapon handling; both games track whether or not there is still a round in the chamber when reloading.
- Both games now have an added "safe and key" mechanic, like in "[Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Chronicles_Pack_DLC_Level))" from the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack). This also led to some small secret areas that were added, in which some of these safes or keys can be found.
- *Metro 2033* has been rebuilt on the same engine used to create *Last Light*. This means that both games now look the same, handle the same and have the same in-game mechanics and additions that were made to *Last Light* e.g. the mask wipe, new UI, takedowns, etc.
- Each title can be played in one of two difficulty modes: Survival or Spartan. Survival is modelled after *Metro 2033*, having more limited supplies and ammunition, lower reload speeds and tougher enemy AI. Spartan makes the game more action-oriented and gives players more resources, in the style of *Last Light*.
- In *Metro 2033 Redux*, several levels have been combined into very long single levels with no loading screens in between them.

## Reception
*Metro Redux* received generally favorable reviews, scoring 84/100 on Metacritic for the PC and Xbox One versions, and 83/100 on PS4. Reviewers praised the improved graphics and gameplay for both games, especially for *Metro 2033* in which most reviewers agreed got the better graphical and gameplay treatment than the less dated *Last Light*. The community on *Slant* was impressed by a very well-done, immersive exploration aspect and by an intense underground combat.
