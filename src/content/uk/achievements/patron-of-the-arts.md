**Patron of the Arts** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)* and *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)* that can be obtained in the level "[Bolshoi](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bolshoi)".

## Огляд

This achievement is awarded to the player by simply sitting down in the Bolshoi Theatre and watching the 10 minute performance. Doing this will also reward the player with a positive [moral point](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points). The achievement/trophy will be unlocked when you leave the Theatre after the performance and meet [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov).
