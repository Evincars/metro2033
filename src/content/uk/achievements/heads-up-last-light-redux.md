**Heads Up!** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)*, featured by default in *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)*.

## Огляд

This achievement is awarded simply for completing the [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Chronicles_Pack_Level)) level on any difficulty. This is a moderately long level with optional stealth sequences during its first half, which are recommended, as most of the first enemies drop very little [ammunition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/ammunition) when killed.
