**Spoiled Dinner** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [Yamantau](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Yamantau_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Огляд

This achievement is obtained by completing the level [Yamantau](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Yamantau_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) on any difficulty. This is a story-related achievement that can not be missed.

## Цікаві факти

- The icon of this achievement contains a double-headed eagle, which is featured in a coat of arms of the Russian Federation. This is because [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) crew originally thought Yamantau was populated by the remnants of former Russian government.
