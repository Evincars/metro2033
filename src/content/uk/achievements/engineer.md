**Engineer** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Огляд

This achievement is unlocked by using 10 lever switches. These can be encountered on numerous occasions during the game and their purpose is usually opening doors/gates, switching the power on or off etc.
