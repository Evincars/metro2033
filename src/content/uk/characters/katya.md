## Огляд

Katya (Russian: Катя) is a trained nurse from the southern Urals who joins the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) crew along with her daughter [Nastya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nastya) during the events of [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus). She and Nastya were part of a group of about fifteen people who traveled west by rail in search of a better life. Upon arriving at the [Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Volga) region, they fell under the control of the [Church of the Water Tsar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Water_Tsar), a technophobic religious cult led by [Silantius](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Silantius). Katya's husband, Senya, was killed by the cult when he was sent on a suicidal mission to "exorcise" an [electrical anomaly](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anomaly_(Phenomenon)) as punishment for using technology.

## Роль у Metro Exodus

Katya and Nastya are rescued from the Church of the Water Tsar by [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) during the Volga chapter and subsequently join the Aurora crew. Aboard the train, Katya serves as the crew's nurse and medic. She later marries [Stepan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stepan), one of the Spartan Rangers. Katya plays a critical role in the story when Anna falls seriously ill from lung damage caused by exposure to poisonous gas. Katya examines Anna and later recalls that an anti-air defense unit in her village during the war received an experimental drug effective at treating similar conditions. She identifies this drug as [Renergan-F](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Renergan-F), produced at the [Akademgorodok Institute](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Akademgorodok) in [Novosibirsk](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk), which sets in motion the final act of the game.
