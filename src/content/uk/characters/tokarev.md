## Огляд

Nikolai Tokarev (Russian: Николай Токарев) is the [Spartan Order](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers)'s weapons expert who joins [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s crew aboard the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) armored train. A veteran Ranger, Tokarev was one of the survivors of the [Battle for D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_for_D6) and joined the crew on their journey eastward. He is renowned across the Order as the man responsible for creating many of the faction's [weapons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons) and modifications. Totally devoted to his craft, he spends much of his time in the workshop aboard the Aurora, maintaining and improving equipment and firearms for his fellow Rangers.

## Роль у Metro Exodus

Tokarev operates the Aurora's workshop, which serves as the game's primary weapon customization station. Players can visit him to modify and upgrade their weapons using parts found during exploration. Beyond his mechanical duties, Tokarev is a seasoned storyteller who shares tales of his past exploits with the crew during downtime. He provides a steady, experienced presence aboard the train and is one of the non-determinant crew members who survives the entire journey regardless of the player's choices. His expertise ensures that [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and the other Rangers are always properly equipped for the dangers they face across the Russian wilderness on their way to [Lake Baikal](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lake_Baikal).
