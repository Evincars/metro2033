## Огляд

Sergei (Russian: Сергей), better known by his self-chosen nickname Idiot (Russian: Идиот), is a [Spartan Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers) who serves aboard the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) armored train. Contrary to what his nickname suggests, Idiot is one of the most intelligent members of [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s crew. He selected the name himself, likely as a reference to [Dostoevsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Idiot)'s famous novel. Relatively quiet and philosophical by nature, when Idiot does speak, he often does so cryptically, offering insights that others may not immediately grasp. He is an avid reader and the Aurora's resident thinker.

## Роль у Metro Exodus

Idiot serves as the crew's analyst and intellectual resource throughout the journey. His observational skills and intelligence prove crucial at several points in the story. Most notably, after the crew obtains satellite images during [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) chapter, Idiot examines them closely and discovers that [Novosibirsk](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Novosibirsk) is so heavily irradiated that radiation levels are completely off the scale, presumably due to a cobalt bomb detonation. This critical finding informs [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller)'s decision to limit the search party for [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna)'s medicine to just himself and Artyom. Throughout the journey, Idiot can often be found reading or engaged in philosophical discussions with other crew members, including [Sam](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam), with whom he debates the practicalities of bringing the Metro's inhabitants to the surface.
