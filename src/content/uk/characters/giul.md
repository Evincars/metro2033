## Огляд

Giulnara "Giul" Khakimova (Russian: Гюльнара "Гюль" Хакимова) is a native of the [Caspian Desert](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Caspian_Sea) and a resistance fighter against the [Munai-bailer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Munai-bailer) slavers. Known as "the Witch" by the Munai-bailer, Giul was five years old when the bombs fell. Her father did not survive, but she and her mother took shelter in a communication centre that doubled as a bunker, where her mother worked as a senior researcher. As conditions deteriorated, they moved to a lighthouse above the bunker. Giul grew up to become a skilled and determined fighter, waging a one-woman guerrilla war against the Baron and his forces who had enslaved her people.

## Роль у Metro Exodus

Giul is a key ally during [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) chapter of Metro Exodus. She encounters [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) crew when they arrive in the dried-up Caspian Sea region. Giul provides crucial intelligence about [the Baron](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Baron) and his operation, and guides Artyom through the desert. She fights alongside Artyom against the Munai-bailer slavers, seeking to free her enslaved people. During the chapter's climax, the Baron captures Artyom and nearly kills Giul by pushing her from the top of his base, but [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) saves them both by shooting the Baron from a distance. The player's interactions with the local population and Giul during this chapter determine whether [Damir](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Damir) chooses to stay behind to help the Caspian people or continues with the Aurora crew.
