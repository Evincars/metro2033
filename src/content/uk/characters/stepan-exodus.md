## Огляд

Stepan (Russian: Степан) is a distinguished [Spartan Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers) and heavy weapons specialist who serves aboard the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) armored train. Not to be confused with the [Stepan from Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stepan_(Metro_2033)), this Stepan is a separate character who first appears in [Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035) and returns in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus). A survivor of the [Battle for D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_for_D6), Stepan is significantly taller than average and known for his physical strength. He originally hails from [Smolenskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Smolenskaya) station on the Arbatsko-Pokrovskaya line. During the events of Metro 2035, Stepan sided with [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and other veteran Rangers when a split occurred within the Order.

## Роль у Metro Exodus

Stepan serves as the Aurora crew's heavy weapons specialist throughout the journey across Russia. His large stature and strength make him the crew's muscle in dangerous situations. During the journey, Stepan develops a relationship with [Katya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Katya), the nurse who was rescued along with her daughter [Nastya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nastya) from the [Church of the Water Tsar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Water_Tsar) at the [Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Volga). The two eventually marry aboard the Aurora, with the entire crew celebrating their wedding. Stepan becomes Nastya's stepfather, showing a tender side beneath his imposing exterior. He is one of the non-determinant crew members, remaining with the team throughout the entire journey to [Lake Baikal](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lake_Baikal), where the crew ultimately settles.
