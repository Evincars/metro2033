## Огляд

Krest (Russian: Крест) is an engineer and mechanic who joins the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) crew during the events of [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus). Before the war, Krest lived in the small town of Kaduy. When the bombs fell, Kaduy was mostly spared, but Krest foresaw the coming nuclear winter and repaired an old truck to move south. After surviving several winters alone, he realized solitary survival was impossible and joined various groups. He traveled as far as [Astrakhan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Astrakhan), where he encountered bandits who captured and sold people fleeing the winter as slaves. Eventually, Krest found the Aurora's railcar and began trading supplies with [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s crew before officially joining them.

## Роль у Metro Exodus

Krest serves as a key support member of the Aurora crew, contributing his engineering and mechanical expertise to keep the train and its equipment running. His practical skills and resourcefulness make him invaluable during the long journey across Russia. He also operates a railcar that is used during the [Taiga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Taiga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) chapter, where [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Alyosha](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alyosha) use it to scout the forested region. The railcar is derailed by a landslide triggered by the [Forest Master](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bear), sending Artyom and Alyosha into the river. Despite his checkered past and rough exterior, Krest is a dependable and knowledgeable member of the crew who helps maintain the Aurora throughout the journey to [Lake Baikal](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lake_Baikal).
