The **Universal Charger** is a simple but necessary device used by [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) throughout his travels.

## Overview
In a world with little electricity outside of the stations, it is a vital piece of equipment that allows [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) to charge up most battery-powered devices. A simple, but efficient hand-operated dynamo, it can be used an unlimited number of times to charge and recharge the batteries of most electronic devices Artyom can carry. The universal charger of *Metro: Last Light* and the Redux version differs from its [2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) counterpart in that it charges several times faster, and if the battery is fully charged or close to it, trying to equip the charger will cause a green battery icon to shon on the screen accompanied by a sharp 'beep'.

## Appearances
Artyom receives it before his trip to [Riga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_Station_(Location)). It will see use throughout the game. It also returns in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light)*, with Artyom receiving it from [Vladimir](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vladimir).

## Uses
The charger affects the following devices:
- [Flashlight](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Flashlight): When charged, the beam of light the flashlight is more intense and has longer range.
- [Night Vision Goggles](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Vision_Goggles): Provides power. The goggles will become nonfunctional if the power runs out.
- [Hellbreath](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hellbreath_(Volt_Driver)): A variation on this charger is integerated into the Hellbreath to provide power for accelerating its ammunition, [ball bearings](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ball_Bearings). However, using the main charger will not recharge the Hellbreath.
