**Ammunition** is the term given to any item that can be used by a ranged , usually a firearm. Ammunition is commonly found in clips, cylindrical casings that hold cartridges (classically 6), or magazines, cartridges stacked in a line covered by a casing. Ammunition in the [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro) can vary from simple [ball bearings](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/ball_bearings) to high caliber [sniper-rifle ammo](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/12.7x108mm). Ammunition in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Videogame))* is used in long range combat to kill enemies.

In the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Metro_System), pre-war [military grade 5.45x39mm](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Grade_Rounds) rounds, or **MGR**, ammunition is used as currency as not only have not enough bills and coins lasted since the nuclear devastation, but when metro inhabitants are in need they can use it as ammunition itself, though this does result in the person literally "shooting money". Furthermore, they are impossible to replicate to match pre-war standards, so counterfeiting is difficult. Ammunition is uncommon and is usually found on dead humans or in caches scattered through the game, but [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) can also gain ammunition by exchanging ammo types that they have for others at [exchange kiosk](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/exchange_kiosk)s for a service fee.

## Ammunition Crafting
In *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*, ammunition can no longer be bought, as neither MGRs, nor traders are present. It's possible, however, to find it in the game world, or craft at a [workbench](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/workbench) using any mechanical and chemical materials acquired. In the field, when using the [backpack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/backpack), only ball bearings and bolts can be crafted, but when using a workbench, it is possible to craft all ammunition types. Any ammunition previously collected or crafted can be scrapped for additional materials. Scrapping only yields a fraction (one-sixth) of crafting materials back, but can still be helpful when low on resources and need to immediately craft [medkit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/medkit)s, [filters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/filters), or different ammunition types.
