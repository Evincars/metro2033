The **binoculars** are a new tool in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*.

## Overview
Appearing for the first time in the third game, the binoculars are a useful tool for scouting the environment, searching for paths and scanning for enemies. They are one of the numerous items at [Artyom's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) disposal during his journey from [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow) - across what is left of the [Russian Federation](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Russia) - in pursuit of a new place to call home in the East. Players can use the binoculars to mark points of interest in the various regions. After holding your gaze on a location, a question mark appears on your physical map pinpointing that place.

## Trivia
- The binoculars appearing in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)* are based on the WWII period KOMZ (Kazan Optical-Mechanical Factory) 6x30 model, used by the USSR since the 1940s.
