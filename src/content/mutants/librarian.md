# Librarian

Librarians are quasi-intelligent beasts, very dangerous, found only in the Moscow State Library. There are two varieties: common and black. Their appearance resembles grotesque gorillas, passively suggesting they are mutated from humans or apes.

Librarians are among the most feared mutants in all of Moscow. Their combination of raw physical power, surprising intelligence, and territorial aggression makes them extraordinarily dangerous adversaries.

## Behavior

Librarians are highly territorial and will challenge intruders with a direct stare. A known tactic for surviving an encounter is to maintain eye contact without showing fear — if the human does not look away or flee, the Librarian may back down. However, turning one's back or showing weakness will trigger an immediate attack.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Major enemies in the Library level.
