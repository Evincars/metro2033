# Spore Bud

Spore Buds are inactive plant species that pose no harm to Artyom. They are among the few passive organisms in the Metro, representing mutated plant life that has adapted to underground conditions.

While harmless individually, their presence indicates areas with higher radiation levels and active biological mutation.
