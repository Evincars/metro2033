# Ghosts

Though not true mutants, Ghosts are strange after-images of people or creatures that died in the metro and have not passed on. They are residual imprints of the massive loss of life that occurred during and after the Great War.

Ghosts typically appear as translucent, flickering images that replay moments from the past. While generally harmless, some Ghost phenomena can be dangerous, causing disorientation or psychological trauma.

## Behavior

Ghosts are generally passive, endlessly replaying moments from their lives or deaths. However, certain ghost phenomena are more active and can interact with the physical world in limited ways.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Encountered in various locations.
- **Metro: Last Light** — Ghost sequences appear throughout.
