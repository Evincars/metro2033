# Wolf

Wolves are pack animals that have remained virtually unchanged by radiation. They are among the few species that survived the nuclear apocalypse with minimal mutation.

Their natural hardiness and pack-based social structure have served them well in the post-apocalyptic wilderness, where they continue to thrive as effective predators.

## Behavior

Wolves hunt in coordinated packs, using the same tactics their ancestors employed for millennia. They are cautious around armed humans but will attack lone or wounded individuals.
