# Great Door

The Great Door is a seemingly hypnotic entity found in the lost catacombs, embodied by a large red door. It is one of the most enigmatic and disturbing phenomena in the Metro universe.

This entity takes the form of a massive, imposing door that exerts an irresistible pull on those who encounter it. Those drawn to it are compelled to open it, despite an overwhelming sense of dread.

## Behavior

The Great Door appears to possess a form of intelligence or willpower, actively luring people toward it through hypnotic suggestion. What lies beyond the door — and what happens to those who open it — remains one of the Metro's deepest mysteries.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Found in the Lost Catacombs level.
