# Blind Ones

The Blind Ones are giant gorilla-like mutants living in the abandoned research facility at Novosibirsk. They might be a family of the Librarians or a related species that evolved independently in similar conditions.

These massive humanoid creatures have lost their sight entirely, relying instead on an incredibly acute sense of hearing to navigate their dark environment. Despite their blindness, they are formidable predators.

## Behavior

Blind Ones are highly territorial and will attack anything that makes noise in their domain. However, if one moves slowly and quietly enough, it is possible to pass through their territory without conflict. They display a rudimentary social structure and communicate through low grunts and growls.
