# Nops

Nops are heavily mutated and altered dogs that are almost entirely changed by radiation. Unlike the relatively unchanged wolves and domesticated dogs that still exist, Nops have been transformed beyond recognition.

## Behavior

Nops retain the pack mentality of their canine ancestors but are far more aggressive and unpredictable. Their mutations have given them enhanced physical capabilities at the cost of any domesticated temperament.
