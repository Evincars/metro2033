# Błotniak

Błotniaks are semi-intelligent, humanoid, mutated creatures living in the Vistula river, but also capable of breathing and movement on land. They are somewhat similar to the mythical Slavic utopiec/topielec (drowner) creatures.

These amphibious mutants are among the more unusual species in the Metro universe, being one of the few mutants adapted to both aquatic and terrestrial environments.

## Behavior

Błotniaks are ambush predators that prefer to drag their prey into the water. They display signs of semi-intelligence, sometimes using rudimentary tools and coordinating attacks in groups.
