# Biomass

The Biomass is a very large mass of radioactive organic material formed after the use of so-called "Ancient Weapons." It is one of the most mysterious and terrifying entities encountered in the Metro universe.

Unlike most mutants, the Biomass is not a creature in the traditional sense — it is an amorphous mass of living organic matter that has fused together into a single, pulsating entity. Its origins are tied to experimental weapons technology that was deployed before or during the Great War of 2013.

## Behavior

The Biomass is largely stationary but continues to grow and absorb organic material that comes into contact with it. It is considered extremely dangerous to approach.
