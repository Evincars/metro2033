# Corpse Eaters

Corpse Eaters are non-lethal scavenger mutants that feed on the corpses of all creatures, including members of their own species. They fill an important ecological niche in the post-apocalyptic world as nature's cleanup crew.

These relatively small creatures pose little direct threat to healthy, alert humans. However, they can become dangerous in large numbers, especially to wounded or unconscious individuals.

## Behavior

Corpse Eaters are opportunistic feeders that congregate wherever death has occurred. They are drawn to the smell of decay and will swarm corpses in large numbers. While they prefer dead prey, they will attack weakened or sleeping creatures.
