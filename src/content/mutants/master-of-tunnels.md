# Master of the Tunnels

The Master of the Tunnels is a disembodied entity and a revered character of folklore, the keeper of the St. Petersburg Metro. This mysterious being is said to watch over the tunnels and those who travel through them.

In the St. Petersburg Metro, stories of the Master are told to children and adults alike. The entity is believed to protect those who respect the tunnels and punish those who would bring harm to the underground community.
