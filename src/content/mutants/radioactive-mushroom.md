# Radioactive Luminescent Mushroom

Radioactive Luminescent Mushrooms are common throughout the Metro. These mushrooms either cause or feed off radiation, creating an eerie bioluminescent glow in the darkest tunnels.

These fungi have become one of the defining features of the Metro's ecosystem, providing dim natural lighting in otherwise pitch-black tunnels. While they serve as a useful light source, their radioactive nature makes prolonged exposure dangerous.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Environmental feature throughout.
- **Metro: Last Light** — Continue as environmental elements.
- **Metro Exodus** — Found in underground sections.
