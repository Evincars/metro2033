# Dark Ones

The Dark Ones are a very powerful species of sentient humanoids with telepathic abilities that attempt to communicate with Artyom. They are the results of (possibly deliberately engineered) mutations in humans, and are also known as *Homo novus*.

The Dark Ones represent perhaps the most significant evolutionary leap in the post-apocalyptic world — beings that have not merely survived radiation but have been transformed into something entirely new. Their telepathic abilities allow them to communicate directly with the minds of humans, though this contact is often overwhelming and potentially lethal to those unprepared for it.

## Behavior

Dark Ones are intelligent, peaceful beings who seek to establish communication with humanity. Their telepathic contact, however, drives most humans insane or kills them outright — Artyom being a rare exception who can withstand and even understand their messages.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Central to the plot as the perceived threat to VDNKh.
- **Metro: Last Light** — The Baby Dark One becomes Artyom's ally.
