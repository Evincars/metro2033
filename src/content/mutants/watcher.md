# Watcher

Watchers (also known as Watchmen) are large mutants covered with dark grey fur, known for their distinct howling. They are one of the most commonly encountered surface mutants.

These quadrupedal creatures resemble a hybrid of wolves and apes, with powerful limbs and keen senses. Their distinctive howling serves as both a communication method and a warning to rivals.

## Behavior

Watchers are pack hunters that coordinate attacks using their howling. An alpha will typically observe from a distance while subordinates probe for weaknesses. When they detect vulnerability, the entire pack attacks simultaneously.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Common surface enemies.
- **Metro: Last Light** — Found on surface levels.
- **Metro Exodus** — Encountered across multiple regions.
