# Tsar Fish

The Tsar Fish is a large aquatic mutant inhabiting the Volga river. It is one of the most massive creatures encountered in the Metro universe, ruling the waters of the post-apocalyptic Volga region.

This colossal fish-like creature has grown to enormous proportions, becoming the undisputed apex predator of the Volga's waterways.

## Behavior

The Tsar Fish is territorial and will attack boats and swimmers that enter its domain. It is capable of capsizing small watercraft and dragging victims beneath the surface.

## Appearances

- **Metro Exodus** — Major threat in the Volga level, encountered during boat travel.
