# River of Fate

The River of Fate is an anomalous flow of water, said to be able to change the destiny of those who enter it. It is one of the many supernatural phenomena that exist in the Metro tunnels.

This mysterious underground river is spoken of in Metro folklore as having the power to alter the course of a person's life. Whether this is literal truth or metaphor remains debated among Metro dwellers.

## Behavior

The River flows through hidden sections of the Metro, and those who find it and enter its waters reportedly experience visions and emerge changed in some fundamental way.
