# Liana

Lianas are large tentacles that hang from the ceilings of the interiors of buildings. These mutated plants have developed an active predatory nature, lashing out at anything that passes beneath them.

## Behavior

Lianas hang motionless until prey passes within striking distance, then lash out with surprising speed. They can entangle and crush victims or drag them upward. Fire is an effective countermeasure.

## Appearances

- **Metro: Last Light** — Found in overgrown building interiors.
