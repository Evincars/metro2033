# Nosalis

The Nosalis is a common mutant found in the metro that likely mutated from moles. There are six varieties in Metro 2033: common, black, winged, albino, plated, and rhino. They are also known as "upyrs" in the books and appear in Metro 2034.

Nosalises are perhaps the most iconic mutants of the Metro universe. Their mole-like origins are evident in their powerful digging claws and sensitivity to light, but radiation has transformed them into swift, aggressive predators.

## Varieties

- **Common** — The standard variety, encountered in large numbers.
- **Black** — A darker, more aggressive variant.
- **Winged** — A rare variant with membranous wing-like appendages.
- **Albino** — A pale variant, often found in deeper tunnels.
- **Plated** — An armored variant with hardened skin plates.
- **Rhino** — The largest variant, serving as an alpha.

## Behavior

Nosalises are pack creatures that hunt in groups, often led by a dominant female (the Rhino variant). They are most dangerous in enclosed spaces where their speed and numbers can overwhelm defenders.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Common enemies from the beginning.
- **Metro: Last Light** — Continue to be a primary threat.
- **Metro Exodus** — Found in underground sections.
