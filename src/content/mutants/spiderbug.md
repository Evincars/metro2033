# Spiderbug

Spiderbugs are large scorpion and spider-like arthropods that are deathly vulnerable to light. They are among the most terrifying creatures in the Metro, combining the worst features of arachnids and scorpions.

These creatures thrive in the absolute darkness of the deepest tunnels. Their exoskeletons have become hypersensitive to light — even a flashlight beam can cause them to recoil and eventually kill them.

## Behavior

Spiderbugs are nocturnal ambush predators that attack from darkness. They will avoid lit areas and can be driven back with flashlights or other light sources. In total darkness, however, they are extremely dangerous and nearly invisible.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Found in dark tunnel sections.
- **Metro: Last Light** — Continue to inhabit dark areas.
