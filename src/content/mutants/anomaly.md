# Anomaly

Anomalies are supernatural, bright blue balls of light that will electrify and kill everything around them as they drift through the metro. They are one of the most mysterious and dangerous phenomena in the tunnels.

These glowing spheres move slowly through certain sections of the Metro, emitting deadly electrical discharges. Their origin is unknown — they may be related to the high radiation levels, supernatural forces, or some unknown physical phenomenon.

## Behavior

Anomalies drift through tunnels at unpredictable speeds and directions. Anything that comes within their electrical field is instantly killed. The only defense is avoidance.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Encountered in certain tunnel sections.
- **Metro: Last Light** — Continue to appear in the tunnels.
