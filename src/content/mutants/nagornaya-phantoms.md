# Nagornaya Phantoms

The **Nagornaya Phantoms** are supernatural entities described in *Metro 2034*. Very little is known about their appearance, except that they are giants partially obscured by the fog of the station. The Nagornaya phantoms will try to confuse any who enter the station, and kill the youngest of a group to supposedly consume their life force. It's unknown if the Nagornaya phantoms are truly supernatural beings or simply mutants who have developed an ability to somehow create fog.

## In the Novel

Homer and Hunter encounter the Nagornaya Phantoms during their journey through the abandoned stations of the Serpukhovsko-Timiryazevskaya line. The phantoms haunt Nagornaya station, creating an impenetrable fog that fills the tunnels and platforms. Those who enter the station become disoriented, and the phantoms target the youngest member of any group that passes through, draining their life force.
