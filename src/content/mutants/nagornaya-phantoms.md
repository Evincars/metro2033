# Nagornaya Phantoms

Nagornaya Phantoms are giant supernatural entities obscured by fog who kill the youngest member of groups to apparently consume their life force. It is possible that these are not supernatural beings, but mutants who have somehow gained the ability to create fog.

These mysterious entities haunt the area near Nagornaya station, appearing only when fog rolls through the tunnels.

## Behavior

The Phantoms create or are accompanied by thick fog that reduces visibility to near zero. They then target the youngest person in any group, killing them through unknown means — possibly by draining their life force.
