# Nosalis Rhino

The Nosalis Rhino is a massive nosalis leader that acts as an alpha female to common nosalises. It is significantly larger and more heavily armored than standard nosalises.

The Rhino serves as the matriarch of nosalis packs, directing their movements and coordinating attacks. Its armored head plate gives it a battering-ram capability that can break through barricades.

## Behavior

The Rhino leads charges against fortified positions, using its bulk to smash through barriers while common nosalises follow in its wake. It is extremely durable and requires concentrated firepower to bring down.

- **Metro 2033** — Boss encounter.
