# Shrimp

Shrimps are large chitinous marine mutants found in the underground metro "river" and the swamps on the surface. Despite their name, they bear little resemblance to their pre-war crustacean namesakes.

These amphibious creatures have developed powerful limbs that allow them to move both in water and on land, making them dangerous in flooded areas of the Metro and surface swamps alike.

## Behavior

Shrimps are ambush predators that lurk in shallow water, striking at prey that ventures too close. They can also emerge onto land for short periods. In groups, they can overwhelm even well-armed humans.

## Appearances

- **Metro: Last Light** — Found in swamp areas.
- **Metro Exodus** — Encountered in the Volga and other water areas.
