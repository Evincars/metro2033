# Bear

The Bear is a creature that was affected by the radiation and grew to monstrous proportions, now a powerful boss-entity. It is one of the largest and most dangerous surface creatures encountered in the Metro universe.

Bears were once ordinary members of the Ursidae family, but twenty years of radiation exposure have transformed them into hulking, armored monstrosities. Their thick, mutated hide provides natural armor plating, and their already considerable strength has been amplified many times over.

## Behavior

Bears are solitary apex predators that roam the surface, claiming vast territories for themselves. They are extremely aggressive and will attack anything that enters their domain. Their massive size and raw power make them nearly impossible to take down with conventional small arms.

## Appearances

- **Metro Exodus** — Encountered as a boss creature in the Taiga level.
