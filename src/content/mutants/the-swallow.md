# The Swallow

The Swallow resembles a long gray caterpillar. The body is similarly elongated, with the front of the body curving upward at right angles. At the very top of the curve is the mouth.

The Swallow's body is covered with hair. Six furry spider-like legs stick out from under its belly, with which it can move quickly and silently, or walk sideways like a crab. Each leg ends in three predatory curved claws.

## Behavior

Swallows are stealthy predators that can move almost silently despite their size. Their ability to move sideways like a crab makes them unpredictable in combat, and their curved claws are capable of tearing through armor.
