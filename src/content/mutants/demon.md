# Demon

Demons are giant bat-like creatures found only on the surface. They are among the most feared airborne predators in the post-apocalyptic Moscow, capable of swooping down to snatch prey from the ground.

These massive flying creatures have leathery wings spanning several meters and powerful talons capable of lifting a fully-grown man. They nest on the tops of ruined buildings and tower structures.

## Behavior

Demons are aggressive aerial predators that patrol the skies above Moscow's ruins. They hunt by sight, circling above potential prey before diving at tremendous speed. Their preferred tactic is to grab victims and carry them to great heights before dropping them.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — First encountered on surface levels.
- **Metro: Last Light** — Appear during surface sections.
- **Metro Exodus** — Encountered in various surface areas.
