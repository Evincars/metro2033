# Damned Souls

Damned Souls are malevolent dead that cannot move on, and drag down anyone they are able to reach. They are among the supernatural horrors that lurk in the deepest and most dangerous sections of the Metro.

These restless spirits are trapped between life and death, consumed by rage and despair. Unlike the relatively passive Ghosts, Damned Souls actively seek to harm the living.

## Behavior

Damned Souls are drawn to the living, attempting to seize and drag them into whatever dark realm they inhabit. They are most active in areas where great suffering or death occurred.
