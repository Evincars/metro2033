# Lurker

Lurkers are small, numerous, and agile creatures likely mutated from large rodents. They are among the most common mutants encountered in the Metro tunnels and represent one of the most successful post-apocalyptic species.

Their high reproductive rate has allowed them to become the most populous mutant species in the Metro. While individually weak, their strength lies in overwhelming numbers.

## Behavior

Lurkers are pack hunters that rely on speed and numbers to bring down prey. They are cowardly individually but become emboldened when in groups, swarming targets from multiple directions. They often flee when their numbers are significantly reduced.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Common enemies throughout.
- **Metro: Last Light** — Encountered in tunnel sections.
- **Metro Exodus** — Found in underground areas.
