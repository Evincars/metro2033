# Giant Amoeba

The Giant Amoeba is a large explosive ball of protoplasm, which seeks out living creatures and explodes on contact. It is one of the more unusual mutants found in the Metro tunnels.

These bizarre organisms appear as translucent, gelatinous spheres that float or slowly crawl through the tunnels. They are attracted to heat and movement, drifting toward any living creature they detect.

## Behavior

Giant Amoebae are essentially biological mines. They slowly move toward heat sources and detonate on contact, releasing corrosive organic material in all directions. They are most dangerous in enclosed tunnel spaces where avoidance is difficult.

## Appearances

- **Metro: Last Light** — Found in certain tunnel sections.
