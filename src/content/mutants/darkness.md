# Darkness

The Darkness consists of ill-defined patches of ghost-related darkness that renders electric light useless. It is one of the most terrifying phenomena in the Metro — areas where light itself seems to die.

These zones of supernatural darkness cannot be penetrated by any artificial light source. Flashlights, lamps, and even fires are smothered by this oppressive void.

## Behavior

The Darkness appears to be sentient on some level, actively snuffing out light sources and closing in on those who enter its domain. Many Metro travelers who entered areas of Darkness were never seen again.

## Appearances

- **Metro 2033** — Experienced in certain tunnel sections.
