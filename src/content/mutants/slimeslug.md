# Slimeslug

Slimeslugs are very powerful, presumably predatory snail-like creatures that leave slimy trails in their wake. They are a mutant only ever mentioned in the books, never directly encountered in the games.

These mysterious creatures are among the lesser-known inhabitants of the Metro tunnels, spoken of in hushed tones by tunnel dwellers who have encountered their distinctive slime trails.

## Behavior

Little is known about Slimeslug behavior, as few who encounter them directly survive to tell the tale. Their slimy trails serve as a warning to experienced Metro travelers.
