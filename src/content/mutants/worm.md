# Worm

Worms are giant leeches living in a flooded station of the Novosibirsk Metro. These blood-sucking parasites have grown to terrifying proportions due to radiation exposure.

The flooded tunnels of Novosibirsk provide the perfect environment for these creatures — dark, warm, and filled with organic material to feed on.

## Behavior

Worms lurk in flooded areas, attaching themselves to anything that moves through the water. They drain blood rapidly and in sufficient numbers can kill a person in minutes.

## Appearances

- **Metro Exodus** — Found in the flooded Novosibirsk metro sections.
