- The events of the novel [Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035) transpire.
- The events of the game [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) transpire.
- February 21: [Vyshny Volochyok](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vyshny_Volochyok) receives a transmission from the [Faroe Islands](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faroe_Islands).
- February 22: Artyom and a group of rangers steal the Hanza train and escape Moscow's ruins.
- February 23: Artyom and crew stop 108 kilometers from Moscow. Discover the area outside the ruins are safe from radiation. Decide to name their train the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora).
- March 8: Aurora crew arrives in [Saratov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Volga). Events of the chapter Volga occur.
