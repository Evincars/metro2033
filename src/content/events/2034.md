- May 14th: [Su](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Su) and [Bar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bar) haul a portable generator into the ruins of the Great Library, making a recording that they leave in one of the buildings in the library's inner courtyard.
- May 19th: Bar makes a recording in the Great Library Catalogue Room regarding the library's records, and lamenting the fact that most have likely been destroyed.
- The events of Metro 2034 take place.
  - Ex-Stalker Hunter stations himself at [Sevastopolskaya Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sevastopolskaya_Station), under the alias of the Brigadier
  - [Remainder unknown to western audiences.]
- According to [The Gospel According to Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Gospel_According_to_Artyom), Artyom returns to the Botanical Gardens to discover a surviving Dark One.
- Artyom is promoted to the position of Ranger.
- The Rangers of the Order have settled in D6.
- The events of [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light) take place some time after the 10th of may.
  - The events of the [Faction Pack DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faction_Pack_DLC) for Metro: Last Light take place some time before or during Artyom's search for the remaining Dark One.
  - A Red Army spy, [Lesnitsky](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lesnitsky), abandons D6 with a covert sample of [a bioweapon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Virus).
  - A [Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dark_One) is found in the ruins of the Botanical Gardens by Khan, Artyom and a Ranger by name of [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) are sent to assassinate it
  - Artyom and Anna fail to kill the Dark One. It's captured by Stalkers of the Fourth Reich, along with Artyom. In Reich custody, Artyom comes across a Red soldier, Major [Pavel Morozov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov).
  - Pavel guides Artyom through the metro, with promise to take him back to the Rangers. Pavel betrays Artyom to the Red Line at [Theatre Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Theatre_Station).
  - Escaping Red Line custody by help of [Leonid](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Leonid), Artyom uncovers plans by Red Army [General Korbut](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Korbut_(Character)) and puppet leader of the Red Line [Moskvin](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moskvin), to use a super weapon to conquer the metro.
  - Sparta Base is destroyed by ex-Ranger Lesnitsky. In the process, Anna is taken hostage.
  - [Oktyabrskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Oktyabrskaya) is infected with the Virus as a test scenario. Roughly one day later, the Red Army, satisfied with the result, storms the station and burns it to the ground.
  - Anna is saved by Artyom, at the cost of them both being infected with the Virus. In quarantine the two become intimate.
  - Artyom tracks down and saves the baby Dark One from a hijacked Hanza train, remembering his pact he once made to them. Together they cross the surface, and uncover plans to use the Virus after the upcoming [Polis Peace Conference](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_Peace_Conference). The two also encounter Lesnitsky and Pavel again - and Artyom is made with the decision to spare or kill each of them.
  - With Khan and Artyom's assistance, the baby Dark One discovers more Dark Ones sleeping in the vaults of D6.
  - Artyom arrives at the peace conference with the baby Dark One, and together they make Moskvin uncover Korbut's plans to invade D6.
    - It is about this time that "[Heavy Squad](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Squad)" takes place. The Reich successfully defends the front line from the Red Army.
  - [The Battle of D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Battle_of_D6) takes place. Despite valiant effort the Rangers are defeated by the sheer number of Red Line soldiers, and their unrivaled equipment.
    - Artyom detonates D6 with a self-destruct system, sacrificing himself and all the Rangers except Anna. The Red Line is annihilated, and the metro is saved.
    - The baby Dark One stops Artyom moments before he activates the self-destruct system, stating that he has returned to help. The second coming of the Dark Ones begins with their defeating the Red Army and saving the metro.
      - Metro: Last Light concludes as the Dark Ones leave the metro, stating that they will return one day to help humanity rebuild.
- August 11th: Expedition from [VDNKh](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) arrive at the abandoned [Botanicheskiy Sad](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Botanicheskiy_Sad) station with the intent of settling it.
- August 12th: Botanicheskiy expedition establishes themselves within the station proper with the goal of sealing the airlock door. Motor on the door is found to have rusted solid since Artyom opened it, requiring extensive work to get functioning.
- August 13th: Botanicheskiy expedition starts running low on filters and coming under attack from Watchmen. The group decides to simply weld a sheet of metal over the door to seal the station.
- August 14th: Welding equipment is delivered to Botanicheskiy Sad, but the group is unable to use it due to a pack of Watchmen that become agitated every time they attempt to. Mikhalych makes a recording regarding the pack, hoping to sell it to Polis. The group is then attacked by the pack and wiped out.
- December 31st: [Vyacheslav Khlebnikov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khlebnikov), his [son](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kirill), and the rest of [Krasny Prospekt](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Krasny_Prospekt) celebrate the New Year in the Novosibirsk Metro.
