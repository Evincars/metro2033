- According to the Animal Gene novel from the Universe of Metro 2033, July 6th: [The Great War of 2013](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Great_War_of_2013) takes place as a result of a nuclear exchange in the Middle East becoming a global battle. Moscow, and most of Russia is destroyed, while a Vengeance Submarine destroys part of the United States of America. However, in the novel, WW3 is ongoing and the countries that survived are America, parts of Europe, Africa, and other countries, but not the Middle East.
  - The events of the [Metro Live Action Short](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Live_Action_Short) take place.
  - The [Currency Exchange Booth Survivor](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Currency_Exchange_Booth_Survivor) is trapped within their booth.
    - July 19th: The Currency Exchange Booth Survivor commits suicide.
- The first supernatural phenomenon arise.
  - The underground of what will be known as [Sparta Base](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sparta_Base_(Location)) becomes home to the first [Nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalises), and from the survivors, most likely the first [Ghosts](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ghosts_(Phenomenon)) of the metro.
- The first mutants arise.
  - The [creature](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kremlin_Creature) of the [Kremlin](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kremlin) first forms from biological warfare.
