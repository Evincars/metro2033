- January 12th: Colonel M.S. Markov leaves final orders for the personnel of [Caspian-1](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Caspian-1) before committing suicide. This note, and the commander's death, are hidden by Lieutenant-Colonel R.V. Novikov, who becomes acting commander of the facility.
- February 12th: Caspian-1 personnel finish analyzing satellite radiation maps of the [Burabay Valley](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Valley). They conclude that, as long as the dam remains intact, the area is likely safe for habitation.
