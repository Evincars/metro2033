- February 8th: [Kuzmich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kuzmich) makes a recording in the Central Storage room of the [Lenin Library](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Library) regarding how [Stalkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers) should deal with [Librarians](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Librarian).
- Various entries of the [Universe of Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Universe_of_Metro_2033_(Book_Series)) take place.
- The events of the [Metro 2033 Novel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)), and [Metro 2033 Videogame](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) take place over the course of 8 days.
  - Veteran Stalker, Ranger, [Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter) travels to VDNKh, Exhibition station to aid against a new mutant threat, the Dark Ones.
  - Artyom is entrusted by Hunter to travel to Polis to get help from the Stalkers, Rangers there.
  - Artyom meets with ex-bandit and Metro guide, [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon). After traveling together for a short time, Bourbon is killed.
  - Artyom first encounters Khan, who shows him the mystical side of the metro.
  - [Cursed Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Station) is saved by Artyom and Khan.
  - Artyom is almost arrested by the Reds at [Armory Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_Station_(Location)). However, he's saved by [Andrew the Blacksmith](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Andrew).
  - Nazi's Attack Kitai Gorod Station, also known as China-Town.
  - Artyom is captured by Nazis. He is saved, by [Revolutionaries](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolutionaries), Rangers [Ulman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ulman) and [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Ranger)).
  - Accompanying Artyom to Black Station, Pavel dies at the Depot.
  - The [Hole Station Massacre](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hole_Station_Massacre) takes place. Artyom manages to save a child, [Sasha](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sasha).
  - Artyom finally arrives at Polis, though the Brahmin there refuse to help Artyom. This is unless Artyom helps them find 'The Future'. Veteran Stalkers Melnik and Ten, Brahmin Daniel, and Artyom travel to the former [Lenin Library](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_State_Library) in search of the legendary book known as 'The Future'. In the process, Daniel dies and the the Future is not found.
  - Elite Stalker, Ranger Colonel, Melnik, Miller conceives that the Dark Ones can be stopped by a missile strike to the Gardens. For which they will need to travel to D6 which can only be found if Documents from the Library can be retrieved.
  - The D6 documents are found in the great library.
  - Kievskaya resident [Oleg](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Oleg) is abducted by the Cult of the Great Worm.
  - Artyom is abducted by the Cult of the Great Worm, and is saved by Stalkers led by Melnik - who has returned from D6.
  - Kievskaya resident and rocket expert [Tretyak](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tretyak) dies.
  - The journey to D6 begins. Many Rangers fall in battle.
  - Oleg, among others, dies from the [Kremlin Creature](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kremlin_Creature).
  - The legendary D6 is found, and the [Biomass](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Biomass_(Mutant)) at the bottom of it is defeated.
  - A group of Stalkers including, Miller and, Artyom travel to [Ostankino Tower](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ostankino_Tower) to attack the Dark Ones.
  - Metro 2033 Concludes with the destruction of the Dark Ones and with Artyom stricken with guilt, questioning whether he did the right thing.
    - Finally understanding their intent for peace all along, the Dark Ones are spared by Artyom. The timeline becomes unstable as the events of Metro: Last Light become unclear, and largely avoided.
