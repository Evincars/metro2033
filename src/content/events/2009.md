- [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) is born in [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow), Russia. Wording in the [Metro 2033 Novel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)) suggests that this takes place later, closer to 2012, though plot predicted to appear in [Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035) would indicate this is the year in the novels as well.
