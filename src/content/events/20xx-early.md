- The first [Stalkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers) climb to the surface to survey the world. Their role of scavengers is to be fully established within a few years.
- The first Metro-made [weapons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons) are developed.
- "United administration", Metro Command falls apart. The result is most likely the beginning of the [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)) faction.
  - [Bandit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits) factions are first established.
- Factions establish themselves at various times, but most probably in the following order:
  - [Watchtower](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Watchtower) is established.
  - [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) is established.
  - The [Arbat Confederation](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arbat_Confederation) is established.
  - [The Rangers of the Order](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Rangers_of_the_Order) are established.
  - The [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) is established.
  - The [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) is established.
  - The [Savage Cannibals of the Great Worm Cult](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Savage_Cannibals_of_the_Great_Worm_Cult) first occupy [Park Pobedy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Park_Pobedy).
  - The [Satanists](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Satanists) are found occupying the Lyublinsko-Dmitrovskaya Line.
- 2020 - this is the year that the [Tower Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tower_Pack_(DLC)) is suspected to take place. The operators inside D6 are most likely the remnants of Metro Command and one of the soldiers in the intro mention that its been seven years since the Great War ended.
- [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)) is sealed. The operatives inside die. If the events of the Tower Pack took place at around this time, it could very well be that the power fluctuations spoken about during the mission were what did in those inside. The cause of the fluctuations can only be [speculated](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Biomass_(Mutant)) about.
- The mutant threat grows out of control, tunnels are collapsed as the surface becomes inhospitable.
  - Sometime in the next few years, [Paveletskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Paveletskaya) falls under attack from constant mutant threat.
- The Red Line and Hanza partake in war, simply known as the [Hansa- Red Line War](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hansa-_Red_Line_War). The victory goes to Hanza, and the Ring Line is completed.
- The Reich-Red Line war begins. The [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline) is drawn.
- As biological research, the [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones) awaken from D6 and find their way to the surface.
- [Timiryazevskaya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Timiryazevskaya) is overrun by rats. Artyom's mother dies, though Artyom is saved by [Sukhoi - Alex](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alex).
  - Sukhoi, Alex, takes Artyom to VDNKh, Exhibiton.
- Approximately 2015: Artyom, [Eugine](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Eugine), and another friend take a journey to the [Botanical Gardens](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Botanicheskiy_Sad). On the surface, Artyom is attacked by mutants, though he's saved by a Dark One. In the resulting conversation, the Dark One "adopts" Artyom into one of their fold. This brief contact with the Dark One damages his memory, so that he forgets of their encounter, but greatly protects him from mental danger in the future.
