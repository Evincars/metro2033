**Walking Bank** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded by having 1000 [Military Grade Rounds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Grade_Rounds) on you at any one time, but not over time. This can be gained legitimately by not buying anything during a single play-through, and making sure you hunt for extra MGRs. Selling extra ammunition will also help. However, a simpler way to unlock this is found on the level [Bandits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits_(Level)). All you have to do is collect the weapons from the [bandits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits_(Faction)), after taking them down, and selling them to the [weapons vendor](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_Vendors) at the start of the level (In the corner of the group of refugees). This is also a great way to earn MGR's faster should you need them anyway.
