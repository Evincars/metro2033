**Who Goes There? **in an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for wiping your [gas mask](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/gas_mask) 20 times. This is one of the easiest achievements to obtain in the game - the moment you can put on a gas mask, just hit the wipe mask button twenty times and this will unlock.
