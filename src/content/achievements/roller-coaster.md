**Roller Coaster** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Overview

This achievement can be unlocked during the Caspian level, when [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Damir](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Damir) infiltrate the [gangster](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Munai-bailer) camp to steal a tank of water. After following Damir through the early stages of the level, the player will be given free reign to proceed however they want. The bucket lift is located in the first area with human enemies; it is prominent in the level. The player simply needs to approach it and press *Use* to ride it to the top. Doing this will not only grant the achievement, it will also help the player to avoid multiple enemies, making this mission easier and quicker to complete.
