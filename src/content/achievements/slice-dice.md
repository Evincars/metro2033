**Slice & Dice** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) and [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux), awarded for obtaining certain amount of kills with the [Trench Knife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Trench_Knife).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing 20 enemies in Metro 2033, and 30 enemies in Metro 2033 Redux using [Artyom's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) knife. This must be done in one playthrough. It is possible to restart any level to farm kills. It is fairly simple to earn this when a stealth approach is used - simply slit as many throats as you can to earn this.
