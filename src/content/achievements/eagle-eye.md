**Eagle Eye** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing more [Watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchmen) than [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) in the level [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_(Level)). The sniper rifle [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_(Character)) has will kill a Watchman in one hit, so line up your shots anywhere on the body and fire. There will be several waves of Watchmen, and killing about 8-9 of them is usually enough to unlock this achievement.
