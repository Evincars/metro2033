**DJ Artyom** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) and [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux) that can be obtained in the level [Outpost](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Outpost_(Level)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for broadcasting the pre-recorded message from [Maxim Komarov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Maxim_Komarov) and the [Children of the Underground](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Children_of_the_Underground) at the tape recorder in the level Outpost. The tape can be acquired after the player tries to defend [Hole Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hole_Station) from the hordes of mutants. During the evacuation, Komarov will hand over the tape to the player on the level [Defense](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Defense_(Level)). The recorder is found on top of the [Nazi](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nazi) occupied ruined building in the level Outpost. The player must climb to the roof and play the message. Upon completion the player will receive the achievement/trophy and will be awarded two positive moral points, one for taking the tape from [Komarov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Maxim_Komorov) and another one for playing the message. If the player stays on the roof after playing the message two [demon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/demon)s will appear and attack the player.
