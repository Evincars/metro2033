**A Man of Principle** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Sam's Story](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam's_Story), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Overview

This achievement is obtained by earning the [Captain](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Captain_(Vladivostok))'s full trust. This is done by preforming several kind actions for the Captain. These include:
- Lowering your gun when you first meet him.
- Helping him move a girder out of the way after you help him fight off [humanimals](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Humanimal).
- Giving him some food before fixing his knee.
- Arming all five traps at his base. This will also unlock the "[Trapper](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Trapper)" achievement.
- Finding the Captain's missing friends.
- Destroying the [submarine](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Mayflower).
All of these actions must be completed to unlock this achievement.

## Notes

- It has been reported that actually destroying the submarine isn't required for some players, and the achievement will unlock once a choice is made during the finale.
