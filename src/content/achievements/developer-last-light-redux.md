**Developer** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Developer Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Developer_Pack_(DLC)), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) available for *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)* and featured by default in *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for spending at least 3 hours (1 hour in Redux) on the DEVELOPER [level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Developer_Pack_(Level)). This includes the [Metro Museum](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Museum), the [AI Arena](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/AI_Arena) and the [Shooting Gallery](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shooting_Gallery). It is unknown exactly how this is calculated, and whether 'cheats' such as setting your console clock forward three hours gets you the achievement/trophy. This can be done over multiple playthroughs, however, playing a different level inbetween resets the timer, as it seems to be tracked in the autosave file. If you are struggling, leave the game running for three hours while you do something else, to get this.
