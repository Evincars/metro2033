**Silent Marksman** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Overview

The achievement is obtained for killing 30 enemies using the [Tikhar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) pneumatic rifle. This weapon is given to [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) by [Tokarev](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tokarev) at the beginning of [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)). It has fairly low damage per shot and has to be regularly pumped so that the damage doesn't drop even further, but it is very silent, compared to firearms that use combustion ammunition. Therefore, the best way to get this achievement is to perform headshots with fully-pumped Tikhar while remaining undetected. This will usually kill the enemy with a single shot.
