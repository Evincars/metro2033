**Saboteur** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Faction Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faction_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)*, featured by default in *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded simply for completing the [Sniper Team](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sniper_Team) level on any difficulty. This is a moderately difficult level where the player takes a role of an elite [Red Line Sniper](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line_Sniper) and sabotages a [Nazi](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nazi) base on the surface. What makes this level difficult is the fact that the player must remain completely undetected during the entire time, or the mission will fail.

## Trivia

- The icon used for this achievement in the original *Metro: Last Light* is instead used for the achievement [Within a Hair of Death](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Within_a_Hair_of_Death) in the Redux version and replaced with a new one.
