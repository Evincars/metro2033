**True Spartan** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)* that can be obtained in the [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Level)) level.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player after completing the level D6 without dying once. If Artyom dies, the player will need to restart the level. The achievement/trophy should unlock after the [flamethrower heavy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Flamethrower) is dead, and before the [armoured train](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armored_Train) breaks through the wall. This is considered easy as long as the player takes cover throughout the level. Playing on a low [difficulty](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties) is recommended.
