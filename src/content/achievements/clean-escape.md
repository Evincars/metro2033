**Clean Escape** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)* and *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to players who escape the [Nazis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) on "[Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Reich)" without being caught once. Simply follow [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel) closely and when he fires into the air, sprint after him. As long as you do not stop and are captured, or get killed, this will be unlocked. If you do fail, you will have to start the level again, but it is a short one.
