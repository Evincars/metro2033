**It's Just a Scratch** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[The Two Colonels](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Two_Colonels)*, a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*.

## Overview

This achievement is obtained after finishing the entire playthrough of The Two Colonels on at least Normal [difficulty](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties) without using a single [Medkit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Medkit). Unlike in previous games, [player's character](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khlebnikov) doesn't fully regenerate his health back after a while when playing on Normal or higher, so getting this achievement might be tricky. A careful approach, engaging from afar and using stealth when possible is recommended. When the player survives an encounter with enemies but gets seriously injured in the process, it is better to reload the last save instead of immediately continuing further.

The achievement is not awarded when you use a [Medkit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Medkit) and later load an earlier savepoint. This is important for players trying to unlock [Mind You, It's Quite Heavy!](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_You,_It's_Quite_Heavy!) together with this in one run.

## Trivia
