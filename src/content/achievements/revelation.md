**Revelation** is a hidden [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and Metro Last Light Redux that can be obtained in the level [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for revealing [Secretary Moskvin's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moskvin) plans during the level Polis. It is a story related achievement that can not be missed.
