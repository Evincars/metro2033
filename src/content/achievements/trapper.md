## Overview

This achievement can be obtained by setting all five traps in the [Captain](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Captain_(Vladivostok))'s base. The locations of these traps are marked on the floor with white chalk, and the player should come across all five on their way down from the roof. Keep in mind that you will need 5 [Molotov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Molotov_Cocktail)'s, one for each trap.
