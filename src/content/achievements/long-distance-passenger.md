**Long Distance Passenger** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Overview

This achievement is received for stealing the [passenger carriage](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora's_Passenger_Train_Car) from the [bandits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/bandits) and bringing it to the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) using [Krest's railcar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Krest's_Railcar). As this is a story mission that is required for completing the level and moving to the next one, this achievement is impossible to miss.
