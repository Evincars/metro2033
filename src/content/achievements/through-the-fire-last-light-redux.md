**Through the Fire** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Developer Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Developer_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)*, featured by default in *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded simply for completing the [Spider Lair](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_Lair) level on any difficulty.
