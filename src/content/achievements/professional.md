**Professional** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Overview

On order to receive this achievement, the player must simply kill an enemy with each of the firearm weapons available to [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom). Kills with explosives and other throwables are not required to get this achievement.

Weapons that the player needs to use are:
***[Shambler](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shambler)** - This is a starting weapon in the [very first level](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Introduction_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) of the game.
***[Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash)** - Artyom is given one during the [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level. Can be picked up off most human enemies throughout the game.
***[Revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver)** - Artyom is given one during the Moscow level by [Yermak](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Yermak).
***[Bastard](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bastard)** - This weapon can be picked up off most human enemies throughout the game. One can be found in a house on the water near the area where Artyom first comandeers a [boat](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rowboat) at the beginning of the [Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.
***[Ashot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashot)** - Can be picked up off most human enemies throughout the game or found in the enviroment starting in the [Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.
***[Tikhar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar)** - Given to Artyom by [Tokarev](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tokarev) during the Volga level.
***[Valve](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Valve)** - Can be found during the [Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level from some enemies. It can also be found in the bridge of the ship that is about halfway between [Saul](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Saul)'s outpost and the lighthouse, laying on a crate near an oil lamp. Guaranteed drop from a sniper enemy on a crane that [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) gives the player a mission to eliminate.
***[Bulldog](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bulldog)** - Can be found during the Caspian level dropped from certain enemies when helping [Giul](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Giul) in the lighthouse.
***[Helsing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Helsing)** - Picked up at the beginning of the [Taiga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Taiga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level from a statue in front of the starting point.
***[Gatling](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gatling)** - Rare gun, dropped from the Armord Heavy Troopers. One during the [Yamantau](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Yamantau_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level just before rescuing Anna, three during the Caspian level, one when Aurora's crew is attacked at the Aurora by the Barons men, one is guarding the slaves in the prison hulk, and the last when defending against the attackers at the top of the oil rig.
