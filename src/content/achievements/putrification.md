**Putrification** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [The Dead City](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dead_City_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Overview

This achievement is obtained by passing through the biomass-covered, half-flooded tunnel with a [rowboat](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/rowboat). This area is encountered shortly after [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) separate at [Kirill's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kirill_(Exodus)) hideout and Artyom passes an abandoned station infested with mutants. On higher difficulties, it is recommended to kill all acid-spitting [worms](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Worm) with a long ranged weapon before going through the tunnel, as they can overwhelm the player, given their large numbers.
