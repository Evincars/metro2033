**Cheers!** is a secret [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

In order to receive this achievement, there are three occasions where the player must pay for a drink at the bars inside the metro station. Fortunately, all three locations take place at the same bar in the [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Level)) level. Simply approach the bartender, pay for your drink and then take a swig. Repeat this two more times here and the achievement will finally unlock before [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) blacks out.
