**Manhattan Project **is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for spending 60 seconds in a radiation hotspot. The hotspots are commonly found on the surface - when your watch beeps, your [Geiger Counter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Geiger_Counter) starts crackling and you begin to take damage, you are in one. The [Dead City](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_City_1) is a good place to get this achievement.

## Trivia

- [The Manhattan Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan_Project) was the codename for the USA's atomic bomb project during World War II.
