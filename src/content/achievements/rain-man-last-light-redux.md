**Rain Man** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)* and *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to players who do not kill anything on the [Bridge](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bridge_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)) level. It is possible to run past all of the [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/mutants), but it's highly advised that you take a stealth approach, as they can easily kill you.
