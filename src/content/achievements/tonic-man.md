**Tonic Man **is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for using [medkits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Medkit) 75 times. This will likely unlock through natural gameplay, but you can also load a chapter, take damage, use a medkit, reload and repeat. This is very easily accomplished in the Dead City level, as in a certain part of the level, an autosave occurs very close to a radiation hotspot. Stand in the hotspot for a while, use all five medkits, reload the save and do it again until enough medkit uses are farmed.
