**The Sunset of Hope** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)*, featured by default in *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded simply for completing the "[Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_(Level))" chapter on any difficulty. This level is basically the level "[Ashes](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashes)", but viewed from [Anna's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_(Character)) perspective, with added stealth sequence in its last part where Anna is trying to save [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom), who has been captured by the [Nazis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nazis).
