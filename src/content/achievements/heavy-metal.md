**Heavy Metal** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for obtaining a kill count of 15 whilst using a [Mounted Machinegun](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mounted_Machinegun). The kills can either be obtained on human enemies or on [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/mutants) and has to be done in a single playthrough of the story.

A stationary machine gun can be used in the following locations:
- [Cursed](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_(Level)) - Usable by the player and is an effective weapon for defending from the mutants.
- [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level)) - Several of them are found, all are usable but only one is advantageous.
- [Trolley Combat](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Trolley_Combat_(Level)) - Mounted on the [rail car](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rail_Car), as it is the only weapon in the level.

## Trivia

- The achievement's name is referring to the stationary machinegun as it is the heaviest weapon in the game and that it fires the heaviest ammunition.
- The name also refers to the music genre of the same name.
- The achievement icon is a belt of 12.7x108mm rounds which is the type of ammunition that the stationary machinegun fires.
- Vehicles will not count toward this achievement, including the [Panzer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Panzer), the crews in it count though.
