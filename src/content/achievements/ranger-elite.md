**Ranger Elite** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) DLC, [Ranger Mode](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Mode), awarded to the player for completing [Ranger Mode Easy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties).

## Overwiew

This can either be seen as easy or hard depending on the play style of the player, as there is a limited [Heads-Up Display](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heads-Up_Display), ammunition is limited and enemies do massive damage. Playing the game on this difficulty demands a more stealthy approach to the game - going in guns blazing will most likely get you killed quickly. This is most definitely the case on levels where [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) is fighting human enemies and even some where he is on the surface ([Bridge](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bridge) and [Sundown](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sundown) are notable). Simply loading the last level and setting the difficulty to Ranger Mode Easy will not give you the achievement/trophy; you must select new game from the main menu, and select Ranger Easy. However, completing the game on Ranger Hardcore will unlock both this and [Survivalist](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Survivalist).
