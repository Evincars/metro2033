**Ninja **is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) awarded for getting 10 [throwing knife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives) kills in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)), and 30 in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is fairly easy to earn once the player diverts a little attention to it; most humans and virtually all [mutant](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/mutant)s are vulnerable to throwing knives. It is easier to earn this on stealth missions such as [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level)) and [Black Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Station_(Level)) as knives are a one-hit kill on an unsuspecting individual. Note that killing with the secondary attack (stab) also contributes towards this.
