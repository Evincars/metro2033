**Full Strength** is a secret [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained at the end of the game.

## Overview

This achievement is obtained at the end of the game, alongside the [Your Destination](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Your_Destination) achievement, if the player got a "good" ending for [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)), [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)), and [The Taiga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Taiga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)), that is they obtained enough [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points) in each of these chapters so [Duke](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duke) doesn't die, [Damir](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Damir) stays with the crew, and [Alyosha](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alyosha) doesn't get shot.

## Trivia

- This achievement is tied with [Your Destination](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Your_Destination) as being the most valuable achievement in the game, at 60 points. However, it is the harder of the two to get, as the player can lose one crew member and still receive Your Destination, but needs to save all three to receive Full Strength.
