**Weapon Smith** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).

## Owerview

This achievement is awarded to the player for fully customizing a weapon. This can be done relatively easily as early as [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Level)). The game seems a big ambiguous on what [attachments](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/attachments) you need to add, and how many - generally add one scope and all of the other extra attachments you can. For some reason though, the weapon does not need to be fully customised. For example, the [Duplet](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duplet) does not need the Quad Barrel attachment, and no weapons need a laser sight. The best way is to collect the [Lolife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lolife) from [Facility](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Facility) (the one needed for the [A Present](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/A_Present) achievement/trophy) and add on any extra attachments you can.
