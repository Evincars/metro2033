**Survivor 2034** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

When starting a new game, the player can choose between two game modes - Spartan and Survivor. Survivor limits the amount of [ammo](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ammunition) and consumables (such as [medkits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Medkit)) that can be found in the game world and makes stealth more challenging while Spartan mode is more action-oriented and there is plenty of ammo and consumables available. Overall, Survivor requires the player to think and explore more and to play a bit more carefully while Spartan allows them to just go through most situations with guns blazing. Neither of these modes dramatically effect the behavior or intelligence of the AI; such modifications are reserved to the [base difficulty](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties) settings (Normal, Hardcore, Ranger, Ranger Hardcore).

Completing the game in Survivor mode grants the player this achievement, regardless of the chosen difficulty.
