**Complete Road Map** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Overview

After meeting [Giul](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Giul) at the lighthouse, she accompanies Artyom into an underground complex, which serves as a command center of [Kaspik-1](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kaspik-1) satellite array. Artyom's task is to find satellite maps showing levels of radioactivity in [Russia](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Russia) and Central Asia, so that the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) crew can find a place to settle down. The achievement is obtained after finding the maps in the underground base. The complex is fairly linear, so this task should not cause the player any problems, it just takes a bit of time because the player has to fight their way through lots of [spiderbugs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spiderbug) and deal with malfunctioning doors and, in some parts of the base, unbreathable air.
