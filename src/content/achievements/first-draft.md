**First Draft** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).

## Overview

This achievement is unlocked by finding 10 of the 43 [diary pages](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom's_Journal_(Metro_Last_Light)) from any level. A complete guide for all 43 is found on the page for [Published](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Published).
