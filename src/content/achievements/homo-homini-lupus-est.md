**Homo Homini Lupus Est** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Faction Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faction_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing 100 enemies in one playthrough (not cumulative) of the mission, [Heavy Squad](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Squad). The description says 'with heavy weapons', but all three guns [Hans](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hans) uses: the [Gatling](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gatling); the [Hellbreath](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hellbreath_(Volt_Driver)); the [Medved](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Medved) - count as kills. The [tank](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line_Tank) itself does not count as a kill (though the soldiers on it do) and the enemies continually running up the right side of the bridge do not either; only soldiers in the central bridge count. It is advised to take out as many snipers as you can, and delay killing the Gatling-gunner to kill more snipers. Use the Gatling on everyone else. If done correctly, this should unlock during the final wave, before the tank.

## Trivia

*'Homo Homini Lupus Est', as translated from Latin means  'Man is a wolf to his fellow man', alluding to Hans' power and dangerousness or, the ironic fact that humanity still refuses to give up self-destruction even after the world has been destroyed by nuclear war.
- Unfortunately, the achievement appears to be glitched on various platforms, as the achievement generally will not pop after player reached the goal. Player may need to keep replaying this chapter over and over, until it does.
