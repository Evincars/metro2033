**Soft Touch** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) and [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux), awarded for disarming certain amount of [traps](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/traps).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for disarming 10 wire traps in Metro 2033, and 15 in Metro 2033 Redux. Traps can be found throughout the game though there are notable concentrations on the levels [Dead City 1](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_City_1_(Level)), [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level)), and [Outpost](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Outpost_(Level)). Bullet and grenade tripwires or falling log traps are the only traps that can be disarmed and that count towards the achievement/trophy.
