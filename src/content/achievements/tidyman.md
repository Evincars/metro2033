**Tidyman** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Overview

This achievement is obtained for using a total of 500 units of [Chemicals](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Crafting#Chemicals) for cleaning weapons at a [workbench](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/workbench). Chemicals spent on [crafting](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/crafting) do not count towards the progression of this achievement, however, there is a separate achievement called [Handyman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Handyman) for spending 500 resources on crafting.

## Trivia

- A weapon that is completely dirty costs 20 Chemicals to restore to full condition. This means that the player would have to completely clean 25 weapons with zero condition to get this achievement.
- Being outside during a sandstorm in [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) or during a  thunderstorm in other locations, or falling into water in any location significantly worsens weapon's condition. This "trick" can be used when trying to get this achievement, as otherwise, weapons take quite a while to get completely dirty from full condition.
