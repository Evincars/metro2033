**Test Complete** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Tower Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tower_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light), featured by default in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing the [Tower Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tower_Pack_(Level)) level on any difficulty.

## Bugs

You can essentially skip each stage of the level by pressing the mask equip button as soon as the stage starts. Atyom won't equip the mask, but return to the virtual hub after a short time. This makes this achievement the easiest achievment in the game, while also being the rarest achievement among players as well.
