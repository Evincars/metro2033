**Kshatriya** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Faction Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faction_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light), featured by default in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing the mission [Kshatriya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kshatriya_(Level)). It can only be completed by finding and returning the Secret Metro Map to [Shaman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shaman), and then saving the game at [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)), at which point the mission ends. Therefore, players looking to get the [Artifact](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artifact) trophy too should collect all other artifacts before collecting and saving with the map. However, you do not need to have all 30 artifacts to unlock this. The Metro Map is enough.
