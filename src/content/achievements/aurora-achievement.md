**Aurora** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in the level [Winter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Winter_(Metro_Exodus_Level)).

## Overview

This achievement is received shortly after the start of the level, after managing to tune the right frequency on the radio. When this happens, the crew will talk about naming [the locomotive](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) they had taken from [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza), and eventually settle on the name Aurora, hence the description of this achievement. As this is a story mission that is required for completing the level and moving to the next one, this achievement is impossible to miss.
