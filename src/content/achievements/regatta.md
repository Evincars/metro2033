**Regatta** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Overview

This achievement is received shortly after the start of the level, when [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) find a [boat](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rowboat) and Artyom uses it to get to a half-flooded church, inhabited by local [cultists](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Water_Tsar). As this is a story mission that is required for completing the level and moving to the next one, this achievement is impossible to miss. It is probably going to be one of the first achievements unlocked by the player in The Volga.

## Trivia

- The word "regatta" means "a sporting event consisting of a series of boat or yacht races".
