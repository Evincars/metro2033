**Sniper** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) and [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux), awarded for killing certain amount of enemies with headshots.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for getting 25 headshots in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)), and 30 in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux). These must be on human enemies. This can only be completed through the use of firearms - [throwing knives](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives) do not count. This achievement is easiest to complete on [Ranger Mode Easy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties) as most human enemies will go down from a single shot to the head from a [VSV](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/VSV) and it will not alert enemies to your location.
