**New Order** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Overview

This achievement is obtained by completing the level [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) on any difficulty, regardless of the result (based on the collected amount of moral points, there are two possible outcomes. Completing this level with the "good" outcome also grants the player another achievement - [Damir](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Damir_(Achievement))).
