**Firebird** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Overview

The achievement is obtained for killing a [Demon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Demon) with an incendiary weapon - either a [Molotov Cocktail](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Molotov_Cocktail), or the [Tikhar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) loaded with [incendiary capsules](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Incendiary_Capsules).

The easiest way to get this achievement is by throwing a Molotov into the Demon's nest located on top of storage tanks in [Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) where the teddy bear for [Nastya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nastya) is located. If the player approaches this place quietly, the Demon will be resting in his nest, so he can be caught by surprise. Otherwise, Demons are usually flying around and attacking the player from the air, making Molotov kills nearly impossible and making the expensive incendiary capsules for Tikhar the only option.
