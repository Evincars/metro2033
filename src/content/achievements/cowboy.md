**Cowboy** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game))* and *[Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux)*, awarded for obtaining certain amount of kills with the [Revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver).

## Overview

This achievement, in a similar fashion to [Old School](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Old_School) and [Air Gunner](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Air_Gunner), requires the player to kill 30 enemies in *Metro 2033*, and 100 in *Metro 2033 Redux*, both with any variant of the Revolver. The counter includes both [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/:Category:Mutants) and hostile humans. This can be done at any time throughout the game and the player can use any variation of the revolver. This achievement is easiest to earn on [Ranger Mode Easy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties), as one shot to the chest will kill most enemies.
