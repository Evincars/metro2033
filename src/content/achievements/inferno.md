"**Inferno**" is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for setting 2 human enemies on fire at once. You don't have to do this with one [incendiary grenade](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Incendiary_Grenade), you can use two or more of them.

- A good place (and first opportunity) to unlock this is on the level "[Revolution](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolution)", in the [armored train](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armored_Train) yard — wait for the captain to call over a soldier to ask him to "burn the rats". They will be close enough together for an [incendiary grenade](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Incendiary_Grenade) to set them both on fire.
- Other levels that can be used to unlock this achievement are "[Bandits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))", "[Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))", "[Contagion](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Contagion_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))", and "[Depot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Depot_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))". The end of the "[Red Square](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Square_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))" level is too intense, but it is technically possible to unlock the achievement here as well.
- A good place (and last opportunity story-wise) to unlock "Inferno" is on the level "[D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))". Just wait for the first wave of enemies to appear and approach, and throw your grenades next to their hideouts. More precisely, wait until the central cover is used by an enemy, then throw an [incendiary grenade](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Incendiary_Grenade) to the right of his cover — and immediately after that, throw another grenade next to the cover to the right (another enemy should be running here at this moment). With the right timing, you should set two enemies on fire at once. You need to do this quickly, because shortly after the start of the enemy attack, you will be dragged back to the second defensive position.
  - To unlock the achievement, you can try different positions on the "D6" level; just make sure you have at least two incendiary grenades at the time you activate the checkpoint. You should try to unlock it before defensive shield bearers (and flamethrower soldier) come in; if this happened, just restart the checkpoint and try again.
