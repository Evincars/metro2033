**Librarian** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Overview

The achievement is obtained for finding all 70 [Diary entries](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom's_Journal_(Metro_Exodus)), scattered around the post-apocalyptic world. Collected entries can be viewed in a special section in the game menu and can tell the player more about the in-game world, events, characters etc.

This is one of the hardest achievements to obtain, given the large amount of diary pages and the fact that they are fairly easy to miss (but they are usually easier to spot than [postcards](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Postcards)). Plus, the open world sections of the game are quite large, so the search for diary pages can take many hours, if not tens of hours. The video tutorial below can help the player with the search, but only using it as a last resort is recommended.
