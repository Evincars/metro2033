**Spartan 2033 **is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing the game in Spartan Mode. Spartan Mode is similar in playstyle to [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light), with more [ammunition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/ammunition), resources and movement speed, as well as the newer digital [watch](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro-Made_Watch). This must be done in one playthrough.
