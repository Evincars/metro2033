**Spider Hunter **is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing 10 [Spiderbugs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spiderbugs). Spiderbugs are only found in the level, [Caves](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Caves_(Level)), and replace the [plated nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) from the original [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)). The best tactic is to either shine your [flashlight](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Flashlight) on them until they flip belly up, then [knife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom's_Knife) them, or just burn them with a [flamethrower](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/flamethrower).
