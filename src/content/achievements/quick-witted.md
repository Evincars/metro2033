**Quick Witted** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for shooting the chain supporting the chandelier in under 20 seconds of entering the room on the level [Library](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Library_(Level)). Once the player climbs up the fallen bookcase they will find themselves in a medium sized room with stair leading up to another door, quickly look up and shoot the support wire that holds the chandelier. It will have a noticeable gold hue that will show you what to shoot. If done quick enough you'll earn the achievement.
