**Secret** is a hidden [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux) that can be obtained in the level [Depot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Depot_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)).

## Overview

This achievement is earned automatically when the player finishes the level Depot on any [difficulty](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties). It is a story achievement that can not be missed.
