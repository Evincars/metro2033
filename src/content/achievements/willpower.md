**Willpower** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Tower Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tower_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing at least one mission from the [Tower Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Tower_Pack_(Level)) with the an applicable "Huge" modifier (rounds 2, 3, and 4). Huge modifiers are considered more difficult through, as they increase the amount of waves each round will have, by one.
