**Edison** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)* and *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)*.

## Overview

This achievement is unlocked by turning off 40 lights without breaking them - this is done by simply interacting with them when at close proximity. Another way of turning the lights off is by shooting them, doing this 50 times will grant the player a different achievement - [Tesla](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tesla) (note that this achievement doesn't appear in the Redux version of the game).
