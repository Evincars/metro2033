**Pathoanatomist** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game))* and *[Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux)*, awarded for killing 5 [Giant Amoebae](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_Amoeba).

## Overview

In order for the kills to count, Giant Amoebae must be killed by the player, and not by [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller) or by exploding on their own. Giant Amoebae only appear in the lower levels of the [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Metro_2033_Level)) complex, on the way to the [Biomass](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Biomass_(Mutant)). The simplest way to kill them is by using the [revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/revolver) as it is usually a one-hit kill.

## Trivia

- Pathoanatomy is the study of the way disease changes body tissue and organs.
