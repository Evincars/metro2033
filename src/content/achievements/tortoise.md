**Tortoise** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for making 10 [spiderbugs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/spiderbugs) flip over onto their backs, by concentrating the [flashlight](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/flashlight) on them for a few moments. They must be killed for it to count - to save [ammunition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/ammunition), simply keep the flashlight trained on them and they will burn and die, or you can approach them and use the [knife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Trench_Knife) attack to kill them. The first spiderbugs are found on the level [Torchlight](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Torchlight), but there are not usually enough to unlock this. More spiderbugs are found later on in the game.

## Trivia

- The achievement/trophy icon shows a male Spiderbug with its characteristic scorpion-like tail.
