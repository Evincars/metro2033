**Shadow** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for stealth killing 15 human enemies. The 15 kills need to be consecutive, and can be performed with a combination of [throwing knives](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives) and sneak kills using the [knife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Trench_Knife).
