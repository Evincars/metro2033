**Artifact** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Faction Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faction_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for finding all 30 artifacts, including the Secret Metro Map, in [Kshatriya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kshatriya_(Level)). Unlike the [Kshatriya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kshatriya_(Faction_Pack_Achievement)) achievement/trophy, the mission does not need to end - simply find the last artifact and the achievement/trophy should unlock. Consult Kshatriya's wiki page for a walkthrough on the general locations of the 30 artifacts.
