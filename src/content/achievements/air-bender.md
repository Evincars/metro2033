**Air Bender** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game))* and *[Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux)*, awarded for obtaining certain amount of kills with pneumatic weapons.

## Overview

In *Metro 2033*, this achievement is awarded for 30 human kills using pneumatic [weapons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/:Category:Weapons) only, either the [Tihar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) or the [Helsing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Helsing). Obtaining the kills does not have to be consecutive nor with the same weapon. Any kills will be added up over one playthrough. [Front Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Front_Line_(Level)) is an excellent level to accumulate kills, as both of these weapons can be found in the level for free. It can also be earned at the same time as the [Air Gunner](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Air_Gunner) achievement as that requires 30 enemies, human or non-human, to be killed with pneumatic weapons.

In *Metro 2033 Redux*, the required amount of kills was increased to 50 but otherwise, this achievement remains unchanged.
