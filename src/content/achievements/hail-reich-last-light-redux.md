**Hail Reich!** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Faction Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faction_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light), featured by default in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing the mission [Heavy Squad](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Squad). The objective is this mission is simple, the player (as [Hans](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hans)) has to defend [Nazi](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nazi) outpost from several waves of [Reds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line), supported by heavy units, snipers and even [tanks](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line_Tank). This mission is fairly hard even on easier [difficulties](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/difficulties), so this achievement might take a bit of patience to unlock.
