**Marksman** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing 15 humans with headshots at a range of over 30 metres. It is difficult to judge how far 30 metres actually is, so try and headshot enemies from as far away as you can. Moving back the way your ride came in [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level)) and sniping the commissar while he is motivating the troops is a good start. The [Hellbreath](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hellbreath_(Volt_Driver)) and [Tihar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) are great weapons for this. This must be done in one playthrough but you can restart chapters to farm kills.
