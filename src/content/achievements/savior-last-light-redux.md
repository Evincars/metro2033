**Savior** is a hidden [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and Metro Last Light Redux that can be obtained in the level [Contagion](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Contagion).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for taking off their [gasmask](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/gasmask) when [Lesnitsky](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lesnitsky) demands it. This achievement is earned near the end of the level Contagion and can be missed by shooting Lesnitsky instead (which also gives a negative [Moral Point](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Point)).
