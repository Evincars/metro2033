**Survivor** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing the story on [Ranger Mode Easy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties#Ranger_Easy). The game is quite easy as long as the player avoids direct combat with small arms and attempts to avoid most firefights. This achievement can only be obtained once the [Ranger Pack DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Pack) is purchased.

Completing the game on [Ranger Mode Hard](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties#Ranger_Hardcore) will *not* give the player this achievement. Instead, [Last Man Standing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Last_Man_Standing) is awarded.
