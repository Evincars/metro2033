**Exorcist** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game))*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing the levels "[Ghosts](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ghosts_(Level))" and "[Anomaly](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anomaly_(Level))" without dying. This is a relatively easy achievement to earn as all the player needs to do is just do as [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)) says, and for Anomaly, on most [difficulties](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/difficulties) the player will not even need to engage in combat, as Khan can usually take care of the [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/nosalises) for the player.

## Trivia

- An exorcist is a person who believes that they can drive away evil spirits and ghosts, through prayer or religion. This is most likely a reference to Khan.﻿
- The icon and name of this achievement could also be a reference to the popular horror film *The Exorcist*.
