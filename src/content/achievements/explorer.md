**Explorer** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for entering as many locations as possible in the [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Metro_System). This includes every [moral point](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points) boosting area consisting of everything from small rooms in the metro tunnels to houses and apartments on [the surface](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow). Visit the [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points) article for locations and how-tos of every moral point in the game.
