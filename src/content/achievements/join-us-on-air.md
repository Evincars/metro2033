**Join Us on Air** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in one of the intermission levels set onboard [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora), these being [Winter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Winter_(Metro_Exodus_Level)), [Spring](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spring_(Metro_Exodus_Level)), [Summer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Summer_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) and [Autumn](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Autumn_(Metro_Exodus_Level)).

## Overview

In the level Winter, the player is required to tune the radio by turning the frequency up and down until a transmission from [The Ark](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ark) is received. However, the radio can later be accessed again in any of the Aurora intermissions and it is possible to tune to a frequency where songs start playing. Doing so will reward the player with this achievement.
