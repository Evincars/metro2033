**Warrior **is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing 100 human enemies. This will likely unlock naturally through gameplay, but you can reload chapters to farm kills - in [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level)), human enemies are particularly abundant. However it must be completed in one playthrough.
