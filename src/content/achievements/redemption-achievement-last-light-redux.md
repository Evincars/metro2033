**Redemption** is a hidden [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux) that can be obtained at the end of the game.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to players who have enough positive [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Point), and will receive the ['Redemption' ending](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Endings). A lot of positive moral points are required for this ending, meaning the player will have to go out of their way to hunt for them - killing either of the [two](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov) [villains](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lesnitsky) will usually void this and give you the [C'est la Vie](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/C'est_la_Vie_(Achievement)) ending. It is also the canonical ending for the game and in the books, as *[Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035)* and [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) follow on from this.
