**Rabbit** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for completing the training sequence at [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)) during the level [Sparta](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sparta_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)). This achievement can not be missed and will probably be one of the first achievements unlocked by the player.
