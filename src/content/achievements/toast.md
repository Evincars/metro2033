**Toast!** is a hidden [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) for Xbox One and regular trophy for PS4 in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for drinking at every occasion. You must drink once at the bar in [Market](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_(Metro_2033_Level)), and twice in [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Metro_2033_Level)), for this to unlock. In the PC version, you can unlock it by drinking three times in Polis; this can also be achieved on the PS4 version. This must be completed in one playthrough.
