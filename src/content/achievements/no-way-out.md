**No Way Out** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Chronicles Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chronicles_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light), featured by default in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded simply for completing the [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Chronicles_Pack_Level)) level on any difficulty.
This is a moderately long level with [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan) and [Ulman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ulman) passing through [Polyanka](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polyanka), an abandoned station notoriously known for the visions it causes to those who visit it.
