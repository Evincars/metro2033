**Metro Trader** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) and [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux), awarded for making a certain amount of deals (buying/selling [weapons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/weapons) and equipment at [weapon vendors](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_Vendors)).

## Overview

In order to get this achievement, the player has to make 10 deals in Metro 2033 and 30 deals in Metro 2033 Redux. Purchasing or selling [weapons](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/weapons) or equipment (e.g., [filters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/filters), [grenade](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/grenade)s and [medkit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/medkit)s) counts towards this achievement, while trading [ammunition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/ammunition) does not count.

The player can obtain this achievement at any time during the missions [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) and [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)), since there are no more vendors after the aforementioned stations. In the Redux version, trades from Metro 2033 are combined with trades from Metro: Last Light. However, it is not difficult to get it by playing Metro 2033 alone.
