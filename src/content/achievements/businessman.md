**Businessman** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for making 100 purchases at weapon or equipment vendors. It can be easily gained at any equipment vendor on any normal difficulty ([Ranger Mode](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Mode#Ranger_Mode) increases the buying price of everything) by repeatedly buying and selling [throwing knifes](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives), since they are bought and sold for 1 [military-grade rounds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR) each, therefore no MGR is lost. If done legitimately, you will not achieve this until far into the campaign, if at all, without making regular purchases whenever you can.
