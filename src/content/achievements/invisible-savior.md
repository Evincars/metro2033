**Invisible Savior** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)* and *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)* that can be obtained in the level "[Facility](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Facility)".

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing "[Facility](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Facility_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))" without being caught, raising any [alarms](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alarm_Systems) or killing anyone. If you fail in any way, you have to restart the level from the beginning (restarting is not necessary in Redux, reloading the last checkpoint is enough).
