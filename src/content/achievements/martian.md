"**Martian**" is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*.

## Overview

The achievement is obtained by patching up your gas mask with duct tape after it's heavily damaged (you can tell this by the appearance of a hole on the left side of the glass). The easiest way and the earliest opportunity to obtain it is during the level "[Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_(Metro_Exodus_Level))", since the player will be engaged in firefights while wearing the gas mask.

If you want to unlock this achievement, do not under any circumstances use the [armored glass](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gas_Mask#Upgrades) upgrade from the "[Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level))" level. Otherwise, you will most likely get the achievement in a natural way.

## Trivia

- This achievement is an obvious refence to *[The Martian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/wikipedia:The_Martian_(film))*, a movie based on the [novel of the same name](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/wikipedia:The_Martian_(Weir_novel)), written by [Andy Weir](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/wikipedia:Andy_Weir). In the movie, the main protagonist finds himself standed on [Mars](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/wikipedia:Mars) and during a dust storm, his space suit's visor gets damaged. He repairs the visor with a duct tape, same way as Artyom can temporarily fix his gas mask in the game.
