**Lower the Bridge** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Overview

This achievement is obtained by completing the level [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) on any difficulty, regardless of the result (based on the collected amount of moral points, there are two possible outcomes. Completing this level with the "good" outcome also grants the player another achievement - [Duke](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duke_(Achievement))).

## Trivia

- The icon of this achievement shows the symbol of [Church of the Water Tsar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Water_Tsar), technophobic cultists who serve as the main antagonists in this level. This symbol can be seen in numerous places throughout the level, most notably at the entrance to the flooded terminal.
