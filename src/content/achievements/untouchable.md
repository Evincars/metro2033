## Overview

This achievement can be obtained on Normal or higher [difficulty](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties) by fighting off the [Batwing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Demon) in three encounters without dying once. The achievement will not pop up until Sam's Story has been completed.

The first encounter takes place at the very beginning of the level, after meeting [the Captain](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Eduard_Baranov). [Sam](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam) will be trapped on a small platform with a train car in the middle, that will provide decent cover for the Batwing's attacks.

The second encounter takes place in a scrap yard filled with old cars. The arena can be accessed at any moment after leaving the Captain's base. Every time the Batwing takes enough damage, it will drop behind a pile of cars, and it will push those cars foward, making the arena a little smaller. The Batwing will do this four times before being defeated, and it can insta-kill the player.

The third encounter takes place right after the fire station segment. The Batwing now lacks its wings after a group of [watcher](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/watcher)s devoured them in the previous encounter. Because of this, it will resort to run around behind the trees of the arena, out of your view, then reveal itself before charging you. You can track the batwing by the movement of the trees.
