**Realist** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing [Riga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_(Level)) without giving away any [military grade rounds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR). Players can achieve this by not giving any to the child that leads the player to [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) and not giving any bullets to the many beggars in the area. Interacting with [Nikki](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nikki) does not seem to affect this achievement.
