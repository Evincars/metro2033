**Platinum Trophy** is a [trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) (so it's unique to Playstation and doesn't appear in Xbox and PC versions) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux), [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light), [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux) and [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Overview

In all games, this trophy is rewarded for unlocking all other trophies in that respective game. After unlocking your last regular trophy, the Platinum trophy should unlock soon after. Trophies featured in DLCs are not required to be unlocked to receive this trophy (however, trophies that are considered DLC in Metro Last Light are considered regular trophies in Last Light Redux, because [Metro Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Redux) includes all previously released downloadable content by default.)

You can find a detailed list of trophies for each game at the [Achievements and Trophies](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) page or in navigation boxes below. You can use the information to create a "checklist" of trophies that you still need to unlock if you are willing to obtain the Platinum trophy.
