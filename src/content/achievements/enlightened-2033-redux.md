**Enlightened** is a hidden [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) and regular achievement/hidden trophy in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux), that can be obtained at the end of the game.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for sparing the [Dark Ones](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Ones) at the [end of the game](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tower_(Level)) in the non-canonical [enlightened ending](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Endings). To receive this ending, the player must acquire enough positive [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points) for Artyom to awaken from his dream sequence in time to stop the missiles from destroying the Dark Ones "city" (nest) at [Botanicheskiy Sad](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Botanicheskiy_Sad). Once he has awoken, a Dark One will tell him, "We want peace." Another will be reaching for the missile guidance system - shoot the device to stop the missiles, saving the Dark Ones. If you do not have any ammo left, you can walk right to the guidance system and press *Use* to drop it down from the tower.
