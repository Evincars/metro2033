## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for killing at least one enemy with every single weapon available in the game. That includes all guns, your knife, [throwing knives](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives), [claymore mines](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Claymore_Mine) and the two types of [grenade](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/grenade). It can be a kill on an enemy human or a [mutant](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/mutant). Note that all small non-hostile mutants, such as [mutant crows](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutant_crow) and [hatchling shrimp](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shrimp) do not count - due to a glitch, [Spiderbugs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spiderbugs) do not count either(might because it's hard to know that it's killed by your light or your gun). Below is a written guide for a foolproof way of getting this achievement/trophy without missing a weapon. Alternatively, refer to the [list of Last Light weapons](http://metrovideogame.wikia.com/wiki/Template:Weapons_Of_Metro_Last_Light) as a checklist of weapons. Note that DLC weapons, including the [RPK-74](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/RPK-74) and the [Abzats Shotgun](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Automatic_Shotgun_(Abzats)), [VSSK Vychlop](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Clapper) and the [DShK 12.7mm Machinegun](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DShK_12.7mm_Machinegun) do not count.

## Location Guide

The following guide is for [Ranger Mode](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Mode) (2 guns), attempting [Shadow Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Ranger), [Invisible Intruder](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Invisible_Intruder), [Invisible Man](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Invisible_Man), [Invisible Savior](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Invisible_Savior), [Invisible Soldier](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Invisible_Soldier) and [Rain Man](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rain_Man) achievements/trophies, and only using weapons found in the Metro, none that have been bought. The guide contains spoilers.

### Sparta)

Take the [Valve](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Valve) and [Revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver) with you to the surface.

### Ashes

Kill the [Watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchmen) with the Revolver and Valve.

### Facility

You could pick [Ashot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashot) from one of the enemies on second floor. The gun about [A Present](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/A_Present) is [Lolife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashot).

### Torchlight

Head down tracks & check body on left side of tracks there is an [Lolife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashot) with an extended barrel. After the elevator, when you jump down, [Ashot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ashot) is on you left body. Kill [Spiderbugs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spiderbugs) by gun could be counted, but might have glitch.

### Echoes

Do not get the [Duplet](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duplet) here, just stick with the Lolife and Ashot to kill the Watchmen .

### Revolution

Check the locker when you get down: there is a [Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_(AK-74M)) here if you look carefully. Inside the room where the workers are doing a service check, there is a [Tikhar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) up the ladder on the right as you enter, follow the pipe above the soldiers and turn left, it is located at the end of the catwalk - pick it up.

### Regina)

Inside the flooded room to the left there is a [Bastard](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bastard). Swap it with the Tikhar.

Keep going. Just before where you need to push the abandoned metro trains with Regina, to the right there is a room (a Watchman will run into this room when you approach, so look for that). Kill the Watchmen here with the Kalash and Bastard. Inside the room there is a [Shambler](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shambler). Backtrack to get the Tikhar.

Keep going. You will be attacked by Watchmen from behind - kill them with Tikhar and Shambler, and wait for one to jump on [Regina](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Regina_(Vehicle)) to knife it.

### Bandits

Near the end where you have to ring the bell for the boat there is a quad-barrel [Duplet](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duplet) up stairs - use it to defend against the [Nosalis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) while waiting for the boat.

### Sundown

Use [grenades](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Grenade), [incendiary grenades](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Incendiary_Grenade) and [Claymore Mine](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Claymore_Mine)s to kill the [shrimp](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/shrimp). Incendiary grenades will instantly kill any shrimp. The big Shrimp will take more than a grenade to take down so fire at it first and lay down Claymores while backpedalling.

Pick up the [Kalash 2012](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_2012) here in a house near the red missile.

### Nightfall

Kill the Watchmen here with the Kalash 2012.

### Undercity

In a room where there are a few Nosalis eating the bodies, look up - there are Winged Nosalis hanging bat-like to the ceiling. One [throwing knife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives) to the torso should kill it silently.

### Contagion

Get into toxic area, go into area where incendiary grenades are been thrown into stalls one enemy has a  [Saiga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Saiga-12).(He will keep knocking on the door, and after opening it he will stay on the first floor of the train.)  Enter toxic area & go up ladders to enter a kitchen with multiple bodies, check table for a [Helsing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Helsing).

### Khan)

After you cross the pipe, you and Khan have to face monsters, kill them with the Saiga and Helsing.

### The Crossing

Kill the Watchmen here with the Saiga and Helsing. Pick up the [VSV](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/VSV) near the ladder toward the end.

Alternative can be found on Bridge chapter where Moral Point 2 is.  Once on actual bridge go part way across bridge & look on left side to see a ramp down.  Go down & to right, on a body.  See also [VSV (VSK-94)](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/VSV_(VSK-94))

### The Dead City/Walkthrough)

When you see the vision of being hit by a car, kill the Watchman with the VSV. Immediately walk to the left and into the house. There is a corpse clutching a [Preved](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Preved) in the bath tub on the second floor. Kill the Watchmen here with the Preved.

### D6)

As soon as [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) has access to use the [Gatling](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gatling) you must kill regular soldiers *before *the riot shield guard and [flamethrower heavy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Flamethrower), as they do not count as a kill. As soon as you kill one soldier with the Gatling, the achievement/trophy will unlock.
