**Invisible Soldier** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux) that can be obtained in the level [Revolution](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolution_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to players that complete Revolution without being caught, raising any [alarms](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alarm_Systems) or killing anyone. If you fail in any way, you must restart the level from the beginning.
