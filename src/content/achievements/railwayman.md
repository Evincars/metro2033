**Railwayman** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Overview

After navigating through the half-flooded warehouse that the [cultists](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Water_Tsar) use to get rid of technological devices, the player will find [Krest's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Krest) [railcar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Krest's_Railcar), a railway vehicle that they need in order to pull a [passenger carriage](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora's_Passenger_Train_Car) to the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora). The achievement is obtained after first entering the railcar and using the circuit breaker received from Krest to start the engine. This task is story-related and has to be completed in order to progress to the next level, so this achievement can not be missed.
