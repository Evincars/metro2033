**Pyromaniac** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded after burning 50 cobwebs. This is done by approaching a cobweb while holding a lit [lighter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lighter). Burning cobwebs in confined areas is a good idea, as they slow the player down and make them an easier target for [Spiderbugs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spiderbugs) or other enemies.
