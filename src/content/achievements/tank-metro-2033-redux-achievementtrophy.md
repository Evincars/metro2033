**Tank **is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing 10 enemies in a row without taking damage. This is incredibly easy to earn if you kill 10 enemies stealthily. You can also kill one enemy, then reload the checkpoint and kill him again, repeating up to 10 kills.
