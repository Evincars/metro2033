## Overview

In the third part of the story, a [Blind One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Blind_Ones) is encountered and must be dealt with. The mutant will throw various objects at the player, including [Nosalis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) mutants. Dealing with the Blind One while avoiding all throwing attacks without receiving a single hit grants the player this achievement.
