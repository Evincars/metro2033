**Handyman** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Overview

This achievement is obtained for using a total of 500 units of crafting resources ([Materials](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Crafting#Materials) and/or [Chemicals](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Crafting#Chemicals)) to manufacture any craftable item ([medkits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Medkit), [filters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/filters), [ammo](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ammunition) or throwables) at a [workbench](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/workbench) or using Artyom's [backpack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/backpack). Resources spent on repairing the [gas mask](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/gas_mask) or cleaning weapons do not count towards the progression of this achievement, however, there is a separate achievement called [Tidyman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tidyman) for spending 500 Chemicals on cleaning weapons.
