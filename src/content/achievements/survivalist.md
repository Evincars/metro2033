**Survivalist** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) DLC, [Ranger Mode](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Mode), awarded to the player after completing the story on [Ranger Mode Hardcore](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties).

## Overview

Getting this achievement can either be seen as easy or hard depending on the play style of the player, as there is no [HUD](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heads-Up_Display), ammunition is limited and enemies do massive damage. Playing the game on this difficulty demands a more stealthy approach to the game - going in guns blazing will most likely get you killed quickly. This is most definitely the case on levels where [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) is fighting human enemies and even some where he is on the surface ([Bridge](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bridge) and [Sundown](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sundown) are notable). Simply loading the last level and setting the difficulty to Ranger Mode Hardcore will not give you the achievement/trophy; you must select new game from the main menu, and select Ranger Hardcore.

## Bugs

- Previously, downloading Ranger Mode and beating it would not reward you with the achievement/trophy. The offical fix was to uninstall and re-install the DLC, then replay [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)) to unlock it.
  - This bug has now been fixed in a patch - the achievement/trophy should unlock normally after completing D6.

## Trivia

- Earning this will also earn the [Ranger Elite](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Elite) achievement/trophy by default.
