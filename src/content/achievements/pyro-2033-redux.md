**Pyro** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)) and [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux), awarded for obtaining certain amount of kills with the [Flamethrower](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Flamethrower).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for 5 flamethrower kills in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)), and 30 in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux). In Metro 2033, the flamethrower is only found on [Dark Star](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Star_(Level)) - [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and the other [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) are attacked by Nosalises, so this is easy to unlock. In Metro 2033 Redux, a portable version of the flamethrower exists (similar to the one from [Spider Lair](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_Lair)) and it carries over to [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Metro_2033_Level)) as well.
