## Overview

The achievement is obtained for finding all 12 upgrades for [Artyom's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) [suit](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armor). All suit upgrades can be found in [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) and [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) - two open-world levels of the game.
