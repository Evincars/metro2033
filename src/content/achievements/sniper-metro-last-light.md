**Sniper** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in the [Faction Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Faction_Pack), a [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) for [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing 10 enemies with headshots in the mission [Sniper Team](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sniper_Team). This can only be done with the [Clapper](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Clapper_(VSSK_Vychlop)) but the kills do not have to be consecutive. An enemy can also be injured in the body, as long as the killing blow is a headshot, for it to count.
