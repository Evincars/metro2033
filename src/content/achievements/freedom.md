**Freedom!** is a secret [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)* and a regular achievement/trophy in *[Metro: Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light_Redux)* that can be obtained in the level "[Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Level))".

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for releasing the prisoners from their cells in the concentration camp in the chapter "Pavel". Simply activate the lever in the control room before opening the door [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Red_Line)) asks you to open. Doing so will also garner the player a positive [Moral Point](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Point).
