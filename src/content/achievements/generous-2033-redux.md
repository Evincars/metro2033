**Generous** is a secret [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)), and a regular achievement/secret trophy in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for being generous to Metro dwellers and donating [bullets](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR) when asked. You need to give a bullet to three different people: a boy, a man and a vagrant:
- The man and his wife, in the [Exhibition](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Metro_2033_Level)) marketplace, need two bullets to pay for medicine for their son.
- A boy will escort you to [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) on the Black Street in return for a bullet, in [Riga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_(Metro_2033_Level)).
- There is a vagrant near to where the boy was, sitting on some stairs, who asks for a bullet.
