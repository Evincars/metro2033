## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing 30 enemies with [incendiary grenades](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Incendiary_Grenade). Any enemies, mutant or human will count. All 30 must be killed in one playthrough, but you can restart chapters to farm more kills without resetting the counter.
