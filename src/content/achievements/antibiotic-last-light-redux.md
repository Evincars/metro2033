**Antibiotic** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light), [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux) and [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) awarded to the player for killing a certain amount of [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/mutants).

## Overview

### Metro Last Light & Redux

In order to receive this achievements, one has to kill 100 mutants. The player will likely unlock this achievement on their first playthrough. Small, non-hostile mutants, such as [Mutant Crows](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutant_Crow) and [Shrimp Hatchlings](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shrimp) as well as mutants in [downloadable content](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Downloadable_Content) do not count towards the achievement.

### Metro Exodus

In Metro Exodus, the required number of kills has been increased to 300 mutants, given the fact that the game is longer and features open environments. Same as before, small, non-hostile mutants, such as [Mutant Crows](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutant_Crow) and [Shrimp Hatchlings](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shrimp#Hatchling_Shrimp) do not count towards the achievement, neither do mutated plants.
