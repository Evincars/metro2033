- [The Rangers of the Order](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Rangers) - [Polis'](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)) elite military force in the [Metro video game series](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Video_Game_Series), ensured with the safety of the [metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/metro).
- [Ranger Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Pack) - [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) available for the [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)), featuring the HUD-less Ranger modes.
- [Ranger (Achievement/trophy)](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_(Achievement/trophy)) - An [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) awarded for finding all Ranger stashes in  [Dead City 1](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_City_1_(Metro_2033_Level)) and [2](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_City_2_(Metro_2033_Level)).
