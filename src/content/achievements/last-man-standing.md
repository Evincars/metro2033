**Last Man Standing** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game))*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing the story on [Ranger Mode Hardcore](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties). This can only be obtained once the [Ranger Pack](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Pack) [DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/DLC) is purchased. This achievement can be either seen as easy to hard depending on the play style. Playing the game on this difficulty demands a more stealthy approach to the game, which means that purchasing the stealth suit on the mission "[Armory](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_(Level))" is a good idea. This is most definitely the case on levels where [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) is fighting human enemies, such as "[Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level))". Simply loading the last level and setting the difficulty to Ranger Mode Hardcore will *not* give you the achievement; you have to play the entire game on that difficulty. Furthermore, earning this does *not *automatically earn the [Survivor](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Survivor_(Achievement)) achievement.
