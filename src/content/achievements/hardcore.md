**Hardcore** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Overview

Probably the most prestigious and also the hardest trophy to receive, in order to get this achievement, the player needs to complete the game on Ranger Hardcore, the hardest difficulty in the game.

Ranger Hardcore makes enemies much stronger and enhances their senses, allowing them to see the player at longer distances. [Ammo](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ammunition) and [crafting resources](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Crafting#Crafting_Resources) are very limited and the player loses health extremely quickly when shot at or attacked by [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/mutants). Furthermore, most HUD elements are removed and the manual save feature becomes unavailable. Finishing the game on this difficulty is an ultimate challenge that requires a lot of skill and patience.
