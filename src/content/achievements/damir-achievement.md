**Damir** is a secret [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Overview

This achievement is obtained if [Damir](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Damir) accompanies you back to the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) at the end of The Caspian instead of staying with [Giul](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Giul), which is achieved by acquiring enough [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points) in the level. This can be done by avoiding killing too many slaves and/or freeing them.
