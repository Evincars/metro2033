**Sterling Effort** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing a total of 50 [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/mutants) with the [trench knife](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Trench_Knife). This can be a rather tricky achievement to earn as the knife requires a few hits to kill a mutant and whilst attempting to kill one with it can leave the player rather defenseless. Kills with the alternate attack of the [throwing knives](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives) do not count towards this achievement.

On the level [Cursed](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed) waves of mutants will rush a central point non stop as long as you do not complete any of the mission's objectives, which allows you to kill 50 mutants quite easily.
