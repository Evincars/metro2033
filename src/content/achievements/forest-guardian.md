**Forest Guardian** is a secret [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and a regular achievement/trophy in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux) that can be obtained in the level [The Garden](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Garden_(Level)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for saving the [giant bear](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bear), from the [Watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchmen), in The Garden. After fighting the Bear and injuring it enough, it will run away through the bushes. Follow it and you will find it being attacked by Watchmen, with its two cubs nearby calling for their mother. Simply shoot off the Watchmen on its back. The Bear will recover and look at you, but walk off with its cubs. The achievement/trophy will unlock soon after. It also gives a positive [moral point](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Point).
