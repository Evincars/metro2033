**Big Momma** is a hidden [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and a regular achievement/trophy in [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux) that can be obtained in the level [Undercity](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Undercity).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to players who kill the [Rhino](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rhino) - the boss of the Undercity level. It is a story achievement and cannot be missed.
