**No Shooting Allowed** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player if they kill 10 enemies in a row with [throwing knives](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Knives). It is very easy to do on any human stealth section, but early levels, like [Facility](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Facility) or [Separation](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Separation), are best as the enemies do not wear armour. The kills do not have to be stealthy, but, obviously, it will make the achievement/trophy easier to unlock if they are. Unless you do this on [Ranger Mode](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Mode), you will not be able to carry 10 throwing knives at once, so you will have to retrieve them from bodies.
