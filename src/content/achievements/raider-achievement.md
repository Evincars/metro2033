## Overview

This achievement is awarded for stealthily entering the first [Nazi](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nazi) occupied room in the level Depot. This can be accomplished by killing the first guard before he gets near the room and alert the others. Although you should do this stealthily, it can also be done with a non-suppressed weapon as long as the player kills the guard quickly. A good indicator if the player is too late or not is that the soldier has not shouted and entered the station.
