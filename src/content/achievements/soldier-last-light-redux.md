**Soldier** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for killing 100 human enemies. The player may use any [weapon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons) to complete this achievement and will likely unlock this on their first playthrough if they are not attempting to unlock the [Shadow Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Ranger) achievement.
