**First Blood** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded when the damage indicator (seen as bloodstains on the screen) has been triggered for the first time. This is caused by enemies wounding [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom).

It is usually earned in the [Prologue](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_(Level)) level, which means this is generally the first achievement players will get.
