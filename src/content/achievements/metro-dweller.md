**Metro Dweller** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for speaking or listening to everyone in a single station, such as [Riga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_Station_(Location)) or [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_Location). Players can do this by speaking repeatedly with people, by knocking on doors, and just standing by a person (or multiple people) when they talk. Contrary to popular belief, and what one would assume from the achievement's description, the player does not have to venture out of even [Exhibiton](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition) (recommended) to gain this achievement, as players have gained it the first time playing through the level. At least on PC, players will need to speak and listen to everything in Exhibition, from [Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Ranger)), all the way to the bar in Riga, where the achievement will unlock upon listening to a few people in the bar.
