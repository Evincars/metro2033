**Musician** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for finding and playing all 17 musical instruments in the game. There are only four types - guitars, accordions, balalaikas (triangle shaped guitar) and pianos. There are a total of 17 musical instruments in the game throughout the levels. All 17 must be played in one playthrough, in level order. If you miss an instrument, you may use level select, but you must then play the game and collect all instruments in that level and all other levels after that one again. You cannot use level select to only play ones you missed, unlike [diary pages](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom's_Journal_(Metro_Last_Light)). Below is a complete list of all 17 instruments.

## Location Guide

### Sparta)

#### Guitar

Location: In the level [Sparta](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sparta_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)), when you awake from your dream, the guitar will be sitting on a chair in the room.

#### Balalaika

Location: Before your talk with the armorer, play the Balalaika on the chair in the lounge.

### Facility)

#### Accordion

Location:  In the level [Facility](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Facility), during the first main area, where you see the soldier being brutalized by his allies. The accordion is on a stool by a table and some bunks.

### Bolshoi)

#### Balalaika

Location: In the level [Bolshoi](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bolshoi), right at the start you will spot a man and woman talking on a bench. The instrument is right beside them on the ground.

#### Guitar

Location: In the section before reaching the marketplace, it is leaning against a chair; directly before the juggeler and the shadow artist.

#### Balalaika

Location: It is lying on the last market stall on the left, by the entrance to the theatre area.

#### Balalaika

Location: After watching the show, before sitting down to drink, it is on the floor just to the right of the table.

### Revolution)

#### Balalaika

Location: In the level [Revolution](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolution), head down the stairs and you will spot the instrument behind a desk where the note is located.

#### Piano

Location: After the enemies in the large area with the metal train, pull the lever on the right and head through the large door. The piano is in the next room with a soldier sitting in front of it.

#### Accordion

Location: Open the single locker found inside the corridor. This is immediately before the door to the rail tunnel (at the end of the warehouse) but after crawling through the fan.

### Regina)

#### Accordion

Location: In the level [Regina](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Regina_(Level)), when you start driving Regina, stop at the first room on the left and at the back. Head up stairs to find the accordion.

### Undercity)

#### Balalaika

Location: In the level [Undercity](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Undercity), it is right in the room where you wake up.

### Contagion)

#### Guitar

Location: In the level [Contagion](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Contagion), a guitar is in the leftmost room of the ground-floor homes on the right as you enter the first burning section of the station.

#### Guitar

Location: Enter the market room on the left where the Red soldiers are tossing incendiary grenades into the shops and play the guitar behind the centre counter.

#### Guitar

Location: A guitar is sitting on an armchair at the very end of the first-floor carriage homes just before the jump to the otherside of the gate.

### Quarantine)

#### Guitar

Location: In the level [Quarantine](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Quarantine), after entering the red door with [Khan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Khan_(Character)), you will see the guitar on the left against the wall. Note that this guitar is picked up by a [Ranger](http://metrovideogame.wikia.com/wiki/Rangers), after their conversation, and will be unplayable, so play it first.

### The Dead City)

#### Piano

Location: In the level [The Dead City](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dead_City_(Metro_Last_Light_Level)), after going underground, take the first exit to go back above ground and enter the building there.
