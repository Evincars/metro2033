**Watchman Hunter **is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

This achievement is awarded for killing 50 [Watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchmen). There are well over 50 Watchmen in the campaign, so this will be easy to unlock. If you do not get 50 kills, you can reload a chapter to farm kills. Watchmen are usually found on the surface, as they are basically the surface counterpart of the [Nosalis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis), so playing levels that take part on the surface is recommended if one wishes to receive this achievement.
