**Mouse** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and [Metro Last Light Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light_Redux) that can be obtained in the level [Echoes](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Echoes).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing Echoes without being caught by the pack of [watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/watchmen). However, the achievement/trophy can be unlocked after you find the [Duplet](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duplet) in a side passage, and are ambushed by a watchman. After leaving this area, follow [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov) up the stairs until he asks you to stop. A pack of watchmen will run across the landscape. As long as you keep still, they will not see you, you will gain [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points) and unlock the achievement/trophy. However, if you move too much or attack them, Pavel will complain, you will lose moral points and the pack will attack.
