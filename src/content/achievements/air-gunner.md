**Air Gunner** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)), awarded for obtaining 30 kills with pneumatic weapons, either the [Tihar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tihar) or [Helsing](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Helsing).

## Overview

A good place to get this achievement is in the mission [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level)). Just past the [Reds'](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) outpost at the beginning of the level will be a destroyed metro train, inside the player will find a Tihar, which is a perfect gun for this mission; effective and silent, and if players plan ahead, can be abundant in ammo after [Armory](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_(Level)). There is a hidden Helsing in [Dead City 1](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_City_1), past the [demon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/demon) feeding on a corpse near the end of the mission. Using this Helsing during [Dead City 2](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_City_2) can also help the player get the achievement. A Tihar can also be found in a garbage bin about halfway through Dead City 2 or in the secret areas in Dead City 1. If the player hasn't had the chance to find and use a pneumatic weapon in the places listed above, a good idea is to acquire/buy one before going to the [library](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Library_(Level)), preferably a Helsing, since they are very effective on [librarian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/librarian)s and there are quite a few librarians on this mission. As an added bonus, the player will also earn the [Heavy Reader](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Reader) achievement by killing a single librarian. Black Station also has a Tihar hidden in a wooden box, and many Nazis to use it on.
