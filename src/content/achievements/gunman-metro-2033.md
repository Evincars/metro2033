**Gunman** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)), awarded for obtaining a total of 50 kills with the [Heavy Automatic Shotgun](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_Automatic_Shotgun).

## Overview

It is only possible to obtain this achievement after the [Ranger Pack DLC](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ranger_Pack) has been purchased. The player can obtain a Heavy Automatic Shotgun on the level [Armory](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_(Level)) which is extremely helpful if the player decides to go head first into firefights in the next level [Frontline](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Frontline_(Level)) where there are lots of enemies that can swarm the player after a general alarm has been raised. The other place where this can be obtained is once the player reaches the level [Sparta](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sparta_Base_(Location)), which proves more than useful against the hoards of mutants that the player will face in the level [Tower](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tower). This achievement differs from the [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux) achievement/trophy, [Gunman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gunman_(Metro_2033_Redux)), awarded for kiling 100 enemies with *any* shotgun.
