**Old School** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game))*.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing 30 enemies with the [Duplet](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duplet). This counter includes [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/:Category:Mutants) and hostile humans. The achievement is not difficult to earn, as the double barreled shotgun is gained very early in the game, and on most [difficulties](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/difficulties) it can kill most enemies in one shot at close range. If the player uses Duplet with frequency in the 2nd (Bourbon) and the 3rd (Khan) chapters, the achievement is easily earned.
