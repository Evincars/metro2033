**Revenge** is a hidden [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light) and Metro Last Light Redux that can be obtained in the [Red Square](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Square) level.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded to the player for leaving [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov) to die during Red Square. As you can choose whether or not to let Pavel die, this achievement/trophy is missable. It also garners the player a lot of negative [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Point) that will likely give the player the [C'est la Vie](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/C'est_la_Vie_(Achievement)) [ending](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Endings), so think thorougly what consequences your actions might have before deciding Pavel's fate.
