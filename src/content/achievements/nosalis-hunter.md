**Nosalis Hunter** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) awarded for killing 30 [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/nosalises) in [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)), and 100 in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

Nosalises are encountered very often in the game so this is quite easy to obtain. It must be done in a single playthrough of the story; starting a new game will reset the counter, but you can restart chapters to farm kills.
