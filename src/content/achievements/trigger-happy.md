**Trigger Happy **is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux).

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for killing 100 enemies with assault rifles. Humans and [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/mutants) both count towards this. The [Bastard](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bastard), [Kalash](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash) and [Kalash 2012](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kalash_2012) all count. This must be completed in one playthrough, but you can restart chapters to farm kills.

## Trivia

- The background associated with this achievement shows a [Gatling](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gatling), even though it is not classified as an assault rifle and thus unrelated to this achievement.
