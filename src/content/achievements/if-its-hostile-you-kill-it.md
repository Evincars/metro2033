**If It's Hostile, You Kill It** is an [achievement/trophy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game))* and *[Metro 2033 Redux](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_Redux)* that can be obtained at the end of the game.

## Overview

This achievement is awarded for completing the game on any [difficulty](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/difficulties) with the standard [Ranger ending](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Endings#Ranger_-_Destroy_the_Dark_Ones), or by not destroying the missile guidance system during the otherwise [Enlightened ending](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Endings#Enlightened_-_Spare_the_Dark_Ones). The standard ending is earned by default if the player does not have a large number of [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points) and will likely be unlocked before [Enlightened](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Enlightened_(Achievement)) when playing through normally.

## Trivia

- The name of this achievement is a direct reference to the [Ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Rangers) code, which is reiterated by [Hunter](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_(Ranger)) when he throws [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) his [revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/revolver) in the chapter "[Ethereal](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ethereal_(Level))".
