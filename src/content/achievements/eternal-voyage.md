**Eternal Voyage** is a secret [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained at the end of the game.

## Overview

This achievement is obtained at the end of the game if the player got a "bad" ending, which is triggered if they got a "bad" ending for two or three of the listed chapters: [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)), [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)), and [The Taiga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Taiga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)). If they don't obtain enough [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points) for two of these chapters, the "bad" ending is triggered.

If the player accumulates enough moral points and receives the good ending, a more valuable achievement - [Your Destination](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Your_Destination) - is awarded instead.
