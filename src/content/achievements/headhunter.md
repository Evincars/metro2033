**Headhunter** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus).

## Overview

The achievement is obtained for killing 300 human enemies, such as [Bandits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits), [Cultists](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Water_Tsar) or [Gangsters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Munai-bailer). Killing non-hostile humans, such as civilians and slaves does not count towards the achievement and usually grants the player negative [moral points](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points), so think twice before pulling the trigger.

If the player wants to receive the good ending, obtaining this achievement is hard, because killing often has to be avoided in order to receive positive moral points. Two walkthroughs might be needed to accumulate enough kills.
