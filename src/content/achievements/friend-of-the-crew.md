**Friend of the Crew** is an [achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements_and_Trophies) in [Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus) that can be obtained in [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) level.

## Overview

In order to receive this achievement, two items have to be collected in The Volga. Firstly, there is a guitar on top of the power tower that the [bandits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/bandits) converted into a base. The tower can be found near [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora), just follow the main railway track that goes through the entire location. The second item is a teddy bear that was taken from [Nastya](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nastya) by a [Demon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Demon). The teddy bear is located on one of the big storage tanks found in the southern part of the marshalling yard - you will visit this location during the story mission which requires finding [Krest's railcar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Krest's_Railcar) and using it to pull a [passenger carriage](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora's_Passenger_Train_Car) to the Aurora. The guitar can be given to [Stepan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stepan_(Metro_2035)) and the teddy bear can be returned to Nastya, but this is not required for getting the achievement. However, it gives the player a positive [moral point](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Points).

## Trivia

- There is also a nest with a sleeping Demon on top of the storage tank where the teddy bear is located. Killing the Demon with a [Molotov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Molotov_Cocktail) while he's still in his nest is the easiest way of obtaining another achievement - [Firebird](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Firebird).
