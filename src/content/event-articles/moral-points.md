**Moral Points** (referred to internally as **Karma Points**) form a hidden system in the [games](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Video_Game_Series) that follows [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s progress and affects the ending of each game. This system is never explained to the player and its mechanics can only be speculated about. As the [series](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Series) is about Artyom's journey and him seeking to understand the world around him, the game rewards the player by guiding Artyom through experiences that help him better understand the metro and its strange phenomena.

It's commonly understood that every time Artyom gets a moral point, the screen flashes light blue and whispers or the sound of water dripping can be heard. When Artyom loses a moral point, the screen darkens and an ominous sound can be heard. There are more opportunities to gain moral points than to lose them, and moral points that are gained and lost vary in severity. Moral points can be gained through making morally correct decisions, being stealthy in certain levels, but can also be awarded for exploring every part of the map to get weapons and supplies.

There is no telling how many moral points are required to get the good ending since the statistics are hidden; however, making wrong choices, killing surrendering NPC's, stealing, or going loud in the levels that require stealth will likely cause the player to not gain enough moral points to trigger the good ending.

## Metro 2033

Artyom has not left [VDNKh](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Exhibition_(Location)) since his childhood and does not know anything about the world outside the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro). If Artyom pokes around corners, eavesdrops dialogues and explores the levels he learns how this new world works and manages to make a decision based on this knowledge. In other words, he achieves a different *Enlightened* [ending](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Endings), as opposed to the *Ranger* one in which he blindly follows his orders. There are 99 possible moral points to earn (91 in the original game). Artyom must gain 50 moral points to get the good ending.

## Last Light

In *[Metro Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Last_Light)*, moral points have not changed from *[2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game))*. This time around, Artyom is much more experienced, so moral points are earned based on whether or not he does the right thing. They are earned the same way as in *2033*, i.e. listening to certain dialogue, performing kind or merciful acts, finding certain hidden areas, etc. Gaining 110 moral points will unlock the *Redemption* ending, where Artyom earns forgiveness for his past, as opposed to the standard *C'est la vie* ending where Artyom sacrifices himself for the Metro. There are 202 possible moral points to earn, and up to 107 to lose, though many of these are mutually exclusive.

## Metro Exodus

Moral points return in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*. As with the previous games, Artyom is awarded points for doing good deeds or doing certain deeds. Unlike its predecessors, listening in on conversations does not affect moral points, and instead can be used to gain locations to stashes. Moral points are also present in the [Sam's Story](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam's_Story) DLC, but instead of indicating good deeds, they reflect actions taken by [Sam](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sam) to gain [Eduard Baranov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Eduard_Baranov)'s trust. Completing all actions will gain the [A Man of Principle](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/A_Man_of_Principle) achievement.

## Moral Points in Last Light

Whenever you receive a moral point, a white flash like effect will appear on your screen along with a sound. This indicates that you did something (e.g. listened to a conversation, went some place) that awarded you with a moral point.

Contrary to a popular misconception, killing human enemies is allowed in most levels, and will not affect your karma. Only the following levels directly reward moral points if completed non-lethally:

- Facility
- Venice (only if completely ghosted)
- Contagion

Revolution level only rewards moral points for overhearing NPC banter, so it indirectly rewards stealth. However, killing enemies does not affect the moral score. You may even raise alarms after overhearing all the dialogue in the area without missing out on moral points.

Note: Killing the story characters does not lead to the bad ending by itself, but it deducts a lot of moral points.

Gain:

Freeing him before he is killed will earn a moral point. This may be done later when Idiot asks you to get a guitar for Stepan. If the player misses [Idiot](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Idiot)'s dialogue, [Stepan](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stepan_(Metro_2035)) will radio Artyom asking him for the guitar.

**Optional - Teddy

**Optional - The Paladins
Lose:

**Optional - Bandit Camp
Lose:

**Tug Boat
Gain:
- One is earned if deciding to stealth unnoticed by the guards. Doing so will prevent [Duke](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duke) from getting injured and Anna will thank you, explaining no one got hurt. The moral point earned once you are inside the tug boat.

- Kill any of the slaves.
Note: Once Artyom has pulled the lever to open the gate and has received the moral point in this mission, the sound of a negative point may be played immediately after, although the screen does not dim and Corsair iCue lighting integration does not respond to this. It is unknown if this is a bug. If you do two steps mentioned above there is no sound of negative point played immediately after.

**Oil Derrick
Gain:
- When going though the cellar where the slaves are, keep undetected until getting out of the cellar. (Note: you can kill the tribals but you need to avoid killing the slaves. It's preferable to just knock out in that part)
- When going up towards the [Baron](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Baron) on the oil derrick, knocking out a soldier harming his slave will earn a point. Be sure to knock him out when no one is looking at him otherwise the next point is impossible to acquire.
- Further up the stairs, a man is about to hurt his female slave. Knock him out to earn another point. (Note: this will cause the bandits to become hostile.)

Lose:
- Kill any of the slaves.
Note: When infiltrating the oil derrick, the first slave you come across will raise the alarm if he sees you kill or knock anyone out.

### Taiga map

**Bandit
Gain:
- Just after Artyom finds the note on the arrow, straight ahead there is a bandit tied to a pole with a goat's skull and two candles. Kill the wolf he is fighting off and untie him to earn a point.
Note: Taking too much time will cause the bandit to be killed by the wolf, rendering this point unobtainable.

Lose:
- Kill the bandit after saving him.

**Pirate Outpost
Gain:
- Sneaking through the Pirate guard post without killing anyone will earn a point once Artyom crosses a zipline at the top of the bell tower.
Note: There is a very obvious path (mostly behind the buildings, hugging the fence) to take after taking a zipline from the adjacent school's roof. When nearing the tower's location, it is possible to climb up the external edge, thereby avoiding the guards in front of it entirely. When taking this approach, it's entirely possible to get through the camp without having to incapacitate anyone at all.

**Pioneer Camp
Gain:
- Sneaking past the camp without killing anyone just after retrieving Artyom's backpack will earn a point once he has crossed a zipline at the end of the camp.

Lose:
- Kill non-hostile Pioneers.

**Optional - Bandit Camp
Gain:
- Once the bandits are dealt with, free the captured Pioneer in the biggest house at the center of the camp. Note that you need to kill all the bandits before you can free him. There are 2 bandits at a perimeter guard post that are quite far from the main camp also need to be killed. Knocking the bandits out will also work.

Lose:
- Kill the captured Pioneer.

**Pirate Swamp Camp
Gain:
- After meeting up with [Olga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Olga), there is a pirate camp where you need to get the boat from. The point is granted for getting out of the camp on said boat while being undetected. Even though you can kill the pirates and still have the point, a stealthy approach with only knocking out enemies is still the best strategy.
Note: Earning this point is incredibly difficult during the day since there are only a few paths into it and all are heavily guarded.

**Lumber Mill
Lose:
- Kill the [Admiral](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Admiral).

An additional moral point is earned if on the last map in search of medicine for Anna, in the Research Institute you overcome all the blind mutants without killing them. The point is obtained as soon as you pass the last section where the power must be restarted and there are 2 blind mutants. Going past the electric sliding doors that close by themselves as soon as you pass through which will separate you from the mutants will make you get the point.
