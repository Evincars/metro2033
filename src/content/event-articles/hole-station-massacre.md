# Hole Station Massacre

The **[Hole Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hole_Station) Massacre** is an event depicted in *[Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game))* where Hole Station underwent intense [mutant](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants) attack, and its defenses failed.

## Overview

The home station of the [Children of the Underground](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Children_of_the_Underground), Hole Station fell under the attack of a relentless horde of [nosalises](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) and [lurkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lurker) that emerged from the dark side of the metro, sometime before the events of *Metro 2033*. On their last legs, the group of its defenders recruited [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) as he appeared coming from the tunnel of the nosalises. He is given a short brief from Captain [Maxim](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Maxim_Komarov), shortly before the winged nosalises attack them. Due to nosalis attacks outnumbering the defenders own capabilities, Captain Maxim, who is injured, instructs Artyom to send a radio broadcast to a beacon, which grants a [Moral Point](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Point) if he accepts it. As Artyom enters Hole station, which is already devastated by nosalises and followed by lurkers infesting the station, he finds the last surviving child, [Sasha](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sasha_(Metro_2033)), near his [uncle's](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sasha%27s_Uncle) corpse, who died from [nosalis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nosalis) bite wounds. As Artyom begins venturing within the destroyed Hole Station, with aid from Sasha, he navigates through the lurker packs and successfully returns to a group of survivors, one of them being [Sasha's mother](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sasha%27s_Mother).

It's unknown how many citizens were killed from the attack, but the number is firmly in double digits - if not reaching into triple digits. What remains of the survivors remains a mystery, as they take shelter in the higher part of the station tunnels as Artyom heads to the surface. Though from a map in *[Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light)*, it can be observed that the [Nazis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Nazis) from the [outpost](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Outpost) above were able to descend and secure the station for themselves. Yet perhaps even the Nazis were unable to hold the station, as when passed by in "[Sniper Mission](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sniper_Team)", the entire station front is submerged from melted snow in the nuclear spring.
