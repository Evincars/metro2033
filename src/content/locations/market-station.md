# Prospekt Mira (Market)

> I've never seen a Market like this — you could buy almost anything.
> — Artyom's description of Market in 2033

Prospekt Mira (Russian: Проспект Мира) or Market Station is a settlement under [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) control. It is a major market hub on the [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Metro_System) and the closest Hanza station to the [VDNKh Commonwealth](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/VDNKh_Commonwealth).

## Overview

The station is fairly large and includes an open bar area, a vast market area, and, in the room before the gate to the [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza) tunnels, there is a [rail bike](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Railbike) fixing area as well as ammunition and [weapon vendors](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_vendors). In one of the entrances, there is a train car retro-fitted to be living quarters. Once entering the main hall of the station, you can hear a radio operator trying to reach other stations or outside signals.

[Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) seems to have a bad reputation with some of the guards, as they treat him as a criminal or someone shunned from the station. Similar to [Riga Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga_Station_(Location)), a man looking like [Alex](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Alex) can be seen in the office; again this would be impossible.

In the novel, in the conversation between Artyom and Zhenya, there is mention of a magician (who also happens to be addicted to drugs) that can see the future.

## Trivia

- At the back of the station, two men can be heard talking about the mystery surrounding the [Kremlin](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kremlin), and why it is dangerous to look at it. This references a major part of the novel, directly quoting a conversation had there.
- A child can be heard arguing with his mother over a pet rat.
- This is one of the levels where [MGR](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR) can be found for the [Sherlock achievement](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sherlock_(Achievement)).
- The Market is one of the only stations in 2033 where you can buy filters, the other being [Riga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Riga).
- There is a shooting range in the weapons vendor area. The drill consists of 6 challenges and is considered the hardest shooting range of the game. It is recommended to use your [revolver](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolver) once it has a decent amount of ammunition and a fast reload. Ammo is infinite on all difficulties during the range.
