**Moscow Metro** (Russian: Московский метрополитен), alluded to as the **Last Refuge** and known in-universe as simply **The Metro**, is the central location and namesake of the [Metro Series](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Series).

## Overview

An [apocalyptic war](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_III) on a grand scale devastated the [surface](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Surface) of the Earth, leaving deadly radiation and monstrous [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants) in its wake. The survivors of [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow) retreated underground into the metro system -- the biggest air-raid shelter ever built. By 2033, a few thousand people live on, not knowing if they are the only survivors on the planet.

The metro system consists of 12 primary lines with 188 stations and over 313 km of track, plus at least 3 secret [Metro-2](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6) lines. Its deep tunnels provide adequate radiation shielding, allowing communities to develop and even thrive underground. However, the metro is divided among rival [factions](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Factions), including the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line), the [Fourth Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich), [Hanza](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hanza), [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis), and the [Spartan Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers).

Life in the metro is harsh: stations serve as city-states, mushrooms are a staple food, filtered air is a necessity, and [military-grade rounds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Military-Grade_Rounds) serve as currency. The tunnels between stations are dangerous, infested with mutants, [anomalies](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anomaly_(Phenomenon)), and other threats.

## Role in Metro Exodus

In *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*, the Moscow Metro serves as the starting point of [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s journey. After discovering that the metro's leadership has been jamming radio signals to prevent contact with the outside world, Artyom and the [Spartan Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Spartan_Rangers) commandeer the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) locomotive and leave Moscow to find a new home, marking the departure from the underground setting that defined the previous games.

## Trivia

- The Moscow Metro is the primary setting of all three Metro novels and the first two games in the series.
- The real-world Moscow Metro is one of the deepest metro systems in the world, which is why it served as a plausible shelter in the fiction.
- The departure from the Moscow Metro in Metro Exodus was a major shift in the series' gameplay and setting.
