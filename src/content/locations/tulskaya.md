# Tulskaya

Tulskaya (Russian: Tульская), also known as Tula or Tulska, is a metro station that appears in [Metro 2034](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2034).

## Metro 2034

In the novel, Tula station is struck by a plague epidemic. The soldiers garrisoned there, among them [Artyom Popov](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom_Popov), are ordered to open fire on the local population to stop the spread of the infected into the rest of the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Metro_System). Eventually, the series of events that transpire there lead to the station flooding and the presumed death of Popov. [Sasha](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sasha_(Metro_2034)), who is also there at the time of the flood, is thought to be dead as well (though her body is never found) until the events of [Metro 2035](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2035), when she is discovered alive by [Artyom Alekseyevich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) at a brothel in Tsvetnoy Bulvar station.

## Trivia

- One of the soldiers at the station is named [Artyom (Popov)](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom_Popov). He is commonly mistaken for [Artyom Alekseyevich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) from the other two books and the games.
