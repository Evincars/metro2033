# Lubyanka

Lubyanka (Russian: Лубянка) on the [Sokolnicheskaya Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sokolnicheskaya_Line) is a metro station underneath Lubyanka Square. The square is located in front of the former KGB headquarters, which now serves as the headquarters of the FSB. Its real-world counterpart is Lubyanka.

## Overview

The building now holds the Lubyanka Prison and one directorate of the Federal Security Service of the Russian Federation (FSB). In addition, a museum of the KGB was opened to the public.

In the [Metro Series](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Series), this station is used by the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line) as a political prison and gulag to suppress those who oppose their rule; people suspected of defiance are also sent there. As [Andrew](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Andrew) the Blacksmith in [Armory Station](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_(Level)) explains:

> "He could get sent to Lubyanka, and that's a one-way ticket."

## Metro 2035

During the events of the novel, the prisoners of Lubyanka are released and forced to rush the Reich lines at Schiller Station. They must run down a dark tunnel without weapons.
