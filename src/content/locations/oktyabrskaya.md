# Oktyabrskaya

Oktyabrskaya (Russian: Октябрьская) is an important location appearing in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It is a two-part station split into two: the [Koltsevaya Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Koltsevaya_Line) (Ring) side controlled by [Hansa](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hansa), and the [Kaluzhsko-Rizhskaya Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kaluzhsko-Rizhskaya_Line) (Radial) side controlled by the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line). It is said to be a peaceful station.

## Metro: Last Light

Oktyabrskaya is first mentioned by bandits in [Venice](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Venice_(Location)) as the then-current location of the [Baby Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One). [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_Morozov) confirms this and says he will help [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) get there, but the two are interrupted by [Simon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Simon), and Pavel escapes. Artyom is forced to take first the [surface](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sundown) route, then the catacombs beneath [Sparta Base](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sparta_Base_(Location)), to get there.

Upon reaching the Church, Artyom and the [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) plan to reach Oktyabrskaya and find the Dark One, but they are attacked before they can start their journey. Artyom first reaches the vicinity of Oktyabrskaya-Radial after being washed out of the catacombs beneath the church and into the nearby Metro tunnels. His first contact is observing two Red Line soldiers executing some infected residents of the station. However, if Artyom kills the soldiers before they can finish off the surviving man, the latter will tell Artyom how the Red troops came before people started dying of [a mysterious plague](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Virus).

Making his way through the station, it becomes clear that the whole area is in ruins — Red Line troops are burning the infected buildings and clearing bodies. It is clear that the virus is very deadly, as there are no living residents left. Even a Red Soldier is executed just for having a tear in his [Armor](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Armor). There is also the possibility of Artyom himself being infected. Artyom finally gets to the end of the station and finds [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna) being held captive by [Lesnitsky](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lesnitsky). After saving Anna, the two (with no gas masks) run to Oktyabrskaya-Koltsevaya and are rescued by the guards there, where they are both placed in quarantine for possible infection.

Here, Artyom discovers that the Red Line allowed many infected refugees to run into the Hansa section, then followed them to prevent any signs of an outbreak. However, they were repulsed by the Hansa guards and Rangers. These people are now also in quarantine, with many of them dying and being cremated to stop infection. The Reds did this to try and infect the other part of the station as well, before they would go aid in the clean-up effort, therefore occupying the two stations and cutting Hansa in half. Thankfully, this plan failed and the Reds were forced to pull out.

The Hansa doctor's research reveals that the infection is a type of bio-weapon — a deadly strain of Ebola — designed to have maximum lethality (nearly 95%) in the first day, then burn out and go inert, meaning any invading forces will not be infected. Oktyabrskaya was a test of the bio-weapon's destructive capabilities, and clearly it was very successful. The Red's plan was to use this weapon to infect the entire Metro, leaving it clear for them to take over, though there would be no guarantee the disease would not mutate or fail to burn out, killing them as well. However, they had run out of samples of the virus, meaning they needed to occupy [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)) to obtain more, leading to the [Battle for D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Battle_for_D6).

Oktyabrskaya is not mentioned for the rest of the game, but it is presumably left to rot, given the numerous bodies still lingering there, possible infection, and a large part of the station burned down. These factors may make it more difficult to travel to Venice from that part of Hansa.

## Trivia

- Oktyabrskaya is mentioned once briefly in Metro 2033 during "[Chase](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Chase_(Metro_2033_Level))" by one of the tunnel guards. It can be heard just before one of the men narrowly avoids being run over by Artyom's trolley.
