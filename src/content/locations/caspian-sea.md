**Caspian Sea** (Russian: Каспийское море) is a [location](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Locations) in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*. The desertified area around what is left of it is known as the **Caspian Desert** (Russian: Прикаспийская пустыня). It serves as the main setting of [The Caspian](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Caspian_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) chapter.

## Overview

Before the [Great War](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_III), the Caspian Sea was the largest lake in the world, with a surface area of 371,000 km. Being away from major populated areas, the region was spared from nuclear bombing. However, the aftermath brought devastating desertification, and a large portion of the lake dried out, turning the former lake bed and surrounding area into a scorching hot desert. Predictably, famine followed, and the surviving population fought over the few resources that remained.

In recent years, the region came to be dominated by the [Munai-bailer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Munai-bailer), a raider group that managed to secure the oil reserves and subsequently enslaved most of the local population. They are opposed by a resistance movement led by [Giul](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Giul).

## Metro Exodus

The [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) crew arrives at the Caspian region while traveling eastward across Russia. [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) must explore the vast desert landscape -- filled with shipwrecks from the dried-up sea, abandoned oil rigs, and the settlements of enslaved locals -- while dealing with the [Munai-bailer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Munai-bailer) threat and searching for water and fuel.

The level features an open-world design with day-night cycles and sandstorms. [Mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants) such as [humanimals](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Humanimal) and [lurkers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lurker) inhabit the ruins, while the Munai-bailer's patrols and outposts present a constant danger. The player's choices regarding the enslaved population and the Munai-bailer's leadership affect the moral outcome of the game.

## Trivia

- The Caspian Sea is the world's largest inland body of water in real life, located between Europe and Asia.
- The environmental storytelling in this level includes abandoned ships stranded in the desert, illustrating how far the waters have receded.
- The Caspian chapter is one of the open-world sandbox levels in Metro Exodus, offering greater freedom of exploration compared to the linear levels.
