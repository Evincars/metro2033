# Nakhimovsky Prospekt

Nakhimovsky Prospekt (Russian: Нахимовский Проспект) is a station that appears only in the [Metro 2034](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2034) novel.

## Overview

Described by [Homer](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Homer) as a horrible abandoned station, [corpse eaters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Corpse_Eaters) converted Nakhimovsky Prospekt into a large nest with an abundance of decomposed corpses and bones covering the floor, along with many piled cadavers, which the corpse eaters use for nutrition. This station is a putrid cemetery and might be the house of all corpse eaters in the [metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Metro_System), or at least on that line.

Despite the station being a nest of corpse eaters, these creatures avoid humans when a caravan crosses the station to Sevastopolskaya, so it's prohibited to waste bullets on them.
