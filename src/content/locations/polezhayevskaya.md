# Polezhayevskaya

Polezhayevskaya (Russian: Полежаевская) is a destroyed station from the [Tagansko–Krasnopresnenskaya Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tagansko%E2%80%93Krasnopresnenskaya_Line), located near the [1905 Confederation](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/1905_Confederation), that appears in the [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)) novel and is also mentioned in the [game](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Video_Game)).

## Overview

The station is mentioned by Pyotr Andreevich early in the novel, and very briefly in the Metro 2033 video game on the level [Market](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Market_(Level)). To find the conversation, go to the start of the level; when the guards let you pass through after [Bourbon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bourbon) talks with one of them, leave the room, then immediately return. The guards will then begin to tell the story of the station. Later in the level, a boy can be found talking to a beggar. The beggar will ask the boy to fetch him a sandwich, and in return he will tell the boy about what happened to the squad of veterans who returned to Polezhayevskaya.

At first, scouts from Polezhayevskaya disappeared after going one kilometer into the tunnel. Reinforcements tried to search for them but found nothing. A week later, a second scout group disappeared after going only half a kilometer in. The station took measures to protect itself by establishing a cordon at the three-hundredth meter, with sandbags, machine guns, and a spotlight set up.

A runner was sent from Polezhayevskaya to Begovaya Station (from the "1905 Confederation") to alert them about the bizarre danger in the northern tunnels. The council of Begovaya was still considering a response to the call for help when they received another scared and shocked runner from the station: the entire cordon had been completely killed in silence, as if they had been butchered in their sleep. The council of Begovaya finally decided to send a large group of armed veterans, about a hundred men, toward Polezhayevskaya to solve the threat. However, when the squadron reached the station, it was completely empty. Not even corpses were found. While the entire station was drenched in blood, nobody was able to explain what had happened there.

The veterans saw what the deal was and blew out the tunnel to Polezhayevskaya. Roughly forty meters of the tunnel collapsed, so now the rest of the line is an abandoned dead zone isolated from the rest of the Metro system. Nobody knows what happened there, though a beggar found in the video game's iteration of Riga gives the explanation that the people in the station had all killed each other after a tunnel collapse.
