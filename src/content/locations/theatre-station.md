# Teatralnaya (Theatre)

Theatre Station (Russian: Театральная) is a location in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light). It is situated near the Bolshoi Theatre and is known as Teatralnaya Station. In Metro: Last Light, this station seems to be owned by the [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line).

## Overview

Theatre Station, also known as [Bolshoi](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bolshoi), is the cultural center of the whole Metro. [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Red_Line)) head towards the station after they escape from the [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich) to make contact with [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)). The two access the station by crossing Theatre Square on the surface, providing [travelers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Stalkers) and [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants) access to the station.

The station is controlled by the Red Line, as several communist soldiers are seen guarding it. It also appears, however, that the station has a higher degree of autonomy, as the presence of a free market and the sheer number of welcome refugees from Reich would suggest. Two locals in an office near the entrance can be heard discussing the situation, and it seems the station is technically independent, but effectively owned by the Red Line.

The main surface entrance to the station is locked by automated [hermetic doors](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hermetic_door) (which are usual for any other Moscow Metro station). The station is well protected by Red Line soldiers and two heavily-armoured [handheld-flamethrower](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Flamethrower) wielding guards patrolling the entrance.

In the novels, the station is neutral, but finds itself in a politically volatile position due to being situated between the Reich and the Red Line. As a result, the theatre performers are often quite frustrated with the limited number of plays they can perform to avoid offending either of the two superpowers. The station is attacked and occupied by the Reich in 2035 after Artyom and several other Reich agents detonate mines that are supposed to cut the Red Line off from the station. Due to Artyom not placing his mine in the correct location, however, the station is counterattacked by the Reds from Okhotny Ryad, and eventually taken.
