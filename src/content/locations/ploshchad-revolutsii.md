# Ploshchad Revolutsii

Ploshchad Revolutsii (Russian: Площадь Революции), meaning "Revolution Square", is a station on the [Arbatsko-Pokrovskaya Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arbatsko-Pokrovskaya_Line) in [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro).

## Overview

When the Red Line learned that [Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Rangers) had captured [D6](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/D6_(Location)), they began preparing for a massive attack, aimed at capturing the bunker complex. Ploshchad Revolutsii was turned into a training camp for Red Line soldiers who were supposed to conduct the attack. In huge workshops located deep under the station, the communists started building an [armored train](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line_Armored_Train), which they would later use as a battering ram during their attack. One day, Secretary General Comrade [Moskvin](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Maxim_Moskvin) personally arrives at the station for an inspection, along with his advisor and Intelligence General [Czeslav Korbut](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Czeslav_Korbut).

The station and its surrounding depots are directly seen during several levels. The beginning of the level "[Korbut](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Korbut_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))" takes place in the station, where [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) is interrogated by Moskvin and Korbut. During the next level, "[Revolution](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Revolution_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))", Artyom must sneak his way out of the station, passing the depot with the armored train in the process.

The station was originally a part of [Arbat Confederation](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arbat_Confederation). However, in July 2023, it was captured by the growing [Red Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line), not only for symbolic reasons, but also due to its strategic position, which is close to the Red Square and [Lenin's Mausoleum](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lenin%27s_Mausoleum).

During the [Hansa - Red Line War](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hansa_-_Red_Line_War), this station was turned into an impenetrable fortress where intense battles were fought with heavy casualties on both sides. The Red Line nearly lost the station several times but always quickly regained their ground.

When the war ended, this station turned into a fairly peaceful place. As of 2033, passage is allowed to all Metro citizens.
