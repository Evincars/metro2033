**Volga** (Russian: Волга) is a river that flows through central [Russia](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Russia) and into the [Caspian Sea](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Caspian_Sea), making it the longest river on the European subcontinent. It serves as the main setting of [The Volga](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Volga_(Metro_Exodus_Level)) chapter in *[Metro Exodus](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Exodus)*.

## Overview

Located in the Volga's drainage basin are over half of the Russian Federation's largest cities, including its capital [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow). The Volga has attained a symbolic meaning in the culture of Russia, sometimes dubbed "Volga-matushka" (Волга-матушка, Mother Volga) in the country's folklore and literature. It is considered by many to be the national river of the country. Its importance is so pronounced that even after the [apocalypse](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_III), its waters are still used as trade routes.

The region around the Volga is controlled by the [Church of the Water Tsar](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Water_Tsar), a technophobic religious cult that worships the [Tsar Fish](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tsar_Fish), a massive aquatic mutant inhabiting the river. [Bandits](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits_(Faction)) also operate in the region, raiding caravans and clashing with the cult.

## Metro Exodus

As [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and [Anna](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anna_(Character)) travel aboard the [Aurora](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora) eastward from the Moscow Metro, they encounter the Volga early in their journey. The crew must lower a drawbridge over the river to continue their route, which brings them into conflict with the Church of the Water Tsar, who control the bridge.

The Volga chapter is the first open-world sandbox level in Metro Exodus, allowing players to explore a frozen, flooded landscape dotted with churches, bandit camps, and cult outposts. The player's moral choices -- particularly how they deal with the cultists and whether they rescue captives -- affect [Duke](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Duke)'s fate and the game's ending.

Key threats in the region include the Tsar Fish, the cult's paladins, bandits, [shrimp](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Shrimp), and [watchmen](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Watchman).

## Trivia

- The Volga chapter takes place during winter, with the river partially frozen.
- The Volga is the first location the Aurora crew visits after leaving Moscow, establishing the theme that life exists beyond the metro tunnels.
- The real-world Volga River is approximately 3,530 km in length, the longest river in Europe.
