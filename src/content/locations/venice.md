# Tretyakovskaya (Venice)

> "The subterranean Venice... An island on the dark waters of underground rivers."
> — Artyom

Venice (Russian: Венеция) refers to the flooded sections of Tretyakovskaya and Novokuznetskaya Station in the [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Metro_System). It is run by station master [Simon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Simon). However, his control is limited at best and [local gangsters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits_(Faction)) have filled this void.

## Overview

Venice is an independent station, nicknamed after the real-life Italian city, that is widely renowned for its unique situation. Tretyakovskaya and Novokuznetskaya, nicknamed Venice by the inhabitants, has its share of problems.

### Government

> "You always hear rough stories circulating about this place. I hear it is all one huge den of thieves."
> — Artyom

Venice is an independent station technically run by [Simon](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Simon_(Venice)), although in reality [gangsters](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bandits_(Faction)) rule the station. They are vicious and brutish, and there is no hiding it. This lack of central authority is seen as both a gift and a curse. Its inhabitants enjoy large economic freedoms, and the station is rather open and public to visitors, as seen in [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom)'s arrival. The negative side is that the anarchistic lack of governing authority gives way to organized crime.

### Flooding

Venice may have problems with controlling the rampant thievery within its walls, but a more physical problem is that its streets are flooded and irradiated. This has been caused by the nuclear spring on the surface as the snow and ice begin to melt. As the surface world thaws from nuclear winter, the water flows downward, causing the underground rivers that the metros are built upon to flood beyond the metro system's water pumps to handle.

While the flooding may create many problems, it has proven to form a strong economic property as well. Fishing here is great, and a large economic advantage of being able to fish and sell means they no longer have to depend so greatly on farms from other stations. The fishermen are also known to use a scanner to determine whether the fish they catch have dangerous levels of radiation.

- There is some bandit activity in Venice. However, due to the public part of Venice being relatively peaceful, it's unknown what faction exactly is controlling the station or if Venice is independent or not.
- The station is home to a brothel, some shops, and a rat shooting range where a lot of [MGRs](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/MGR) and a teddy bear can be earned. The bear can be given to a young boy, to earn a [moral point](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moral_Point), and the Reunion achievement/trophy.
- Venice is named after the real Italian city of Venice, known for its waterways and gondola transportation, similar to the Metro Venice.
- In the Chronicles Pack "[Pavel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Pavel_(Chronicles_Pack_DLC_Level))" level, it is shown that Venice gets its electricity from the bandits, which use slave labour to spin giant dynamos and generate power.
