# Vorobyovy Gory

Vorobyovy Gory (Russian: Воробьёвы горы), meaning "Sparrow Hills", is a destroyed surface station located on the Luzhniki Metro Bridge. It's a part of the [Sokolnicheskaya Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sokolnicheskaya_Line) of the [Moscow Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow_Metro).

## Overview

In [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)), it was mentioned that the surface bridge over the river Moskva had been destroyed, which isolated three stations of Sokolnicheskaya line. A semi-mythical community of intellectuals and former university staff known as [Emerald City](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Emerald_City) is said to inhabit the three stations.

In [Metro 2034](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2034), [Leonid](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Leonid) and [Sasha](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sasha) travel to this end of Sokolnicheskaya line in order to reach Emerald City. They are stopped by a massive steel door with a buzzer that no one has ever gotten a response from. Therefore, the novels neither confirm nor deny whether Emerald City is real.

The level "[Bridge](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Bridge_(Metro_Last_Light_Level))" of [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light) takes place on the Luzhniki Metro Bridge and in Vorobyovy Gory Station. [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) and the [Baby Dark One](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Baby_Dark_One) have to cross the bridge, fighting or avoiding various [mutants](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Mutants) in the process. All that remains are several corpses and a radio.
