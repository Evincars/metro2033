# Paveletskaya (Hole Station)

Hole Station (Russian: Павелецкая, Paveletskaya) is a location in the post apocalyptic [Moscow](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Moscow) [Metro](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro), and appears in the video game [Metro 2033](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Videogame)).

## Overview

Hole Station is a large independent station housing many families, soldiers, and scouts. In the game, the station is in the location of Paveletskaya Station, but unlike the Paveletskaya in the [novel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)), this station doesn't have an open [Hermetic Door](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Hermetic_Door) leading directly to the [surface](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_surface). The station is very different to other stations of the metro; instead of having one main passage with several off-shoots to the platforms, it has several different rooms, and vertical stairs leading to passages that are blocked off.

During the events of the video game Metro 2033, Hole Station is the victim to an attack, during which [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) [helps](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Defense) the [defenders](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Children_of_the_Underground). When walking through the station, many bodies of the station's various victims can be found. One such victim could possibly be a [ranger](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Rangers), due to the rangers' signature helmet on a table very near where he took his last stand. The station is one of the largest Artyom has encountered on his travels, as it's featured in two different levels: [Defense](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Defense) and [Child](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Child).

## Metro: Last Light

The definitive fate of the citizens of Hole Station remains unknown, though according to [Miller](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Miller)'s map in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light) the station was eventually taken by the forces of the [Reich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Reich). It's presumed that the mutant threat would have thus been eliminated.
