# Lenin's Mausoleum

Lenin's Mausoleum (Russian: Мавзолей Ленина) is a surviving monument from before the [war](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Great_War_of_2013). It can be visited in [Metro: Last Light](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro:_Last_Light).

## Overview

Lenin's Mausoleum is situated in Red Square in the center of Moscow, and, in the real world, currently houses the preserved remains of Vladimir Lenin. His body, once embalmed by pathologist Alexei Ivanovich Abrikosov, has been on public display there since shortly after his death in 1924 (with rare exceptions in wartime). In the [Metro Series](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_Series), this building is in a rather awful state. Of course this is not only because of the nuclear disaster, but due to 20 years of a lack of maintenance and the destroying forces of nature. Lenin's body itself is almost totally decomposed and in the game is replaced by a common skeleton.

Finding the mausoleum is an optional route the player can take in the level "[Red Square](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Square)". After descending from a trench on the surface, the player comes across a crumbling rubble hole directly pre-empting the confrontation with the [Reds](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Line). A single [lurker](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Lurker) can be seen running into a hatch a few meters to the left of the player. To find the mausoleum, the player must enter this narrow corridor. At the end of it there is a room with a red door and a few cobwebs. Upon climbing the nearby stairs and turning left, one can find a dead end with a filter and some ammo; to the right is the resting place of Lenin. In Lenin's burial chamber is a [diary entry](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Diary_Entries_(Metro_Last_Light)), a small cache of ammunition, a filter, and a [VSV](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/VSV) with a silencer, infrared sight, and laser sight.
