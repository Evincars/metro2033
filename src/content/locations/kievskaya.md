# Kievskaya

Kievskaya (Russian: Киевская) is a station that is south-west of [Polis](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Polis_(Location)). It is one of the few whose role and status has significantly changed in the translation of Metro 2033 from [novel](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Novel)) to [game](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Metro_2033_(Videogame)).

## Game Overview

According to a legend told by [the Rangers](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/The_Rangers), there was an explosion in the tunnels leading to Kievskaya which buried some people alive. Some were said to have survived, but "no longer resemble humans". The Rangers were also told that there was a mysterious entity, which was some kind of fog. The mention of a collapsed tunnel and frightening beings on the other side could well be a reference to the [Great Worm Children](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Worm_Children) from the novel, who menaced the people of Kievskaya through a small hole in the cave-in. After Miller tells the story, [Vladimir](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Vladimir) expresses doubt as to its veracity, and [Ulman](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Ulman) responds that Vladimir lost his family in the explosion but has no memory of what happened after.

The station is not specifically seen during the game, but could possibly be somewhere between [Sparta](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Sparta_Base_(Location)) and the [D6 outskirts](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon_(Level)). This would make sense given that the Rangers pass through to get to D6, but D6 is still quite far away, and they would still have to go through [Park Pobedy](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Park_Pobedy).

## In The Novel

In the novel, Kievskaya is a sparsely populated station. [Melnik](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Melnik) and [Artyom](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Artyom) arrive there after meeting up after the Library. The residents of the station are very scared of an [unknown entity](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Savage_Cannibals_of_the_Great_Worm_Cult). Their tents are very close: "The distance between them [tents] required by the fire safety was not observed anywhere; clearly these people were scared of something more frightening than fire."

One notable member is an old Metro station chief, [Arkadiy Semyonovich](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arkadiy_Semyonovich), who Melnik speaks to in great detail about other station residents, [Tretyak](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Tretyak) and [Anton](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Anton) — Anton being the father of [Oleg](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Oleg), the young boy who goes missing. The station is very worried about the rising amount of children going missing.

## Trivia

- In Moscow, Kievskaya is an interchange station between Kiyevskaya on the [Koltsevaya Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Koltsevaya_Line), Kiyevskaya on the [Arbatsko-Pokrovskaya Line](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Arbatsko-Pokrovskaya_Line), and Kiyevskaya on the Filyovskaya Line.
- The station is named after the nearby Moscow Kiyevskaya railway station, which in turn is named after the capital of Ukraine, [Kiev](https://metrovideogame.fandom.com/wiki/Kiev).
