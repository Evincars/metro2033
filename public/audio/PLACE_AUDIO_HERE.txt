Ambient background audio for the Metro 2033 site
================================================

Drop your audio files into this folder using EXACTLY these filenames so the
player picks them up (see src/composables/useAmbientAudio.js):

  1. metro-ambient.mp3        -> looping Metro-series tunnel ambience
  2. metro-2033-anthem.mp3    -> the track from
                                 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CHVQSch7AnU

The player loops through both tracks, starts on the first click/keypress
(browsers block audio autoplay), and is controlled from the ⚙ Settings dialog
in the top-right corner (on/off + volume, saved to localStorage).

Note: the audio itself is NOT included. These tracks are copyrighted, so please
supply a file you have the right to use (e.g. exported from a source you own).
Other formats work too — just adjust the filenames/extensions in
useAmbientAudio.js (mp3, ogg and m4a are widely supported).
